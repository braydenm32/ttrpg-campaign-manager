Delete your data

Last updated 7 November 2026

You can remove your information from the Warded Atlas at any time.

Delete a world’s cloud copy

Open the world, go to World settings → Cloud sync, sharing and players, and choose to delete the cloud copy. The copy on your device stays unless you delete the world there too.

Delete your whole account

In the app, open ☁️ your account on the front page and choose Delete my account. This removes your account, your saved AI keys, the cloud copy and files of every world you own, and what you added to worlds you joined: your notes, questions, stars and wishes, downtime plans, poll answers, new-character requests and your claims on characters. Worlds saved on your device stay there.

Some things stay in a game master’s world because they’ve become part of it: what you wrote on a character’s page, group notes you helped write, and a character the game master made from your request. Clear what you can yourself first, or ask the game master. Your name can also stay in their own pages, transcripts and recaps, and in backups they’ve saved, which we can’t reach.

Can’t sign in any more? Email brayden.m.32@gmail.com from the email address on your account (for a Google or Facebook sign-in, that account’s address). We’ll reply to that address to check it’s really you, wait 7 days in case you change your mind, then delete the account and confirm by email. We can’t act on a request from any other address, because deleting an account can’t be undone.

Signed in with Facebook or Google?

You can also remove the Warded Atlas from your account settings: on Facebook under Settings → Apps and websites, and on Google at myaccount.google.com/connections. That stops further sign-ins. To also delete what’s stored with us, send the email above.

Data on your device

Worlds you never synced live only in your browser or desktop app. Delete a world in its World settings, or clear the site’s data in your browser settings. On a computer that isn’t yours, use ☁️ your account → Sign out and remove all worlds and keys from this device.

Used the app at its old address, braydenm32.github.io? Once your worlds have moved to wardedatlas.com, the old address forgets its AI keys, tokens and sign-in, but a copy of your worlds stays in that browser until you clear the site data for braydenm32.github.io in your browser settings.
