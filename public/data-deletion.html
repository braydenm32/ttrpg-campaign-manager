Delete your data

Last updated 26 October 2026

You can remove your information from the Warded Atlas at any time.

Delete a world’s cloud copy

Open the world, go to World settings → Cloud sync, sharing and players, and choose to delete the cloud copy. The copy on your device stays unless you delete the world there too.

Delete your whole account

In the app, open ☁️ your account on the front page and choose Delete my account. This removes your account, the cloud copy and files of every world you own, and your notes, questions and characters in worlds you joined. Worlds saved on your device stay there.

Can’t sign in any more? Email brayden.m.32@gmail.com from the address on your account (or tell us the name on your Google or Facebook account) and we’ll delete it within 30 days and confirm by email.

Signed in with Facebook or Google?

You can also remove the Warded Atlas from your account settings: on Facebook under Settings → Apps and websites, and on Google at myaccount.google.com/connections. That stops further sign-ins. To also delete what’s stored with us, send the email above.

Data on your device

Worlds you never synced live only in your browser or desktop app. Delete a world in its World settings, or clear the site’s data in your browser settings.
