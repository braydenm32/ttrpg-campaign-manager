Campaign Codex

Privacy policy

Last updated 23 October 2026

Campaign Codex is a tool for running tabletop roleplaying games, made by Brayden (“we”, “us”) in Australia. This policy explains what information the app handles, where it goes, and the choices you have. We have tried to keep it short and plain.

The short version

  • Without an account, everything you make stays on your own device. We never see it.
  • With an account, the worlds you choose to sync are stored in our cloud database so they can reach your other devices and your players.
  • We don’t sell your information, show ads, or use tracking or analytics.
  • You can delete your account and cloud data at any time.

What we collect

Account details. If you make an account we store your email address and a securely hashed password. If you sign in with Google or Facebook, we receive your name, email address and profile picture link from them. We don’t receive your Google or Facebook password, contacts or posts.

Content you sync. When you turn on sync for a world, its pages, maps, images, sessions, notes and settings are stored in the cloud. If you join someone else’s world as a player, your display name, the characters you claim and the notes, questions and party items you write are stored with that world.

Basic technical records. Our hosting providers keep standard logs (such as IP address, browser type and time of request) for security and to keep the service running. We don’t use these to profile you.

Things that stay on your device. Worlds you don’t sync, session recordings, transcripts made on your device, and any API keys you enter (for AssemblyAI or Anthropic) are kept only in your browser or desktop app. API keys are never uploaded or included in backups.

How we use it

Only to run the app: signing you in, syncing your worlds between your devices, showing players what the game master has chosen to share with them, sending account emails (like confirming your address or resetting your password), and keeping the service secure. We don’t send marketing emails.

Who can see your content

Your synced worlds are private to you and anyone you invite. Co-GMs you invite can see and edit the whole world. Players you invite only see what you reveal to them. Anyone with a share link you create can see what you’ve revealed through that link, until you turn it off.

Services we rely on

  • Supabase stores accounts and synced data (servers in Sydney, Australia).
  • GitHub Pages serves the website.
  • Google and Facebook, only if you choose to sign in with them.
  • Our email provider, to send account emails.
  • AssemblyAI and Anthropic, only if you add your own API key and choose to send a recording for transcription or a transcript for a recap. That data goes directly from your device to them under their own privacy policies, using your own account with them.

Some of these providers may process data outside Australia.

How long we keep it

We keep your account and synced worlds until you delete them. Deleting a cloud copy of a world removes it from our database. Deleting your account removes your account and the worlds you own. Backups held by our providers are cleared on their normal schedule.

Your choices and rights

You can use the app without an account, turn sync off for any world, delete cloud copies, and export your data as a backup file at any time. You can ask us for a copy of your information, to correct it, or to delete it. See how to delete your data. If you’re in Australia, the EU or the UK you have rights under the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) or the GDPR, and you may complain to the OAIC or your local regulator.

Children

Campaign Codex isn’t meant for children under 13, and we don’t knowingly collect their information. If you think a child has made an account, contact us and we’ll delete it.

Changes

If we change this policy we’ll update the date above, and for big changes we’ll let account holders know.
