The Warded Atlas

A campaign and world manager for tabletop roleplaying game masters.

What the Warded Atlas does

The Warded Atlas helps game masters of Dungeons & Dragons and other tabletop roleplaying games keep their whole world in one place, and share just the right parts with their players. It is free, works in your browser or as a desktop app, and keeps working offline. No account is needed to use it; everything is saved on your own device.

  • Wiki pages for places, people, factions, items and lore, linked together.
  • Interactive maps with pins, regions, fog of war and a built-in map maker.
  • Sessions with prep sheets, encounters, recordings, transcripts and recaps.
  • Secrets stay secret: anything marked for the GM never reaches players, and you can reveal pages to individual players.
  • Players join your world to see what you’ve revealed, claim their characters and keep notes.

Why sign in?

Signing in is optional. An account, using your email or your Google account, lets you sync your worlds between your devices and invite players. The Warded Atlas only uses your sign-in to know who you are: your name and email address. It never posts anything or reads your contacts. See the privacy policy for details.
