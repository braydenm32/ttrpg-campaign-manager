A small dented Keel whale-oil tin, the Keel bell stamped in the base, its seal grey wax pressed with a moth. Half full of grey slurry that was once clear oil. It smells of lamp-smoke and, under that, of cold water. Found in the ring round the Cold Captain's chair in the [[The Glass Barrow]].

### What it is
Lantern oil, mixed with fine ash. It will burn, but badly, with a sooty flame that leaves a crust on the wick. Poured into the Ninth's lantern, it would dim the flame within days.

### Clues
The ash is fine and pale and smells of lake water; anyone local knows only Stillwater ash smells like that. The wax is not the Keep's. The cut was done carefully, by someone who knew what they were doing and took the trouble to reseal it.

:::gm
This is the plot's middle link, in the order it happened. Marius Vell's Quiet Office pays [[Ansel Mote]], who buys sealed Keel tins at [[Hesk's Chandlery]] through "a clerk with a cold" (paid in advance; it is in Hesk's order-book). Mote opens them, cuts the oil with Stillwater lake-ash supplied by the Dreaming Widow's people, and reseals them in Hesk's own grey wax with the moth stamp. [[Garrick Sallow]] pays [[Dannic Rook]], whose debts are the lever, to swap them for the Keep's clean tins in the oil store, from about 18 Reaping to 7 Ashfall. [[Brother Tallow]] then pours the dawn measure into the cage's oil-port, and the flame thins. Dannic kept the empties of the swapped tins as proof: seven rinsed Keel tins on the stair landing in the Barrow.

[[Spoons Aldane]] saw the swap twice and knows Sallow and Dannic; he saw who resealed the oil. Hesk did not cut it. [[Fennick Bottlebrook]] is the red herring: he sold four casks of good oil to Kettle for bread and wrote them up as burned.

The Rent-ash story is the red herring the Sleepless want believed: pale plain ash, sold in the Smokes to pilgrims as a charm. It does not smell of the lake. Never: broad arrow, "stolen from stores", "Dannic cut it".
:::

== GM notes ==

A clue, not a macguffin. Let the party find the tin in the Glass Barrow, hold it up to the light, and ask questions. Every faction wants a different answer: the Quiet Office wants it to be the Sleepless, the Sleepless want it to be the Church, and the Church wants it to be Sallow. The smell is the real answer, and a local nose gets it in one sniff.
