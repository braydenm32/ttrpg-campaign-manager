Cinderford keeps more holy days than it has priests for. Soldiers of eight dead companies brought eight calendars; the town that grew up round them kept the lot, the way it kept their badges. Nobody keeps all of them. Everybody keeps Cinders.

The arithmetic matters: a month is five weeks of six days, so the first of every month is an Ironday, the fifteenth a Hearthday with Pallas full, and the thirtieth a Restday with Pallas dark. Four of the year's feasts fall on a thirtieth, which means they fall on [[Isaurana]]'s Unveiling as well, and her houses have long since stopped minding. See [[The Reckoning of Years]] for the count.

### The year, in order

**1 Hollowfrost, the Long Hold** ([[Tharos]]). The first day of the year, a day and a night of silence in every dwarven hold. In Cinderford the surface dwarves of the company keep it on the gatehouse wall, and [[Gerda Hollin]] has been known to hold the whole tally silent with one look. The Returned keep it too, and nobody can tell the difference.

**1 Seedwake, Sowing** ([[Maelis]]). Seed blessed at the hearth-house on Temple Row. There is nothing to sow in the March but goats, so Mother Brisk blesses the goats.

**7 Seedwake, Stake Day.** The company's own day, an Ironday. A work-party goes out from the bridge with a cart of stakes and finds the Ash Road again, fifty paces and a rag at a time, as far as the Treaty Stone and, in a good year, to the Burned Mill. The town walks out behind it with bread and beer and the children race the cart. The rags are dead men's shirts, torn into strips by the widows the night before, and a stake with a rag on it is a grave-marker as much as a waymark; which is why nobody takes one for firewood, and why the Glassers moving them is a thing people spit about. The roster is pegged on the gatehouse board ([[The Gatehouse Board]]). It has not been filled since 3010.

**1 Bloomtide, Greening** ([[Orvanne]]). The first day nothing is hunted. Sael's day; in Cinderford a ribbon on the dead tree.

**7 Bloomtide, the Turning** ([[Sefra]]) and the **Quay-Moot**. First sailing; every bay launches a boat with no crew in it and watches where she goes. On the same day the Mothers of the Water hold their Moot on the river at [[Sevenhaven]], and every barge that can be there is. The Low Quay's floating market empties for a month, [[Kettle Brindlewake]] with it, and Cinderford finds out each spring exactly how much of what it knows it knew by her.

**1 Highsun, Firstfire** ([[The Unburnt Saint]]). The year's new fires lit from a lamp carried from Halcyra. The mission on the Ash Road carries one through the Smokes and lights any hearth that asks. Many ask.

**15 Highsun, Crownfast** ([[Aurelan]]). Pallas full at the height of summer, the empire's founding day: oaths of office renewed, prisoners pardoned by the dozen. The shrine on Temple Row has been locked since 3002; the Reeve opens it alone, with one candle, and locks it again. [[Pell Quillon]] keeps it with one candle wherever he is, and has not spent one here yet.

**2 Emberwane, Shadow-Count.** The Holds' day, since 3004: at noon on the day the Returned came up, every dwarf in a hold stands in the sun and is counted by shadow. The surface clans call it a census. The Returned call it nothing and stand with the rest. In Cinderford Gerda keeps it on the drill yard at noon with the two or three Returned in town beside her and writes "all present" in the gatehouse book, because she refuses to count any other way.

**30 Emberwane, Anvil Night** ([[Grum]]). Forges let go cold and relit at midnight with a prayer one syllable long. The company's farrier says it at the anvil on Temple Row and the whole Ninth turns out to hear him swear.

**17 Reaping, Eye-Night.** The Smokes' day, and nobody else's. On the night of 3006's riot ([[The Night of Open Eyes]]) the company stood in Tanners' Lane with its weapons held low until dawn, and the Smokes has kept the night since, its own way: lamps out, doors open, the Hollowborn children walking the lanes in a line with the youngest in front, and anyone who meets them stepping into a doorway to let them by. The company's patrol stops at the Chapel's end of Temple Row, by Marrow's standing order, and stands there all night with its weapons held low, and does not go in. Nothing is said. Nothing has happened on Eye-Night in five years, which the Smokes puts down to the children and the company puts down to the standing.

**30 Reaping, Last Sheaf** (Maelis). The last sheaf dressed as a woman and burned. In the March, a goat-rope stuffed with ash-grass, and Mother Brisk's Last Sheaf cake, which is better than it sounds.

**3 Ashfall, Tally's Day.** The Glassers' feast, kept since 3008: the day Vexa's crews brought the 11th Foot's pay-chest up out of the glass at [[Tally's Cut]] ([[The Glassers' First Big Find]]). The coin on Old Tally's grave on the lip of the pit is changed with ceremony, every crew at the Cut and the fort drinks at the Ripe One at Ferrow Gant's expense, a digger who was not there has to say why, and it is the only day in the year the Glassers do not dig. By evening every Glasser in the March is drunk and the ones who have reached Cinderford are in the Smokes.

**13 Ashfall, the eve.** Not a holy day. [[Mirrel Vosk]] takes a bottle to [[The Corporal's Gibbet]] and sits with it, and nobody speaks to him, and the Candle keeps its own roll (below).

**14 Ashfall, Cinders Muster.** See below.

**15 Ashfall, rent day.** Pallas full. Not a feast, but kept like one, backwards: rents, tabs and tithes fall due, [[Tollamund & Daughters]] opens early, the Baker's Wife sells Pallas pies, and the quarter-day pay the company has had the day before is spent by nightfall. In 3011 there is nothing to spend, and the whole town knows it by noon.

**Every full Ember, the Saint's Lamp** (the Unburnt Saint). Her faithful put out every other light and sit with one flame. The mission on the Ash Road walks the coals that night and invites the town; the Weeping Orchard bleeds; the Hollowborn dream; and the Ninth's lantern burns a little lower. In 3011 the fulls fall on 16 Ashfall, 9 Leafrot, 2 Frostcall, 25 Frostcall and 18 Longnight.

**30 Ashfall, the Unveiling** (Isaurana). Pallas dark, as on every thirtieth; but the Ashfall Unveiling is the one the Velvet Ledger keeps hardest, because it falls on a Restday, which is the Corporal's, and Sorrel Vey says a god of refusal and a goddess of veils have more in common than either would like. One true thing said aloud in every house of hers. The Ledger's busiest night of the year, and the Candle's quietest.

**1 Leafrot, Doorsnight** ([[Hathe]]). The first night of the dying month. Every door in a house is propped open until dawn, so that anyone who has lingered can leave. The Smokes props its doors and sits up; the Keep props the hall door and the followers' lines; the Ravelers prop the Doorkeeper's Chapel, which has no door, and sit in the black doorway in a row. The priestesses of Maelis lay a loaf on the step of every Doorkeeper's Chapel on the continent, for the road. Since Hathe died the night has got longer, people say, and the doors harder to shut in the morning.

**30 Leafrot, the Culling** (Orvanne). The last day of the hunt; a stag run through Ilmareth. In the March, a goat.

**1 Frostcall, Stonefast** (Tharos). Oaths renewed on bare rock. Gerda's tally swears on the gatehouse step.

**6 Frostcall, Rope Day** ([[The Hanged Corporal]]). The first Restday of the month he died. A company's ropes are checked, nobody is hanged, and the man who knows the words says them over a cup, which is passed, and nobody drinks until it reaches the newest recruit. At the Gibbet, a new noose. Mab Lennard at [[Dunmeer]] sells strands of it to pilgrims ([[A Strand of the Corporal's Rope]]).

**20 Frostcall, Widows' Walk.** The Treaty's anniversary, a Coinday. Widows of the eight companies walk out from Fallowden and further to [[The Treaty Stone]], and the company's whole patrol walks out with the Cinderford ones at dawn and back with them at dusk, which is the one day a year the Ninth stands at the Stone in strength. Somebody polishes LIAR out from under the Regency's signature that morning and somebody scratches it back by dark. The Ninth's own mark on the schedule gets touched by every hand that passes.

**23 Frostcall, Lastwater** (Sefra). The fleets drawn up; the drowned-lists read. Nan Crupp reads the Cinder's from her hut door, and it is short, and this year will have a name on it.

**1 Longnight, the Reckoning** ([[Corvex]]). Every counting-house closes at noon and the books are read aloud. At Tollamund & Daughters, very slowly. Brother Audit reads the Row's prayers back to it on the step.

**30 Longnight, Hearthnight** (Maelis) **and the Long Veil** (Isaurana). The longest night: every door unbarred and a plate set for whoever comes, until the doors shut, and then the Veil until a dawn in no hurry. In the Smokes, since 3002, the plate is set and somebody comes.

### Cinders Muster, 14 Ashfall

The anniversary. Nobody in the March says "the anniversary" and means anything else. It is kept by everyone, differently, all at once.

**The eve, the 13th.** Vosk and his bottle at the Gibbet. At the Candle at dusk [[Mother Hennet]] stands at the slate and reads the oldest columns aloud from the top, the names with no tab under them because the tab was closed by the fire, and nobody drinks until she has finished, and she does not hurry. That is the Candle's roll. The Seventh's are read last.

**Dawn.** Muster, as every morning. The ninth year, the treaty quarter-day, and the pay notice, in 3011. The Ninth's veterans touch the cage on the way down the hill, three times, which old Pike started and nobody admits to copying.

**Morning.** [[Gerda Hollin]] walks to the Treaty Stone with a pebble, whatever day of the week it is, and the dwarves of the tally go with her.

**Dusk, the cooks' camp.** The company walks out the orchard lane to the ring of fused cookpots at the heart of [[The Weeping Orchard]], where the ninth lantern was first seen lit, and stands there in silence until the light goes, and walks back. Nobody speaks, from the bridge to the bridge. [[Brannoc Saltash]] carries the pot out and the company eats his burnt-hand stew standing, which is whatever is in the stores, scorched on the bottom on purpose, because that is how it was the first time. In 3011 Marrow cancels the muster after the pay notice, and the ring of pots stays empty, and Brannoc carries the stew down to the Candle instead, where nobody remembers to eat it.

**Dusk, the river.** The widows set lamps on the Cinder from the Low Quay stair, tallow dips on shingles, one for each name, and watch them go under the bridge. Nan Crupp does not cross while they are on the water. Some of them come to rest against the third pier.

**Night, the Rise.** The Ravelers stand in the Field of Lanterns under the eight dark iron copies and the ninth empty pole and read this year's book of last words aloud, every line, to nobody, until it is done. Townsfolk climb up to listen for a name. [[Quorl Many-Words]] has read a share of it every year since he was fledged.

**Night, the town.** Every company lights a lantern on Cinders night, and there is one company, so every house lights one instead: a dip in every window from the Ford to the Smokes, and the Reeve's standing order that the Treaty Bell is not struck after dusk on the 14th, not even for the ford closing. The ford stays open all night, so that anyone still coming home can come.

**The Ninth's lantern** does nothing in particular, which is the point.

**Dates against the sessions.** The five holy days inside the campaign are all meant: Cinders Muster is session 1, rent day is session 2's morning, the Saint's Lamp is sessions 3 and 8, the Unveiling catches the expedition on the road between 5 and 6 (Pallas dark, a Restday, so a Corporal's night: a good night for a refusal), and session 6 goes down to Uthrem on Doorsnight, with every door in Cinderford propped open behind them. No session lands on a festival by accident. Session 4 (20 Ashfall) is a plain Coinday and market day; session 5 (25 Ashfall) a plain Ironday; session 7 (5 Leafrot) a Wardday, the Reeve's court day, which is why Tollamund chose it.

**Tally's Day and Dannic.** 3 Ashfall is the day Skelt guided Dannic Rook into the Glass Barrow by the culvert and came out alone the next morning. It was Tally's Day, and Skelt was the one Glasser in the March missing from the whole of the feast, because he was holding a rope under the third pier; Ferrow Gant noticed, and has not yet said so to Vexa, and will say so to a soldier who buys him a drink and asks about Skelt by name (see [[Tally's Cut]]). Anyone who asks "what day was that?" and knows the Glassers' calendar gets the clue for free.

**Eye-Night 3011** was 17 Reaping, the night after Ansel Mote arrived. His first evening was tea at the Velvet Ledger; his second was a round at the Candle, which was full, because the company was kept out of the Smokes that night by Marrow's order. He bought the whole room a drink on Eye-Night and watched who took it. Hennet remembers; it was the first round anyone had stood in the Candle since Sallow's last payday.

**Cinders night and the bell.** The Reeve's ban on the bell runs from dusk. The three-and-three at noon in session 1 is lawful; a stroke after dark on the 14th would be the first in nine years, and the town would come out to see who dared.

**The world.md list.** The `holidays:` entries for the calendar are the dated feasts above (not the Saint's Lamp or the Unveiling, which move with the moons). The app shows each beside its date.

== GM notes ==

Use a feast as a texture, not a scene: one line of what the town is doing that day before the party gets to work. The two that earn a scene are Cinders Muster (session 1 is built on it) and Doorsnight (session 6, offstage: when the party comes home on 3 Leafrot the doors are shut again and the town is quieter than it was). Stake Day is the hook for a spring epilogue: whoever is left of the company goes out with the stakes, or nobody does.
