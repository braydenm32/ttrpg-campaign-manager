### Look and voice
A surface dwarf gone the colour of tallow, beard cut to the jaw the way the Returned cut theirs, eyes that water in daylight. He has no shadow. On cold nights he sits at the far edge of [[The Last Candle]]'s fire with both hands held out flat to it, palms down, the way you warm your hands at a fire, except that his are a foot to the left of the flames, over nothing, and he does not seem to know it. He speaks rarely and in a soft Skarn burr, always a beat late, as if waiting for somebody else to answer first.

> "It's warm enough. It's warm enough here. You go on."

> "I set two bowls. I don't know who the second one's for. I always eat it anyway."

### Who he is
Tam Hollowell was a mason out of [[Skarn]], a good one, with a wife and a workshop and a line of grave-markers cut in the Winter Pass yard. Three and a half years ago he went under the Grimmark with a deep-road party looking for kin who had not come back up with the first Returned, and a year ago he came up alone through the gate of [[Hollowhall]], casting no shadow, with two and a half years gone and nothing to say about them. His wife did not take him back. He walked east to Cinderford because, he says, it was downhill, and took a house in Tanners' Lane where the smell keeps people away. He rarely leaves it. The neighbours say he sets a place at the table for himself, opposite himself, every night.

### What he wants
Not to be asked. And, under that, to be told where the others are, by somebody who knows.

### What he knows
The deep roads under the Grimmark as far as the place where he stops remembering. The names of the eleven who went under with him. That one of them was a hunter called Halt, who was ahead of him on the road the last time he remembers anything at all.

### How he treats the PCs
With a mild, absent courtesy, until [[Borrin Halt]] walks in. Then he stands up, and the bowl drops, and he says Borrin's name, and after that he will not be in the same room as him, and will not say why.

:::gm
**Secret.** Tam was in Borrin's deep-road party. He came up six months before Borrin did, by the same gate, and he remembers the thing Borrin does not: at the breached Eighth Ward, in the dark, the party was asked for a price, and paid it, and something walked on ahead of them wearing a borrowed shape. The shape was Borrin's. Tam followed it for a long time because he thought it was Borrin. When it turned round he stopped following, and he remembers nothing after that until Hollowhall's gate. The second bowl is for whatever walked beside him up the Stair; he does not know that, and sets it anyway.

**The key.** Like the Returned smith at Hollowhall, Tam has started carving the same object over and over: a hand-long key, in a pattern he does not know, out of ash-slag and scrap wood. He burns each one when it is done. The pattern matches the keyhole in the Lithic door at the end of the Red Seam under [[The Glass Barrow]], and [[Hathe's Door-Key]]. A party that shows him the Door-Key watches him go grey and say, "That's the one. Don't take it down there."

**Borrin.** Tam is the second witness, after Mardle Ock, that something wearing Borrin's face walked the deep ahead of the party. Unlike Mardle he knows it was not Borrin, and it costs him to say so.
:::

== GM notes ==

**Voice:** soft Skarn burr, a beat late, apologetic. Hands out to the wrong place.

A quiet horror for the players to notice themselves: let them see the hands first and work out the gap. The rumour on the Last Candle table ("a dwarf in the Smokes with no shadow who sets a place at the table for himself") is him. He is a strong hook for session 6's road, when Borrin's shadow walks on ahead, if the party has met him and he refuses to come.
