### Look and voice
**Dob** is the one at the stern: a thickset halfling of fifty with a bald brown head, a pipe that is never lit on the river and never out on the bank, and a barge-pole worn to the shape of his hands. He speaks about once an hour, usually "Mind," and is always right. **Marigold** is the one at the bow: his sister, forty, braided, with a knife in her boot and the clan's tally-string round her wrist, and she speaks slightly more than Dob, usually to Regent. **Regent** is the cat: large, grey, one-eared, named for the stolen officer's hat he sleeps in, who hates everyone on the river equally and is therefore the only creature aboard the whole clan trusts.

> Dob: "Mind."

> Marigold: "She says a penny for the crossing and a story for the tea. Regent says nothing. Regent's the one to watch."

> Regent: (looks at you, then at the deck boards, then at you)

### Who they are
[[Kettle Brindlewake]]'s cousins on her mother's side, and the whole crew of [[The Grey Dumpling]] on a normal run: Dob poles, Marigold ropes, Kettle talks. They were born on the Ardis, came up the Cinder with Kettle when she bought the barge, and have never asked what is in the false floor, which is why they are still her crew. They sleep aboard. They eat what Kettle cooks. They say little, and nothing to the gate-watch.

### What they want
A quiet river, a dry hold, and Kettle alive at the end of the season, in that order. Marigold would like Regent to like her. Regent would not.

### What they know
Every snag, shelf and shallow between Cinderford and Sevenhaven. Who came aboard, when, and whether they were nervous. Where Kettle keeps the lever for the false floor. What is in it, near enough, because a hold has only so many smells; they have never asked, because they have sense.

### How they treat the PCs
Dob nods. Marigold says "Captain's aft," and that is the welcome. A PC who helps pole through the ford shallows without being asked earns a word from Dob, which is something to tell people about. Regent sits in the lap of whoever most dislikes cats.

:::gm
**Secret.** They know what is under the false floor: four casks of the Ninth Lantern's good oil, bought from Fennick for bread and never resold, and a warm crate that hums ([[The Glass-Fused Hand]]). On the deck, which they step round, a small sealed box addressed to "the Warden, Ninth Stone". They will not speak of any of it to the party, the gate-watch, or the Quiet Office, whose letters Kettle reads before she delivers them. If Kettle is arrested, Dob poles the Dumpling downriver by night with everything aboard, and Marigold goes to the Cinder ferry to tell Nan Crupp, which is how the town finds out.

**Regent.** The rumour that Regent is a Quiet Office cat is false, and nobody can prove it. He does, however, sit on Mote's letters.
:::
