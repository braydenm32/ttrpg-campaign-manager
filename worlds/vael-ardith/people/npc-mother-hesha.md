### Look and voice
Sixty, grey-maned, broad, with three parallel scars down the left cheek from a siege-hook forty years ago that she has never had tattooed over. She wears the iron fist of the Mothers on a chain and a sapper's burn-scars on both forearms, because she was one before she was a Mother, and Skarra got the trade from her. Speaks slowly, in whole sentences, with long silences that are not for effect.

> "A daughter who writes every month is telling you something she will not put in the letter."

> "The council does not act. The council decides. Then somebody's daughter acts."

### Who she is
In the orc way she is **Hesha, Ghorra's daughter**, and she holds a hearth: three companies' writs and the sappers' schools of two garrison towns. She sits on [[Grashka]]'s council, speaks rarely, and is listened to when she does. Her daughter [[Skarra]] is the Ninth Lantern's sapper and has written home from Cinderford every month for years: the powder store, the walls, the recruits, the weather, and since the spring, an exile with nine knuckles who signed on at half pay.

### What she wants
The wards shut and the companies' word unbroken, in that order, like every Mother. And her daughter home, which she will not say and does not write.

### What she knows
Everything Skarra has seen at the Keep, a month late. Where [[Varra Nine-Knuckles]] is, and that she is alive, and that she has stood a wall. That Grashka has not asked after her granddaughter once in council, and reads Hesha's face every time a letter comes.

### How she treats the PCs
She never meets them unless the GM brings the Mothers to the Rent. By letter she is formal, exact and entirely unsentimental. Varra, if she ever learns Hesha has been following her, gets one line: "The Iron does not stop watching because it stopped speaking."

:::gm
**Secret.** Hesha is the Mothers' eyes on Varra, and the council knows where Varra is because of her. She has read every letter about "the exile with nine knuckles" aloud to Grashka in private, by Grashka's order, and watched the Mother of Mothers say nothing. It is Hesha who carries the council's answer in session 7: Grashka's letter, under Grashka's seal, in Hesha's own hand because Grashka's shakes now. The second ink is Grashka's.

**The Rust Choir.** Varra's mother's company, which took the Fallowden clearance contract and lost its best file when Varra refused, is under Hesha's hearth. It is between contracts and free to be hired. A party that writes to the Mothers, or that Varra speaks for, can have it: forty orc pikes at the Rent in session 8, if the price is Varra's oath to the Iron again, or something harder.
:::

== GM notes ==

**Voice:** slow, concrete, no flourish. She never lies; she declines.

Hesha exists so the Mothers have a face that is not Grashka's. Use her for the letters, for a Fist who arrives at the Keep with an inspection and a short message, and as the one who can bring the Rust Choir to the Rent. Do not bring her to Cinderford in person before session 7.
