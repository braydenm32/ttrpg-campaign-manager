A slight, soft-voiced Vesh with silver-dusted wings folded under a signaller's coat and antennae that twitch toward every light. Noume works the shuttered lamps and flares on the signal tower at night, sending lamp-code to the patrols on the ash, and is the only person in the company who can hold a conversation with a sentry two miles away. Off duty, Noume talks with flickering hands out of habit.

> "Patrol says: road buried, all well, send soup. Patrol always says send soup."

Like every Vesh, Noume is drawn to magic the way a cat finds the one warm stone in the yard, and drifts toward the lantern's cage between watches without meaning to. Noume thinks [[Ysse Lampwright]] is the most interesting person to arrive in years, and is too shy to say so.

:::gm
Noume can see that the world goes thin around the lantern, the way it does near a ward, and has decided, on principle, to ignore it, as Vesh do. Asked directly by a PC who has earned trust, Noume will admit it. Noume has also seen Borrin where Borrin was not: from the signal tower over the Smokes, standing in the street below at an hour when he was on watch with Vosk. On the red moon night, Noume signals the far patrols and gets back a message from nobody: three flashes from inside the Rent. It is the Last Wardens' old lamp-code, [[Warden Uthrem]] answering the flame from far past where any lamp should carry. In session 6 Noume reads it in the Rent and understands what it said. (What it says, and the drum and bell codes beside it: [[Signals of Cinderford]].)
:::
