### Look and voice
A lean woman in her thirties with cropped dark hair, a surveyor's coat too good for the ash, and feet that are a map of white scars: she dowses barefoot, walking the glass with her eyes shut until she bleeds, and the blood runs toward the metal. She carries a notebook in oilcloth and writes in it in a very small hand. Educated voice, Caer Ardith vowels, roughened by three years of shouting down pits.

> "Everybody can find glass. I find what's in it. The difference is about a pint a week."

> "Vexa digs where it's interesting. I tell her where it's expensive. We are both usually right."

### Who she is
Ottoline Crane came east on the Ash Road in 3006, alone, with a mule and a surveyor's chain, a year after [[Fort Mercy]] burned, and walked into the Glassers' camp asking whether anyone had pulled papers out of the fort's cellar. Nobody had. She stayed. Within a year she was finding more metal under the glass than any digger with a pick, and within two [[Vexa Glassjaw]] had made her second, which among Glassers means the one who is allowed to argue. She keeps the dig tallies, settles the fights Vexa cannot be bothered with, and has read the half-burned papers in the records vault, which Vexa never has.

### What she wants
To finish something a dead man started, and to understand it first. She is not a Glasser for the money, though she takes it.

### What she knows
Where every worked thing under the glass for a mile round the fort lies, by feel. The dig tallies back to 3006. What is in the records vault. That the writing in the cellar is older than the fort by a thousand years, and that [[The Cold Seam]] below it was dug, not found. That Brakk went down the Cold Seam alone.

### How she treats the PCs
As the first people in three years she can talk to about paper. She is cool, exact and curious, and warms visibly to anyone who can read anything: Ysse's lamp-craft, Borrin's dwarf-script, Quorl's ledgers. She will not cross Vexa in front of the crews, and will tell the party, privately, exactly how far Vexa can be pushed and on what.

:::gm
**Secret.** She is Ilbert Crane's granddaughter. Crane was Master of Works to Aurel IX and ran [[The Emperor's Digs]] from 2979, filed as *Drainage Works (Western)*; his papers vanished when the Quiet Office burned Fort Mercy's vault in 3005, the fire that killed 261. Ottoline, raised in Caer Ardith on his stories of a stone that leaned over a chasm, came east after the fire to find what survived. The half-burned papers in the records vault are his: she recognised the hand on the first sheet. Among them is a section drawing of a shaft beneath "the Mercy camp" going down to a chamber with writing in it, and below the chamber a seam, marked in his hand *cold; stop here*. Vexa's diggers broke into the chamber from above without knowing the drawing existed. Ottoline has not told Vexa whose papers they are, because Vexa would sell them. [[A Lithic Road-Marker]] laid on the floor of the black-glass chamber turns to point down the seam along the line of her grandfather's section drawing; she will trade a great deal to hold it for an hour, and she is the one who tells the party it points at the Stone.

She knows the third of Crane's three digs was at the foot of [[The Ninth Stone]], and she wants to see it before anyone else does. A party that shows her the Barrow lintel's line, or the rubbing from the Stone, gets her as an ally for the rest of the campaign; a party that tells Vexa what she is loses her.

**Session 2.** She is in the yard when the party arrives, and the one who notices they have come for the strongbox before Vexa does. She can be the voice that tells Vexa to deal rather than fight. If the party reads the cellar writing, she is the one who writes it down.
:::

== GM notes ==

**Voice:** precise, dry, a scholar who learned to swear in a dig-pit. Uses exact figures. Does not say "I think".

She is the Glassers' head and Vexa their hands: let the party realise this on the second visit, not the first. In sessions 5 and 6 she trades her grandfather's section drawing of the Stone's foot for a place on the expedition, and she keeps her word.
