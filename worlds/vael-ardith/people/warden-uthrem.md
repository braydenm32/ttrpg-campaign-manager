### Look and voice
From the waist down he is stone, grown into the rock at the foot of [[The Ninth Stone]] like a root, on the Warden's Shelf, four hundred feet below the north lip. Above that he is grey, granular and cracked, skin halfway to granite, with veins of dull red light where the stone has split. One arm can still move, slowly. His face is long and grave and very old, with deep-set eyes like wet slate, and his beard has gone to grey crystal. Ash has drifted up over his shoulders. Every few minutes he breathes, a long grinding sigh.

He speaks only on the out-breath: a long grinding sigh, then a sentence or two, old and formal, then the sigh again. The flame being near loosens him a little, and he manages more.

> "You came." *(The sigh.)* "I have been counting. Nine years." *(The sigh.)* "I was beginning to doubt my arithmetic."

> "Do not be sorry for me. I chose this standing." *(The sigh.)* "Be sorry for the ones who did not get to choose."

> "Forgive the lack of chairs." *(The sigh.)* "I have been meaning to see to it for forty years."

### Who he is
**Uthrem of the Ninth**, last of that line, is one of [[The Last Wardens]], the handful of [[The Lithic]] who still tend the Great Wards. He came to the Ninth Stone in 2971, forty years ago, to stand: to let the stone take him, as his people do, and strengthen the ward with his own slow petrification. In 2979 he watched the Emperor's diggers cut at the stone's foot, and could not move to stop them. He was nearly done turning when the [[The Battle of Cinders|Battle of Cinders]] cracked the stone above him, and the Rent opened round his pillar. Since that night he has been holding on, half-made, unable to finish turning and unable to leave. He has stood at the Stone forty years and waited nine since the crack.

### What he wants
A new heart for the ward. The crack cannot be sealed with stone or spells. It needs what the Nine gave: a living heart, willing, poured in. He would give his own, but there is too little of it left.

### What he knows
The truth about [[The Godslaying]]. That [[The Nine]] killed [[Ysmereth]], whose name only the Lithic keep, and that a god cannot fully die. That each ward has one of the Nine inside it, still awake. That the lantern in [[Cinderford]] was lit from a piece of this stone, blown loose in the battle, and that its flame is what's been holding the crack shut. That the flame is failing.

### How he treats the PCs
With immense, tired relief. He has rehearsed what he'll say for nine years. He tells them the truth, all of it, because there is no time for less.

:::gm
**Secret and purpose.** Uthrem has waited nine years for someone who can hear the flame: "the one the flame calls". When [[Ysse Lampwright]] arrives (**Into the Rent**, session 6), he says her name before she gives it. The Ninth inside the stone has told him about her.

He knows the three ways forward (the endings): reseal (a heart for the ward: his, which buys about twenty years with the Standing rite and perhaps only one without it; a willing PC's; or Ysse's), let it crack, or take the flame. He will tell the PCs the cost of each honestly. He will beg them not to take the flame: "Whoever holds it will hear him." *(The sigh.)* "And he is very persuasive." *(The sigh.)* "He persuaded me once."

He also recognises at once what wears [[Borrin Halt]]'s shadow, and is afraid of it.
:::

== GM notes ==

**Voice:** formal, one out-breath at a time; the grinding sigh is the pause, not an ellipsis. Use him to deliver the campaign's big truths, but make each truth cost him visibly: a new crack, a red glow, a breath he struggles to take.

**Across the arc:**
1–5. Never seen. Hints only: the dream of a stone with a crack and someone sitting at its foot; a rubbing from the Ninth Stone ([[A Rubbing from the Ninth Stone]]) shows a figure carved at the base that wasn't there in old drawings.
6. **Into the Rent**: the PCs reach him. He tells the truth about the Godslaying, the wards and the flame.
7. If the PCs leave him, he holds on. He sends the party back with a message for whoever will listen.
8. At the end, if resealing is chosen, he offers himself first; he cannot climb, so someone has to lift him the ten feet into the crack. If a PC or Ysse goes instead, he holds their hand while it happens, and then finally lets himself finish turning to stone.
