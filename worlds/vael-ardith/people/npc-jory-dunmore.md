Lanky, red-haired, freckled, and one half of the Dunmore twins, the best crossbow pair in the March and the worst company on a long march. Jory and his brother [[Jessom Dunmore]] have never once agreed about anything: which of them is older (Jory, he says), the range to the next ridge, whether it's raining, the colour of the Captain's eyes, whether the drowned man was actually drowned.

> "Two hundred paces." "Hundred and eighty." "Two hundred." "You've been wrong since the womb, Jory." "*I* came out first."

They shoot together, though, and when they do, nothing within two hundred paces (or a hundred and eighty) lives long. Jory is the steadier of the two, a little slower, very loyal to [[Captain Marrow]].

== GM notes ==

Sergeant Vosk lends the twins to the PCs for the ash crossing in session 2. Let them argue the entire way and be right exactly half the time. See Jessom for the twist.
