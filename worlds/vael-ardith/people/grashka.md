### Look and voice
Eighty years old and still the tallest orc in any room: stooped, white-maned, broad as a gate, her grey skin seamed with sixty years of scars. Her tusks are capped in iron. She walks with a pike for a staff and has never once leaned on it in public. Her eyes are pale yellow and very steady. She wears no crown and no jewel; her only ornament is a ring of knuckle-bones on a cord, one for every orc she has exiled.

She does not repeat herself, so people learn to listen the first time. What she says arrives low and flat, like a millstone turning over.

> "A contract is the only wall we have, child. Everyone hates us. Everyone needs us. The contract is what stands between those two things."

> "I have done worse things than you can imagine, and I would do them all again. Do not mistake that for pride."

### Who she is
In the orc way she is **Grashka, Ulvra's daughter**, and she is **Mother of Mothers**: first among [[The Mothers of the Iron]], the orc matriarchs who license, bind and judge every orc mercenary company in [[Vael Ardith]]. Her word sends companies to war and calls them off the field.

She heard [[Varra Nine-Knuckles]]'s case herself, and cast her out herself, and took the knuckle Varra put into her hand: the one Varra lost at twelve in a portcullis chain, which her mother had kept.

### What she wants
The wards shut. The companies paid. The orcs alive. In that order.

### What she knows
What happens if a ward is ever broken.

:::gm
**Who she was.** She is Varra's grandmother. Thirty years ago she was a commander of the Iron Guard, the orc soldier caste the empire bred in 2104 to guard its emperors. Everyone knows the Guard murdered Emperor Aurel IX in [[The Kingslaying]]; nobody outside the Mothers knows whose hand struck. She knows why the Emperor died.

**Secret.** Grashka is the Kingslayer. In 2981, the Mothers learned (from a cook, of all people) that [[Aurel IX]] was secretly digging at three ward sites, trying to open one ([[The Emperor's Digs]]). They ordered him killed. On 14 Frostcall 2981, during an audience for petitions on the bean tax in the Gilded Gallery at [[Caer Ardith]], Grashka stepped out of the Guard's line and struck the blow herself. [[Sergeant Vosk]] stood at the door. The Mothers have never told anyone why, because nobody would believe orcs, and because explaining would tell the world where the wards are weak.

Casting Varra out broke her heart. Whether she chose that contract for the Rust Choir knowing Varra would refuse it, and so put her granddaughter where the Mothers needed eyes, she will never say.
:::

== GM notes ==

Grashka should appear late if at all. In session 7 her letter comes by a Fist of the Iron ([[Grashka's Letter]]). If Varra wrote to the Mothers, the Rust Choir (forty orc pikes) catches the column at dusk on 8 Leafrot and holds part of the lip in session 8; if she did not, only the Fist who carried the letter marches east and speaks for the Mothers at the lip. Grashka herself is an optional cameo at the lip, silent, if you want a big entrance. Her offer: the Mothers will back resealing the ward. The price: Varra comes home, and swears to the Iron again. Or something harder.
