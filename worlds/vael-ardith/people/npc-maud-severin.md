Fifty, grey-haired, sleeves rolled, hands steady as a carpenter's until about the fourth drink, after which they're steadier. Maud has sewn up three armies and sawn off more legs than she can count, and she counts everything. She sews with fishing line because it holds, and keeps a list of her dead pinned inside her apron.

> "Bite this. No, the *leather*, not me. There. You'll live, more's the pity. Next."

She prays to [[The Hanged Corporal]], the common soldiers' saint, every night: not reverently, but the way you'd write to a friend who owes you. Lately she swears he's been answering. Her patients have started recovering from things they shouldn't.

She does not like [[Sister Seraphine]]'s mission healing soldiers for nothing. "Nothing is a very high price," she says.

== GM notes ==

A grounded, funny, sad healer for the PCs to come back to. If the Saint's church starts healing the company in session 4, Maud's infirmary empties, and she takes it personally. If she lives to session 8, let the Hanged Corporal answer her once, out loud.
