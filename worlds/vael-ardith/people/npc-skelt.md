### Look and voice
A thin man of forty with a digger's stoop, glass-dust grey in every crease of him, and the hands of someone who stopped feeling cuts years ago: white scars to the elbow, two fingers on the left hand that do not quite close. He smells of stone-pear spirit at any hour. He talks fast and looks past you while he does it, at the door.

> "Chart's two silver. Accurate chart's two silver and I don't know you well enough."

> "I held the rope. That's all I did. You hold a rope, you're not responsible for what's on the other end of it."

### Who he is
Skelt is one of Vexa's sixty, and not one of her best: a [[Fort Mercy]] digger who is better at knowing where things are than at digging them out. He came east with the first Glasser gangs in 3006 and has been under more glass than most men alive, and he drinks to keep that from mattering. On market days he walks the twenty-two miles to [[Gutter Market]] with a roll of charts under his arm and sells them to pilgrims, treasure-hunters and soldiers with a week's pay: the Barrow, the Rent, the Burned Mill cellars. Most are copied. Some are invented. Two silver either way.

He is known in [[The Last Candle]] and not welcome there, because the Glassers say, and Glassers talk, that he went into [[The Glass Barrow]] with [[Dannic Rook]] a fortnight ago and came out without him.

### What he wants
To be paid, to be left alone, and not to go back under the Barrow. He has stopped sleeping well, and he is a man who used to sleep anywhere.

### What he knows
The Barrow's seven rooms and the two ways in and out. Where the spiders nest. What sits in the chair in the Cold Room, and that it does not move until you lift something. That there is a door at the end of the Red Seam with a keyhole and no handle, and a red line under the glass in front of it. A good deal about what Vexa has in her cellar that he has been told, very slowly, not to mention.

### How he treats the PCs
As buyers until they mention Dannic, then as a threat. He sells them a chart. He sells them a better chart if they look like they would notice the difference. He tells them the truth for drink, in the Candle, in a corner, in a whisper; or for his skin, at Fort Mercy, when the party has him against a wall and Vexa is not looking.

:::gm
**Secret.** On 3 Ashfall, for a cut of whatever was in the bundle, Skelt took Dannic Rook into the Barrow by the culvert at low water, past the spiders, down the Cold Stair, and held the rope while Dannic set his stash down in a ring round the Cold Captain's chair and chalked *D.R. 3 Ashfall. Back for the rest* on the wall. Dannic wanted to stay and look at the Red Seam door. Skelt did not. He came out alone by the culvert the next morning; Dannic came out later by the Quay cut, and Skelt did not know that until the wanted bill went up and he realised nobody was asking him questions. He has told nobody what Dannic cached, because he never saw inside the bundle, and he thinks it was Concord coin. It was twelve new Regency shillings and some paper.

His Gutter Market chart of the Rent is a copy of Pike's ([[A Glasser's Chart]]) with the Choir and the Halfway Ledge left off, because a chart that gets you killed is bad for repeat trade; the rest of his stock is copied from dig tallies at Fort Mercy and padded.

**Session 2.** At Fort Mercy he is the Glasser who will talk. For a jug of stone-pear spirit, or at knife-point, he gives up the culvert, the chair, and "the lad went back for the rest and didn't come up my way". He does not know [[Ansel Mote]]. He saw a sealed House cart in the yard on the 7th and was sent to the pits before it was unloaded.
:::

== GM notes ==

**Voice:** quick, hoarse, eyes on the exit. Prices everything. Goes quiet at the word "chair".

If the party pressed Fennick or Spoons about the Barrow in session 1, Skelt's name comes up; use him to connect the ford to Fort Mercy before the wagon's trail does. If he is killed, his charts go on sale at Gutter Market the next Coinday at double, which tells the party someone at Fort Mercy clears the dead quickly.
