Seventy, which is young for an elf: slim, freckled, round-faced, with only three tattoos (all on one wrist, all about exams) and ink on every finger from copying. Merane lives in the Temple Row rooms [[Aelith Sorn]] keeps, in the little room off the stair, and carries [[Aelith Sorn]]'s satchels, copies her notes, and asks every question that comes into her head, usually twice. Her mother is [[Liriel Tarn]], warden of the Wild and keeper of the Unlettered at Ilmareth, who has one tattoo and did not approve of the three.

> "Sorry, but does the ash always taste like that? And is that a real Raveler? Can I ask him about his feathers? Sorry. Sorry."

She adores her teacher. She is beginning to notice things about her teacher that she wishes she hadn't.

**Wants:** to be a Remembrancer, and to be right about Aelith being kind. **Fears:** that the second wish will not survive the first.

:::gm
Merane found, in Aelith's papers, a list of the equipment needed to "preserve a skin record intact": a cutting table, salt, a lead-lined case. She has not let herself understand it yet. Treated kindly, she will warn the PCs before Aelith moves on [[Sorrel Vey]] in session 7, and then she will have to decide whether to go home to [[Sael]] at all. She slips it as a note under a door, or a whisper at the Row's end, with her satchel already packed.
:::
