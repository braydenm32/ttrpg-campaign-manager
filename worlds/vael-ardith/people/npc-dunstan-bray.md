A broad, ruddy, slow-smiling man with hands like shovels, a laugh that fills a barracks and a gift for remembering birthdays. Corporal Bray buys drinks he can't afford, lends money he doesn't have, and always seems to know who's angry and why.

> "Nobody's saying *mutiny*, lad. We're just saying *hungry*. Out loud. Together."

His family served the Sallows in the [[Fallowden]] country for three generations. He followed the Lieutenant into the company and would follow him anywhere.

:::gm
Bray counts heads for [[Garrick Sallow]]'s mutiny: who's wavering, who's loyal, who could be bought with a hot meal. He knows nothing about the oil and nothing of [[Ansel Mote]]; he thinks the silver Sallow spends is family money.

**Where he turns up.** He is the big man who starts the brawl in session 1. In session 3 he is "the lieutenant's man" the oil-store intruder name-drops: he was to let the ash-carrier in at the oil store's yard door "as a favour to the lieutenant", asking no questions, which fits his knowing nothing about the oil. In session 4 he either fights (use the bandit captain stats the session gives him, AC 15, HP 65) or turns himself in, depending on how hard the PCs pressed. If the PCs pry into the mutiny in sessions 4 to 5, Bray is told to "have a word" with them, and brings friends. Caught and pressed, he folds, weeping, and tells everything he knows, which is only enough to hang Sallow. If he talks, Mote kills him in session 7.

**Where he is from.** Bray is one of the forty children of [[Dunmeer]] (about 26 now; eleven on 12 Frostcall 2996). The Brays were tenants of a Sallow farm two miles off, and he was staying with his grandmother in the village. He stood in the line past the Tree, saw the Corporal put down the torch, and has never said where he is from. He hears the Corporal when nobody else does, and Sallow does not. Turn him in session 4 with the Dunmeer story, a strand of rope from [[Mab Lennard]], or "Dask's rule" said with weight.
:::
