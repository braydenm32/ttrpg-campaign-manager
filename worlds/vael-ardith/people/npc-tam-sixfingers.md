A rangy, weather-burned woman of forty with a brass-bound cudgel, a laugh like gravel in a bucket and six fingers on her left hand. Tam keeps the peace at [[Gutter Market]] for [[The Glassers]], which makes her the only law on the Low Quay. Picks are left at the bridge; anyone who starts a fight is thrown in the Cinder; anyone who cheats a Glasser is never seen at market again.

> "Six. Since I was born. Not ward-glass, not a curse, not the Sleeper's mark. My mam counted. Every market day somebody asks."

She knows every face that has ever bought or sold on her stones.

:::gm
Tam has noticed Jaspin Sweet paying out new Concord silver (the escort's travelling purse, though she doesn't know that) and is annoyed she isn't getting her cut. Ask her the right way and she'll tell the PCs exactly which Glasser crew sold it to him, and that they came in from Fort Mercy.
:::
