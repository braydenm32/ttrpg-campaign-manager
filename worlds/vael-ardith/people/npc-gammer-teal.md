### Look and voice
Ninety, bent double, brown as a kipper, with one eye black and one eye the flat pewter grey of the lake, which she took, she says, "the night the water came up and I didn't go down with it". She sits on a stool in the first doorway by the jetty from dawn to dusk with a line in the water and a blanket over her knees, and she has not slept a whole night in five years. Her voice is thin and flat and does not waste anything.

> "You want it pretty, go to the Widow. You want it true, sit down and shut up."

> "It said 'not yet'. Not 'no'. Not 'never'. I've had five years to think about the difference."

### Who she is
Gammer Teal was born in the pear country and lived eighty-odd years with two ordinary eyes, and then on 9 Frostcall 3006 the grey lakes rose in a night and drowned the shore houses ([[The Drowning of Stillwater]]), and she stood on the jetty with the water at her chin and did not go down, and in the morning her left eye had gone grey. She is late-eyed: not born [[The Hollowborn|Hollowborn]], but seeing what they see since that night, which includes the dead standing on the lake bed and, in her dreams at the full Ember, a stone door with a hand on the latch from the inside. Her grandchildren are the Teals of [[The Smokes]], one of the Hollowborn families; six of them came out to the lake in 3004, and she came with them to mind the babies, and stayed.

She is eldest at [[Stillwater]], which means she settles the fish-shares and nothing else, and she is the only person on the lake [[The Dreaming Widow]] did not convert.

### What she wants
To be left alone, to die with both eyes shut, and for nobody to go near the Rent. She is not frightened of the dead under the water. She is frightened of what they are waiting for.

### What she knows
What the Hollowborn dream, plainly, because she dreams it too and did not grow up with it: a stone door, a lamp, a hand on the latch from the inside, and lately a voice. That the dead under the lake take one step toward the jetty at every full Ember. That the Widow's sermons are her own dreams, said kindly.

### How she treats the PCs
As fools until proved otherwise, and then as fools she likes. She tells them the truth flat and once. [[Tibbet Marlowe]] she looks at for a long time and then says, "You're one of Ottilie's six. You've stopped, then. Good." [[Borrin Halt]] she will not let in the house.

:::gm
**Secret.** Once, the winter after the drowning, the voice in the dream spoke to her alone and said "not yet", warmly, and she has not slept properly since. She knows the difference between that voice and the Hollowborn dream the way the lamp-keeper knows the difference between the two voices in the flame: it was too kind. She has told nobody, because the Widow would make a sermon of it.

**The standing village.** From the jetty at low water, when the Ember is up, the Hollowborn see a village standing whole on the lake bed under the dead: a pear-country hamlet the glass took in the war. Gammer Teal knows the way to its door by heart from dreaming it, and can guide a party to it across the shallows at the full Ember: a scene for session 3 or 5, not a dungeon. The door is shut. There is a lamp in the window.

Her dreams match the Widow's sermons line for line because they are the same dream; she is the proof, if the party wants it, that the Widow is not lying, only wrong.
:::

== GM notes ==

**Voice:** flat, thin, no pity, dry as the ash. Says it once.

She is the Sleepless' natural counterweight: the one person the Hollowborn trust who says do not wake it. Use her when the party needs the truth about the dream without a cult attached, and let Tick's player hear her say "you've stopped" before anyone else says it.
