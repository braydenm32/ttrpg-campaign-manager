The notice arrived on every garrison desk in the Ashenreach on 1 Hollowfrost 3009 AW. It was three pages long and began: *"In the interest of the stability of the Realm, the Regency Council wishes to announce a temporary adjustment to certain obligations."*

**What it meant.** The Regency would stop paying its share of the garrison's wages. The other signatories, it was understood, would carry the load. They had no intention of doing so. By spring, the Concord, Iskaveth and the Grimmark had made similar adjustments, quietly, in smaller print.

**Who signed it.** [[Marius Vell]], Regent-Chancellor, who has never been seen to smile and was said to have read the notice aloud twice to be certain it said nothing.

**The Concord's move.** Concord houses bought the garrison's wages at four in ten the same afternoon. The Regency, they noted, would never repay. They would.

**What it left.** A garrison with no master. A chain of IOUs running from Cinderford to Sevenhaven. A company, the Ninth Lantern, paid late and then barely. A growing awareness that the Ashenreach is not a neutral march any more but an account, and that someone in Sevenhaven is patient enough to wait for it to mature.

**How it is told now.** In the Keep, with swearing. In Caer Ardith, with a shrug. In the counting-houses, as the day the Ashenreach became an asset.

:::gm
Vell ordered the stoppage on purpose. A garrison that is not paid will mutiny, and a mutiny gives the Quiet Office an excuse to move into the Ashenreach and take the lantern. The notice is a lit fuse. The Council still votes the Ninth's wages every quarter, and the Treasury still signs them out, "to Account Deferred, by order of the Regent-Chancellor".
:::
