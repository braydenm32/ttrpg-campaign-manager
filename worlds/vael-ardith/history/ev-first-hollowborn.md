The first six of them were born on the night of Cinders. A midwife in the Smokes delivered a boy in a cellar while the sky burned, and noticed that his left eye did not follow her candle.

**The recognition.** For two years the births went unremarked. A child who stares at an empty corner is not news in a town of ruins, and a baby who walks at four months is a wonder for a week. It was a Raveler, [[Mother Corvane]], who put the pieces together in the spring of 3004 AW. She had been burying the dead of Cinderford, and noticed that a certain number of children kept pointing at the graves.

**What they are.** [[The Hollowborn]]: born where a ward has failed, touched by the dreams of the buried god. One eye sees the dead. Many were born on the night of the battle, or in the days after, and more have been born since, in the Ashenreach and beyond. Dwarves, humans and halflings all bear them. The six born in Cinderford on the night itself are stranger still: they grew from cradle to grown in about seven years, and nobody knows why. Every Hollowborn born since has aged like anyone else. A few were born earlier, at the breached Eighth Ward from the winter of 2993-4, and a few grown men and women took the grey eye that night, late; nobody counted those for years.

**How they live.** Mostly quietly. Some are afraid. Some are proud. A few are drawn, as if on a string, to the Rent.

**What it left.** A new people, or an old one made visible. A kind of grief that has two eyes. And a rumour, spreading, that the Hollowborn dream.

**How it is told now.** In the Smokes, as a gift. In the Regency, as a problem. In the Church of the Unburnt Saint, as a sign. The Hollowborn themselves say only that they are tired of being told what they are.

:::gm
Every Hollowborn dreams the same dream at full red moon, and it is getting clearer. Tibbet Marlowe was one of the first six, born that first night in the Smokes, and the midwife in that cellar was his own grandmother. Why he and the other Cinders-born grew so fast is [[Why did the Cinders-born grow up so fast?]].
:::

== GM notes ==

Hollowborn PCs and NPCs should feel like neighbours, not omens. Let them be funny, rude and practical. The dream is the thing that sets them apart, and it can come later.
