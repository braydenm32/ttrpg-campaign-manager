This is the thing the elves of [[Sael]] do not know that they know.

By 1102 AW the elves had already begun to lose their memory: a century at a time, the oldest of them dimmed and the ink on their skin blurred. A circle of loremasters, the first [[The Remembrancers|Remembrancers]], came to believe that the cause was the buried god, and that the buried god could be reasoned with. They went to the Fourth Ward at the heart of the Saelwood to open it a hand's breadth and speak to the [[The Sleeper Below|Sleeper]].

**What happened.** The ward opened. Something answered. Nobody now agrees what, because the elves who were there did their best to make sure nobody would. The ward was forced shut. Eleven of the circle died in the doing. The survivors decided that the only mercy was forgetting, and arranged it.

**The cost.** Memory was burned out of Sael on purpose: records, songs, and the skin of every elf who had borne a tattoo of the rite. They cut away the ink and burned it in the clearing where the ward lay. The clearing is a ring of burnt ground; the tree stands alone in the middle of it, grown through the ward. The Fourth Ward has been weak ever since, and the elves' forgetting has been worse, as if the god had heard them and was holding onto what they gave it.

**What survives.** A few elves still wear ink they do not understand, among them [[Sorrel Vey]], whose back records the full rite in a script she was taught as a girl to call *decorative*, inked on her by her grandmother, a dying Remembrancer who would not burn the record.

:::gm
The Trespass is the most dangerous knowledge in the setting for Sael, and a prize for the Remembrancers' present-day heirs, who will pay any price to recover it. Aelith Sorn suspects. Sorrel Vey does not. If her tattoos are read, the Trespass is read, and the Fourth Ward's weakness becomes a target for every faction with a use for a god-sized door.
:::

== GM notes ==

Never delivered as a lecture. It should reach the players in pieces: a tattooed back in the Velvet Ledger, an elf who loses a name mid-sentence, a clearing in the Saelwood with a single tree in it. The PCs can learn that Sael did something. They should have to work to learn that it was this.
