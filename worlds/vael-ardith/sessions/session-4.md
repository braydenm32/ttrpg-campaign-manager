## Session 4: The House of Unburnt Light
*Level 3. Coinday, 20 Ashfall 3011 AW.*

- *Sky*: Pallas past full and waning; the Ember waning too, rising later each night, its glow gone to copper.
- *Weather*: clear and cold, the first clean sky in a month. You can see the House's white wall from the Keep.
- *Touch these*: Tallow wakes nearly blind, names Ysse keeper in front of witnesses, and warns her off Seraphine without saying why. Fennick comes back grey-faced from a visit by Tollamund's clerk. Old Pike watches Sallow buy a round and says, to nobody: "Seventh had a lieutenant like that. Generous man. We buried him first." Corvane sends to ask Quorl, casually, whether anyone has said anything "odd" lately. At the House gate a dog barks at the empty air behind Borrin until a Lamp-Warden carries it off. After the House, Ysse dreams in the other voice, the sweeter one: *Somewhere warm. Keep me somewhere warm.*
- *In between* (17 to 19 Ashfall): Tallow sleeps a day and wakes nearly blind. Sallow's people go quiet and careful. If anyone pries into the mutiny, Bray is told to "have a word" with them, and brings friends. Kettle's Cargo can run any of these nights. Nib, the Assessor's errand boy, paid in biscuits, knows Mote keeps a second ledger bound in black and locks it in the desk; for a bag of biscuits he'll leave the window unlatched, any time between now and session 5. Today is Coinday, when the post-bag leaves for Caer Ardith: Kettle can copy a page of Mote's for the party, for silver or for friendship ("Report on the lamp. Progress satisfactory.").
- *Level-up*: none. The party stays level 3 through session 5.
- *If the party…* ignores the letter, Seraphine comes to them: to the Keep gate at noon with the little girl, a cart of hot bread for the whole company, and an invitation to Marrow that she makes in front of a hundred hungry soldiers. Sallow loses his audience for a day. The garden door scene can still happen; somebody in the party will want to see the House after that.

**Goal.** Learn what the Saint's church wants with the flame, and survive its kindness.

---

### Scene 1: The letter
A child in a clean white smock brings [[A Perfumed Letter]] to the Keep gate and waits for an answer. Everyone at the Keep has an opinion. Vosk: "Go. Eat everything. Sign nothing." Tallow, from his bed, blind: "Don't let her hold the lamp. Don't let her hold *you*." He will not say why.

---

### Scene 2: The House
> A mile out on the Ash Road a wall rises on the right, whitewashed, the only white thing between here and the sky, and over it the tops of trees. Green trees. Through the gate a courtyard swept clean of ash, a fountain running, a long low house with a lamp burning in every window in daylight, and the smell of bread.

[[The House of Unburnt Light]] is warm, beautiful and a little too comfortable: hot bread from Brother Caddis's ovens, an infirmary of thirty clean beds where the company's worst cases are sitting up and laughing, the pear garden, a bath-house fed from the fountain, Halcyran wine, and a lutenist at supper. [[Seraphine Ashe]] receives them in white and saffron. She is gracious, curious, and very good at listening. She offers to "save" the flame: carry it to Halcyra, where the Saint will keep it safe forever.

Every hour in the House, each PC makes a Wisdom save (DC 13) or finds themselves wanting to agree. She courts Marrow openly, through the party: the best guest room, a standing invitation, an offer of the Saint's coin for the company's arrears.

**The private moments.** Each PC gets a few minutes alone with Seraphine, in the garden or the bath-house or the Lamp Hall, and in each one, once, something else looks out: her amber eyes burn gold and her voice drops, warm and patient and much older. Keep it allusive. She offers each the thing they want most:
- **Ysse**: the library at Halcyra, every folio on every lamp, and the answer at the end of them.
- **Quorl**: Hathe's voice. "He isn't gone. She keeps him. Would you like to hear him?"
- **Tick**: Marla at rest. "Your mother stands in rivers. She's so tired, Tibbet."
- **Borrin**: his shadow back, or the three and a half years he lost underground.
- **Varra**: her name restored by the Mothers, said aloud at the council fire.

**Sorrel at supper.** If the party has asked [[Sorrel Vey]] for help (scene 3), she comes to the House supper as their guest, uninvited by anyone else, in black. At some point she says it to Seraphine's face, once, on purpose, across the table: that she is Ilse Marrow's lover. Seraphine smiles and says she's glad the captain has someone. Nobody else at the table will ever forget it.

---

### Scene 3: The Velvet Ledger
[[Mourn]], the Raveler doorkeeper, lets them in, and laughs at the wrong things. [[Sorrel Vey]] trades secrets for secrets. She knows the Saint's methods ("my goddess's arts, done by someone in a hurry"), and for the right secret tells the party what the church really wants: the Ninth Ward's crack to open, so the Saint can drink what leaks. For a price, and to spite her rival, she'll help them against Seraphine.

[[Aelith Sorn]] is on the Ledger's balcony, visiting Sorrel. She has been courting her for a month and makes, tonight, a formal offer for "a transcription". Sorrel refuses, pleasantly.

**Tesh.** Late in the evening the house goes quiet, and then someone upstairs starts calling a name, over and over, in a voice that isn't hers: slow, warm, patient, in the cadence of the Sleeper's voice from the dream. It is [[Tesh]], the courtesan who does not sleep. Sorrel goes up to her and comes down pale. "She does that. Lately she does it every night." Insight DC 15 on Sorrel: she knows something about Tesh she is not saying. (She is the Regency's lost line: imperial blood through a by-blow of Aurel IX's uncle, and the Regent's own claimant in the capital has stopped speaking. Mote knows. See session 7.)

---

### Scene 4: The ink
If the party wins her trust, Sorrel shows them the oldest of her tattoos, under the gown, down her spine. Saelish script, faded and re-inked by later hands; a list of names; a ward-sign. Nobody in the March can read it. (It's the record of the Saelish Trespass that Sael chose to forget; [[Aelith Sorn]] can read one line in five, and would do almost anything to read the rest.)

---

### Scene 5: Sallow exposed, or not
With Spoons's word (or Bray's, or the letter in the small neat hand) and the coin, the party can bring [[Garrick Sallow]] to Marrow. He denies everything, then smiles: "You'd hang an officer on the word of a bookie?" Marrow confines him to quarters. The mutiny goes quiet and underground.

If the party tries to take him without Marrow, Sallow stays behind his door and Bray comes out of the barracks with two friends: **Sallow's loyalists** (see Encounters).

---

### Scene 6: The garden door (night)
> The garden wall is lower at the bottom, where the pears are, and the door in it is open, as it always is. No lock; there has never been a lock. Inside, the pear trees stand very still, and between them, just at the edge of the lamplight, there are more people than there should be: standing, facing the house, not talking. In the Lamp Hall a single voice is praying, and then, under it, answering it, a second.

If the party comes back to the House at night, uninvited: Seraphine at prayer in the Lamp Hall, speaking in two voices, her own and something warmer. On her desk in the next room, a letter to Halcyra, the ink still wet: "The flame's new keeper is a Vesh girl. Bring her to me." If they're caught and refuse to leave politely, run **The Lamp-Wardens** (one, called off at first blood).

---

### How it ends
The party walks home before dawn, warm and rested and certain they shouldn't feel this good. On the hill, Ysse tends the lantern, and the flame leans toward the east, toward Halcyra, like a plant toward a window. Tick the Saint's clock.

== Strong start ==

A small girl in a clean white smock stands at the Keep gate with a letter held in both hands. She has been told to wait for an answer. She has a smudge of honey on her chin and a very serious face, and she is not going anywhere. The letter smells of warm resin and rain.

== Scenes ==

- The perfumed letter arrives by child messenger; the Keep argues; Tallow warns Ysse.
- An evening at the House of Unburnt Light: Seraphine's offer, and each PC's private temptation.
- Sorrel, at the House supper, tells Seraphine to her face that she is Marrow's lover.
- Trading secrets with Sorrel Vey at the Velvet Ledger; Aelith on the balcony; Tesh calls a name in the Sleeper's voice.
- Sorrel shows the oldest tattoos, down her spine.
- Bringing Sallow before Marrow, or fighting Bray for him.
- The garden door at night: the dead among the pears; Seraphine prays in two voices.

== Clues ==

- The church wants the Ninth Ward's crack to open so the Saint can drink the leak.
- Seraphine's letter to Halcyra: "Bring her to me." (Ysse)
- The Saint speaks through Seraphine: her amber eyes burn gold, her voice drops.
- Sorrel Vey's oldest tattoos are a Saelish record with a ward-sign in it.
- Sorrel Vey has been quietly paying the company's small debts; Marrow doesn't know.
- Sorrel and Marrow are lovers, and Seraphine now knows it too.
- Tesh, at the Ledger, calls out at night in the Sleeper's cadence.
- Mote's Coinday report to Caer Ardith: "Report on the lamp. Progress satisfactory."
- The flame leans east, toward Halcyra, after the party visits the House.
