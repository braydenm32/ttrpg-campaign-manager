## Session 2: The Assessor
*Level 2. Hearthday, 15 Ashfall 3011 AW, the morning after the guttering, into the night at Fort Mercy. Rent day in town.*

- *Sky*: Pallas full; the Ember a night from full. At Fort Mercy tonight both are up at once, one white and one red, and the glass throws two shadows of everything.
- *Weather*: a greywind building from the east all afternoon. Roll on [[Greywind Weather]] once the party leaves the Ash Road.
- *Touch these*: Vosk lends Jory and Jessom Dunmore for the ride, "who argue the whole way". Sallow volunteers to help the Assessor settle in, so he can stand near Mote. Odile gives Pell a room above the cooper's and a very long look. Spoons offers eight to one the wagon is never found, and tells the party the odds have changed when they come back alive. At Fort Mercy, Mardle Ock stares at the space behind Borrin and hums three notes until it stops moving. Old Halloran Pike, as the party leaves: "If Vexa shows you a chart, the Choir's real. The rest is her crews showing off." Ysse, asleep at the fort, hears the warm voice: *Closer.*
- *Level-up*: none. The party stays level 2 through session 3.
- *If the party…* won't go east, Marrow sends Vosk and ten soldiers instead. They come back on the 17th with the box, two wounded and Vexa's grudge. The party gets the bill but not the chart, the reading or Skelt's story.

**What really happened to the pay (GM).** The Regency has paid nothing since 1 Hollowfrost 3009. In Ashfall Tollamund advanced 600 gold in new Sevenhaven coin in the company's name, under the third line of Marrow's note, and sent it under an old Regency pay seal. The wagon left Fallowden on 2 Ashfall; its Concord-hired escort was paid off two days west of Cinderford on the 4th; her drivers brought it into the mill-yard at the Ford on the night of the 6th; at dawn on the 7th it crossed the bridge as a sealed House cart and turned off at the Burned Mill for Fort Mercy, where Cousin Pennamund had Vexa hold it 'until collected'. The note now stands at 4,800 gold.

**Goal.** Find the missing pay wagon, and learn that the money in it was never the Regency's.

### Running this session
**Last time** (read aloud, or have a player tell it). *"Yesterday was Cinders: nine years to the day. The pay notice said nothing, and meant it. At noon a deserter called Dannic Rook climbed out of the ford, dead a week, and told you it was cold under the glass. You followed him down into the Glass Barrow and came back with a tin of lamp oil cut with ash. Towards midnight, in front of the whole company, the lantern went out for the length of one breath. Brother Tallow says someone has been feeding his flame ash."*

**Where the PCs are.** Asleep in the Keep, if they slept at all, after Marrow kept them close for the night. The hammering at the gate wakes them at first light.

**The party should have.** Their wick-lamps (burning small and steady again), [[The Cut Oil Tin]] or Tallow's word that he has it, [[Dannic's Note]], the PAID IN FULL pay-chit, Dannic's twelve new Regency silver, and [[Who Is Poisoning the Flame?]] from Tallow. Perhaps Hesk's order-book and Kettle's job.

**If they missed something.**
- *Never reached the Cold Room?* The Cold Captain still keeps Dannic's stash, and the party can go back for it any night before session 4. Tallow, pouring the dawn measure this morning, finds a cut tin of his own on the oil-store shelf where a clean one should be, so the poisoning quest starts anyway. Pell sees the pay-chit later, when they bring it.
- *Never heard Kettle's "turned off by the mill"?* Mistress Hebbe's toll-log at the gatehouse has the sealed cart crossing east at dawn on the 7th (scene 2), and Nance Oddy at the Burned Mill, six miles out, saw it take her fork.
- *Spent the night chasing Hesk?* Hesk's order-book still has the tin "for the Assessor's Office, paid in advance". It lands harder after scene 1 here, when the party has met the clerk with the cold.

**The session at a glance.** Four scenes and a long ride. Scene 1 at the Keep gate at dawn; scene 2 is the morning's questions and the afternoon on the road; scene 3 is Fort Mercy at dusk; scene 4 is the cellar at night. The session ends with the party asleep at Fort Mercy, and session 3 starts there a few hours later, so don't bring them home tonight.

### Scene 1: The Assessor at the gate (dawn)
> Dawn. Hammering at the Keep gate. A thin, stooped man in a black coat gone green at the seams is nailing a notice to the door, saying "Sorry, so sorry" to the wood with every blow. A smaller man in spectacles and a scarf holds the nails and passes them up one at a time, sniffing. The notice is in triplicate. It demands three thousand, eight hundred and eighty-two silver from a company that was paid nothing yesterday.

This is [[Pell Quillon]], Fourth Assistant Under-Collector for the March of Cinders, who came in at first light on a mule with a writing-desk strapped to it. He has never seen the town before this morning. [[Ansel Mote]], a month in town, holds the nails: he reopened the Assessor's Office in Reaping, with Widow Pruitt, its only clerk since 3009, and has been "getting it ready". The notice is the [[Tax Notice, in Triplicate]]: 3,882 silver, 3,240 of it for one lamp, burning nine years, at a silver a night.

Marrow, very calmly, asks the party to get the Assessor out of her sight before she does something the Regency would have a form for. It is also rent day in town, Pallas full; the Reeve's collectors are working the followers' lines, and Odile has given the Assessor a room and a long look.

**Who's here.**
- **Pell**: earnest, kind and hopeless. He has lost Form 9-K, the one form that lets the company dispute the bill, and is near tears ([[The Assessor's Lost Form]]). He apologises to the gate once more on his way out, and to the mule.
- **Mote**: mild, helpful, forgettable on purpose. "I copy whatever I'm told to copy." He has a cold.
- [[Garrick Sallow]] arrives to offer the Assessor "every help the company can give". He stands very close to Mote.

**Checks.**
- Anyone who saw the wanted poster can compare it with Mote's signature as witness on the tax notice (Investigation DC 13): the same small neat hand.
- If they challenge Mote about the poster, he is puzzled and helpful. "I signed it for the Office, as clerks do. I wrote down the description I was given. If it is wrong I will have it taken down." He does, by noon. Widow Pruitt, asked, confirms the Office paid the printer on the 13th on his word, and adds, pointedly, that nobody told her who gave the description. Nothing ties him to the river, he is a Regency officer, and Marrow tells the party straight that she cannot lock up a clerk over a bad poster. If the PAID IN FULL chit is shown with Mote at Pell's elbow, he looks at the seal one second longer than a clerk should, and says nothing.
- Insight DC 15 on Mote: he's not nervous at all, and he should be.
- If the party shows Pell the PAID IN FULL pay-chit from the Barrow, he goes the colour of his coat. "That isn't a Treasury seal. That is somebody's *private* seal, and nobody should have a private seal on a pay-chit." He asks to keep it. He will start asking the Treasury questions by post, and by session 5 he will have found where the Ninth's wages went.

**Development.** Once Pell is off Marrow's doorstep, she asks the question everyone is thinking: where is the wagon? If the party remembers Kettle's "ask me tomorrow", they go to the Low Quay. If they don't, Fennick sends them to Mistress Hebbe's toll-log at the gatehouse. Either way, by mid-morning they know a sealed cart went east on the 7th, and the greywind is building. Go to scene 2. If they stay to help Pell hunt for Form 9-K, give it a few minutes of comedy among the crates, then move on: it is not here ([[The Assessor's Lost Form]]). If they'd rather tail Mote, he spends the morning copying at his desk, and nothing he does is worth watching, which is the point.

**If the party calls at the mill.** Tollamund & Daughters is in the old mill by the bridge, a hundred yards from the gatehouse, and Kettle has just named the House's cart. Odessa receives them at the counter, courteous and useless: the House sends glass-carts east every week, and keeps no record it can show without the Principals' leave. The Grandmother does not look up. Fennel the scribe can be bribed in lamp-oil for a look at the day-book, which confirms only that a sealed cart left the yard at dawn on the 7th. It costs an hour, and the House now knows the party is asking.

**Treasure.** None. If the party shows Pell the PAID IN FULL chit, he asks to keep it, and it is worth letting him.

### Scene 2: The road east (morning to afternoon)
[[Kettle Brindlewake]] tells them, whether or not they took her job: "Told you to ask me tomorrow. A sealed House cart crossed the bridge at dawn on the 7th, going east. Not a pay wagon. Tollamund's glass-cart, with her drivers on it." If the party goes to the gatehouse instead, [[Mistress Hebbe]] the toll clerk will show them her toll-log on the Captain's word, or for a silver and a promise not to say where it came from: *7 Ashfall, dawn. House cart, sealed, east. 5 cp.* She remembers the coins: new Sevenhaven silver with Tollamund's house-mark, when the House's drivers always pay in Regency copper to be polite.

The cart turned off the Ash Road at the Burned Mill, six miles east, onto the glassland track toward [[Fort Mercy]]. Vosk lends horses from the Keep stable, and Jory and Jessom Dunmore, crossbows, who ride along and argue the whole way about which of them is the better shot. Tracking runs from the mill over the sixteen miles of glassland track: the best tracker rolls Survival DC 12 once an hour, and others may Help. Three successes before three failures puts them at Fort Mercy at dusk; see Development for the rest.

**On the way.** At the mill, [[Nance Oddy]] keeps a Toll of everything that passes ([[The Burned Mill]]): the sealed cart went south at her fork on the morning of the 7th, heavy, and came back on the 8th light, where four Glassers stripped it and burned it. Seven miles short of the fort, the smoke of [[Tally's Cut]] is a short ride off the track (an hour; they reach the fort after dark): [[Ferrow Gant]] saw the cart cross the glass at dusk on the 7th, and will tell a soldier who buys him a drink that Skelt missed Tally's dawn on the 3rd and came in on the 5th with rope-burns. [[Old Tally's Coin]] starts there.

**Development.** The road decides when they arrive. Three tracking successes before three failures: they reach Fort Mercy at dusk, rested enough (scene 3 as written). Three failures, or the detour to Tally's Cut: they arrive after dark, with a level of exhaustion, and the Glassers' fires are already lit, so Vexa is less patient (Persuasion DCs in scene 3 go up by 2). If the party wants to turn back once they see the fort, the Dunmore twins point out, fairly, that the box is the company's pay and they are not going home without it.

**Treasure.** Nance Oddy will sell a night's water and a dry corner of the mill for a copper each. Mistress Hebbe's silence costs a silver, if they bought it.

### Scene 3: Fort Mercy (dusk)
> The fort stands on the only rise for ten miles, a square of blackened Regency stone with one tower still roofed and the others open to the sky, and around it, like a skirt, the Glassers' town: tents, lean-tos, carts, a windmill made of shield-boards, and dig-pits going down into the glass in every direction, each with its windlass and its rope and its bucket. Smoke from a dozen fires. A dog barks. From the tower a woman's voice, carrying: "If that's the company, tell them the toll's doubled and I still don't want them."

An abandoned Regency fort that burned in 3005, now a Glassers' den. [[Vexa Glassjaw]] runs it with a shovel she calls the Bishop. She has the strongbox, unopened, in the cellar behind the loose corner of the Lithic panel, because "a box nobody comes looking for is a box with teeth". A Shellback told her, very slowly, to hold it until collected. She has not opened it.

**Ways to the box.**
- **Talk.** Persuasion DC 13 gets her to admit she has it. DC 15, or anything she actually wants, gets it handed over: a reading of her cellar wall (scene 4), the party's word that they'll tell the Concord factor she was robbed by soldiers, or a promise she can call in later.
- **Sneak.** The yard never sleeps. Stealth DC 15 to reach the cellar; the panel's loose corner needs Investigation DC 12 to find.
- **Fight.** Run **Glassers at Fort Mercy** (see Encounters). Four toughs, then Vexa after round three. The rest of the yard makes a ring and bets; it joins only if the party breaks the ring's rules (see Encounters). She stops it before anyone dies, if she can.

**Who else is here.**
- [[Skelt]], a Glasser who drinks. For a bottle (or to save his skin) he'll admit he guided Dannic Rook into the Glass Barrow on 3 Ashfall by the culvert, held the rope while Dannic stashed something in the cold room, and came out alone the next morning. He knows the spiders and the Red Seam door. He sells dig-charts at Gutter Market, "usually false". He's very sorry. He's always very sorry.
- [[Ottoline Crane]], Vexa's second, a dowser who finds metal under glass by walking barefoot and bleeding. She goes very still when anyone reads the cellar wall aloud.
- [[Mardle Ock]] "Two-Winters", a Returned dwarf who says he will not go below the cellar. He looks at Borrin, then at the space behind Borrin, and hums three low notes until the space stops moving.
- Glassers sorting salvage on blankets in the yard: roll on [[Gutter Market Salvage]] for what's for sale.

**Development.** However they get to it, the box is in the cellar, and the cellar is where the night goes: go to scene 4. If they talked their way in, Vexa takes them down herself with a lamp and the Bishop. If they sneaked, they reach the panel with the yard still awake above them. If they fought, Vexa calls it off as soon as anyone offers to read the wall, and the scene turns into a bargain with a bleeding Glasser. If the party leaves without the box, Marrow sends Vosk and ten soldiers for it on the 16th, as in "If the party…" above: they bring it home on the 17th, with two wounded and Vexa's grudge.

**Treasure.** Skelt's story costs a bottle (a silver at the fort's stall). Gutter Market salvage, at Glasser prices.

### Scene 4: The cellar and the strongbox (night)
**The strongbox.** Iron-bound, under an old Regency pay seal. Inside: 600 gold in new Sevenhaven coin, every piece stamped with the house-mark of [[Tollamund & Daughters]], and a loan-chit ([[The Concord Loan-Chit]]): the Concord's copy of an advance under the third line of Marrow's note, naming the Ninth Lantern debtor "for the full sum, at interest", collateral as in the note. The company owes money it never received.

**Vexa's writing.** On the cellar's far wall is a panel of grey Lithic stone, glyphs on every inch. Behind it the Glassers broke into a black-glass chamber thirty feet down, every wall carved; the Cold Seam drops from the chamber, ninety feet of rope and then ladders. Reading it: Ysse (Arcana DC 13), Borrin (DC 10 as a dwarf), or anyone with the Lithic tongue. It repeats the Barrow's lintel, *WHAT WE BURIED IS NOT AT REST*, and the longer passage below ends: *The lamp holds the lock; the heart holds the lamp.* The lines between have been chipped away by the Glassers' picks: a reader can make out only that they speak of a lid and a lock, and no more. Give the players the first line and the last, nothing else.

**Vexa's trades.** For the reading, or for 20 gp, Vexa trades [[A Glasser's Chart]] of the way down to the Ninth Stone, signed "-P". (An old soldier drew it for her a few winters back, from what he'd seen at the lip and what her crews told him. She won't say who; she promised.) As a second chip she offers [[The Melted Crown]], a slumped gold circlet with glass in its tines, for 50 gp or a favour: "It counts in the night. I'd like it to count somewhere else."

**The Cold Seam (optional).** Behind the panel, the windlass, and the tin plate on the post: BRAKK, 19 REAPING, in six languages. A voice at the bottom asks for the rope, in Brakk's voice. Run **Brakk in the Cold Seam** only if the party goes down. Below sixty feet the cold is extreme (Con save DC 10 each hour or a level of exhaustion). It is a conversation first: the thing answers Borrin in his own voice and wants to know what he brought up with him. Going down here now gives the party a second road to the Ninth Stone in session 6.

**Optional: Kettle's Cargo.** Kettle wants her barge under the bridge "the first night the Lieutenant's patrol is looking the other way". With the party at Fort Mercy tonight, that night comes after session 3: any night from the 17th.

**Development.** The box is open, the chit is read, the wall is read or not, and it is too late and too dark to ride. Vexa gives them the corner of the yard by the windmill and a fire, and tells them to sleep in their boots. Go to "How it ends". If the party insists on riding home tonight, let them try: it is twenty-two miles over the glass in a greywind, at night, under two moons. Run one roll on [[Ashenreach Encounters]] and have the dream (session 3, scene 1) catch them on the road instead of in the yard.

**Treasure.** The strongbox: **600 gold** in new Sevenhaven coin, every piece house-marked, and [[The Concord Loan-Chit]]. [[A Glasser's Chart]] if they traded for it, and [[The Melted Crown]] if they paid Vexa's price. What to do with the 600 gold is session 3's argument.

### How it ends
The party beds down in Fort Mercy's yard under two moons, the box at their feet and the chit in someone's coat, while the greywind hisses over the glass. In the small hours, everyone in the fort wakes at once. That is where session 3 begins: the dream, the ride home, and Fennick counting the coin. (If you want session 2 to end at home, play session 3's first two scenes now and open session 3 at dusk.)

**Clocks.** Debt called in ticks when the loan-chit is read and the party learns what the company owes. Nothing else moves tonight. See [[The Clocks]].
