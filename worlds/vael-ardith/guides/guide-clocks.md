Seven clocks run under this campaign. Each lives on its own page (the faction's, or the Sleeper's), where **Play → Clocks** reads it; this page is the GM's crib for moving them between sessions. Tick in the open when the players can see cause and effect; tick in the dark when a faction moves while nobody is looking. A clock that fills is not a failure: it is a scene arriving. Each session's "How it ends" closes with a one-line tally of where every clock stands after it on the default path; add or take away what your table actually did. The night of the full Ember in session 3 is also the night of the second guttering: it ticks each clock below that names it once, not twice.

### Mutiny (6, starts at 2)
The company's patience. The players can see this one.
- **Ticks:** the lantern gutters at the end of S1 (to 3; the second guttering in S3 does not tick it); pay fails again; rations drop to half (Brannoc's stew is the barometer); a popular soldier dies pointlessly; Sallow wins an argument in the yard; the Concord at the gate (S7).
- **Rolls back:** someone feeds the company (a cartload, a hunt, Mother Brisk's kettle) −1; the pay coin is spent on wages −1; Marrow's savings come out in the open −2; Sallow exposed with proof −1 (once: the coin trail laid out before the company, usually S7 "Expose the coin", or at the S4 muster if the party does it there; confining him moves nothing).
- **At 6:** Sallow's mutiny breaks out openly, S7 at the latest. What marches in S8: clock 0 to 2, about 120; 3 to 4, about 100; 5 to 6, about 60, with Sallow's diehards on the road behind them.

### The Quiet Office closes in (6, starts at 1)
Vell's hand, ticking when nobody looks.
- **Ticks:** the lantern gutters, because Mote reports it (end of S1, to 2; S3, to 3); the pay wagon stays lost past S2; Sallow gets his way (he is not confined in S4); Hennet's slate-book burns (S5); the party ignores Mote; the back of Form 9-K is read and Mote learns it.
- **Rolls back:** an agent is caught or named with proof; a paper trail is burned; Nib's black ledger reaches the Captain; Pell files his Claim of Arrears (he does, in the days before S6).
- **At 6:** the Office closes the account on whoever is in the way. Session 7 reads this clock on Mote's night: full, a third hired knife comes over the wall with him and the other two. Short of full, Mote's night runs as written.

### Debt called in (8, starts at 2)
Tollamund's ledger. The players can see this one too.
- **Ticks:** the quarter's interest unpaid on 14 Ashfall (already counted); the loan-chit is read and the party learns the debt is 4,800 (S2, to 3); the Reeve signs the charter (in the days before S5, to 4); grace ends at sunset on 4 Leafrot (before S7, to 5); a soldier's pay-chit is pledged at the mill; the strongbox coin is spent on anything but the debt; the silver in the thirteenth barrel is traced to the House with proof ([[The Unmarked Barrel]]), and the House notices it has been seen and moves its calendar up.
- **Rolls back:** 480 gold paid on the interest −1; the Treaty's page seven found and read aloud −1; Pennamund turns against the Grandmother −1; the Reeve tears up the charter −1; Mother Hennet's certified slate-book brought to the Keep gate (S7) −1.
- **At 7 or 8:** Tollamund comes to the gate on 5 Leafrot (S7) with lawyers, the Reeve and the clause whatever the clock says; the clock sets how ready she is. Session 7 reads it before the argument: at 7 or 8 her lawyers have an answer for everything but page seven, and the roll is DC 18 instead of 15. Page seven read aloud and argued well still wins with no roll.

### The Saint ascends (8, starts at 3)
Ilyra's hunger, through Seraphine's warmth.
- **Ticks:** the lantern gutters at the end of S1 (to 4); the night of the full Ember and the second guttering (S3, once, to 5); in S4, the party says yes to "saving" the flame, anyone is healed or says yes at the House (one tick between them), Ysse accepts the House's invitation, and a fight with the Lamp-Wardens or a PC striking Seraphine, one tick each; a PC strikes Seraphine in S7; the House feeds the town or the company, or a soldier is "saved" (in the dark, between sessions); the gauntlet's price in S8, if you choose it.
- **Rolls back:** the Saint's hunger exposed in front of witnesses −1 (Seraphine shamed in front of her faithful in S7 counts); the party refuses the House in public −1 (Ysse refusing Seraphine in front of the crowd in S7 counts); the lantern given into someone else's keeping, so the Church loses its chance −2.
- **At the lip (S8):** the clock sets how hard Seraphine fights. 5 or under: she asks. 6 or 7: she takes. 8: she takes, and only Ysse refusing her by name three times talks her back.
- **At 8:** Seraphine takes at the lip, as above. The clock does not decide the ending: Ilyra ascends within the year only if the ward is let crack, and a resealed ward stalls her whatever the clock says.

### Last words decoded (4, starts at 1)
Corvane's slow certainty.
- **Ticks:** Dannic's last words reach the Chapel (S1, to 2); the Barrow congregation's words are taken by rite (S1, to 3); Pike sits up and says it, and the ledger is read with the party (S5, to full).
- **Rolls back:** never. The dead do not unsay things.
- **At 4 (S5):** Corvane is sure of "the lamp is the lock" and of her wrong conclusion. From here she is a friend who may try to snuff the flame to let the dead rest, and the Rook-Court's shovels and dead-roads go to whichever side she chooses.

### The Sleepless rise (6, starts at 2)
The Widow's congregation.
- **Ticks:** the lantern gutters at the end of S1 (to 3); the night of the full Ember and the second guttering (S3, once, to 4); the full Ember at the lip (S8, as it tops the sky in scene 4); a new convert, especially a soldier; the Hollowborn PC engages with the dream in public (Tick sitting with them at midnight in S3 counts); a Sleepless hurt breaking the vigil (S3); the Orchard sap is drunk at a meeting; Ambry Coyle's nail touches the flame (S7).
- **Rolls back:** the Widow reached as a person, through Ottilie or Tick −1; Gammer Teal speaks to the Smokes −1; the company stops a sleepwalk without blood −1.
- **At 6:** the Sleepless march in force on the Rent, sixty adults and their children, to watch the lamp go out. They arrive on the lip in S8 whatever the clock says; at full they arrive first, and are sitting across the top of the rope steps (session 8, scene 3, says how the party gets past them). If the full Ember fills it at the lip, it counts toward the Lip clock (session 8, scene 4).

### The Sleeper stirs (12, starts at 3)
The world clock. One segment for each failing ward: the Fourth weak, the Eighth breached, the Ninth cracked.
- **Ticks:** +1 at each full Ember (16 Ashfall, S3; 9 Leafrot, S8), and nothing else unless you speed it up. Spells in the Rent or within a mile of the Ninth Stone always frost the nearest wall. The Barrow and the Cold Seam frost and feed the Cold Captain but never tick this clock.
- **Speeding it up (your choice, one at a time):** +1 if the lantern goes fully out for more than a breath; a d6 for each spell near the Stone or in the Rent, ticking on a 1 (or 1 to 2 for a fast clock); +1 for battle sorcery near the Stone; +1 when a PC answers the Choir or drinks the Orchard's sap at full Ember; +1 for a finished Sleepless rite; +1 each time the flame speaks through its holder. Full list on [[The Sleeper Below]].
- **Rolls back:** Reseal the Ward −3. Nothing else.
- **Thresholds:** **6**, the dream reaches the whole March of Cinders and the Cinders dead get up out of the ash and walk to the Stone (in S8 only if you sped the clock up); **9**, the Ashenreach dead rise at a red moon unaided; **12**, it speaks through whoever holds the flame. 9 and 12 belong to the Let It Crack and Use It aftermaths. Left alone the clock reaches 5 by S8, so the dead of Cinders stay down; with one or two accelerators it reaches 6 and they stand for the finale. The still figures that line the lip in S6 and S8 are not these: they are the war's dead, drawn there a few at each red moon over nine years. They do not move while watched, and they only turn round in Let It Crack.

### The Lip (6, session 8 only)
A short clock for the finale, kept on scrap paper rather than a page: how long the people holding the Rent's lip can keep everyone else off the rope steps while the party is below. Session 8, scene 4 ("Holding the lip") sets its starting ticks, what ticks it and what buys ticks back. If it fills, whoever still wants the lamp comes down behind the party.

### Moving them between sessions
Before each session, read the previous session's "How it ends" and its clock tally, and tick what the players caused in the open. Then move one clock in the dark, the faction that lost most by the players' choices, and let its consequence arrive at the Keep gate as the strong start. Keep the Sleeper for red moons and the Stone; it is the only clock the players should never see ticked.
