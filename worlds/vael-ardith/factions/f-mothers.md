The Mothers of the Iron are the orcs' mothers, aunts and grandmothers, and they decide who fights, for whom, and for how long. They license every orc company on the continent. A Mother's writ is a contract, and a contract is a promise made in blood. Break it, and you are exiled: no kin, no fire, no name.

### Public face
The people who keep the peace by being too expensive to fight. The Kingslayers are hated and feared, and the Mothers prefer it that way. They are practical, fierce, and unfailingly honest in the ways that matter. They do not discuss the Kingslaying.

### Structure and ranks
**Mother of Mothers** at the top, chosen by the others for life. Beneath her, **Mothers** who each hold a **hearth**: a clan, a region, a set of contracts. **Daughters of the Writ** are their envoys. **Fists** are the officers who carry orders to the companies. Males serve as soldiers and sergeants, and the system works because everyone knows exactly who answers to whom.

### Notable members
- [[Grashka]]: Mother of Mothers, ancient, iron-hard, grandmother of [[Varra Nine-Knuckles]].
- [[Sergeant Vosk]]: Second Blade of the Ninth; old soldiers say he was once of the Iron Guard.

### Others you will meet
- [[Skarra]]: the Ninth's orc sapper, who writes home to her mother every month and does not say what about.

### What they want in the Ashenreach
A garrison that holds, a contract that is honoured, and no one poking at the Ninth Ward. They will tolerate the Ninth Lantern for as long as it keeps its word. If it does not, they will say so once, plainly.

### How they treat the party
Orcs get a measuring look and a short sentence. Everyone else gets courtesy and a precise sense of what is not being said. Varra, an exile, gets nothing from them but cold, and that is its own kind of message.

:::for Varra Nine-Knuckles
Your grandmother Grashka struck the blow that killed the Emperor. Almost nobody outside the Mothers' council knows you are her granddaughter, and Vosk has said nothing. When a Daughter of the Writ passes you in silence, it is because you are an exile; they would be no warmer if they knew the rest.
:::

### Resources
Soldiers, discipline, the writs that bind the companies, and a network of aunts that carries news faster than a courier.

### How they act
- **A Fist arrives:** a stern officer with an inspection and a short message.
- **A writ is checked:** the company's licence is read aloud in front of the officers.
- **A message to Varra:** or about her. Never from her grandmother.
- **A quiet visit:** to the Treaty Stone, at night, by orcs who do not wish to be seen.

:::gm
Secret aim: keep the wards shut. They ordered the Kingslaying because Aurel IX was digging at three ward sites to open one, and nobody would believe them. They still hold that line. Grashka suspects the Ninth Ward is the next one in danger, and the Mothers will move to close the crack by force, or to destroy whatever opens it. They have an understanding with the [[The Last Wardens|Last Wardens]]; they do not tell the company. [[Mother Hesha]] of Grashka's council reads Skarra's monthly letters about Varra and carries the council's answer back.
:::

== GM notes ==

Orc speech: short, concrete, no flourish. Humour is dry and often at the speaker's own expense. The Mothers never lie, which makes them difficult to read; they simply decline to answer. This is the faction where a PC's backstory (Varra) is most in play. Do not make Grashka a villain: she committed regicide to prevent catastrophe, and she would do it again.
