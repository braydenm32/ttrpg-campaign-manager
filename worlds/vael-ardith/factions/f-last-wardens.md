The Lithic are the ward-builders' last descendants, and the Last Wardens are the few who still tend what their ancestors made. Their skin turns to stone as they age. The oldest stop moving altogether and stand at their wards as living statues. The rest walk slowly between them, carrying messages, tools and the weight of knowing what is at stake.

### Public face
Pilgrims, scholars and the faithful visit the Wardens to ask blessings or answers. The Wardens give neither freely. They are patient, courteous, tired professionals, and slow: a conversation takes an afternoon, and the answer is usually "I will think on it."

### Structure and ranks
No hierarchy that outsiders can see. **Standers** are the ones who have stopped moving and hold a ward. **Walkers** are the ones who still can, and who carry word between Standers. **Keepers** are a Walker bound to a single ward, like [[Warden Uthrem]] at the Ninth Stone. The youngest have almost no stone on them, and the oldest have none left to give.

### Notable members
- [[Warden Uthrem]]: half-stone, at the Ninth Stone.

### What they want in the Ashenreach
To keep the Ninth Ward standing. The old stone is failing, the crack widens, and the Warden at the Ninth Stone is more rock than man. They are running out of time, and they do not say what they hope will save it.

### How they treat the party
Gently, and with a courtesy that is never quite warmth. A Warden who likes a PC will say so slowly, over a long afternoon, and then be silent for a week. They are far more afraid than they appear.

### Resources
Knowledge of the wards that the world forgot; the Ninth Stone itself; a few allies like the Mothers; and time, which is running out.

### How they act
- **A summons:** a Walker arrives at the Keep with a request that cannot wait.
- **A warning:** the ground near the Rent hums, or a ward-song reaches the PCs in sleep.
- **A gift:** old tools, a map, a name nobody remembers.
- **A request:** to do something that sounds small and is not.

:::gm
Secret aim: find a living heart for the Ninth Ward before they turn fully to stone. The ward needs a willing mind to anchor it: Uthrem, a PC, or Ysse, whom the flame calls. They know the Godslaying was a mistake. They do not say the Nine's names, because the names were struck from memory. They will accept a willing sacrifice, and they will not force one, but they will encourage a good one. Uthrem has stood at the Ninth Stone forty years and waited nine since the crack, hardly able to move; he has been waiting for [[Ysse Lampwright]] by name since before she arrived.

The tenth signature on the Treaty Stone is theirs, and they signed page seven of the Treaty with the Mothers: the keeping of the Ninth Ward, which makes the lantern a Treaty fixture "kept lit by those who do not know why".
:::

== GM notes ==

Play slowness as a gift: let the table sit in silence, then deliver one line that matters. The Wardens are not mystical oracles; they are tired professionals. Their urgency should only become visible late. A Warden who says "I do not know" is telling the truth.
