The Barge Clans are halfling families who live on the water. Their barges carry cargo, letters, contraband and gossip up and down every river of [[Vael Ardith]]. They are neutral by profession and nosy by nature, and everyone who matters has used them at least once.

### Public face
Good-natured river people, quick with a joke, slow to take offence. They carry anything, for a fair price, to anywhere with a quay. Every barge has a name, a painted eye on the prow, and a very good reason for being where it is. At the Low Quay they tie up along a post-stair, a flight of mooring posts cut down the bank at stepped heights, so a barge can lie to whichever post the river leaves her; every painted eye faces the town, and very little ashore goes unwatched.

### Structure and ranks
Each clan is led by a **Barge-Mother** or **Barge-Father**. Boats are commanded by **skippers**; crews are family. The clans meet in a **Quay-Moot** at every major port, and a Moot's decision binds all. The Mother of the Water of the Reedmarsh flotilla, [[Barge-Mother Posy Reedmarsh]], is the eldest on the Cinder and Kettle's superior; the Fairlock Water holds the Ardis, and its senior Mother, [[Barge-Mother Thimble Fairlock]], convenes the Quay-Moot at Sevenhaven each Turning. The Reedmarsh Water holds the first of four copies of the Charter of the Open River. Younger halflings start as **bargelings**, run errands, and learn who to trust.

### Notable members
- [[Kettle Brindlewake]]: halfling barge-captain and smuggler at [[The Low Quay]], gossip, profit-seeker, fond of the PCs.
- [[Barge-Mother Posy Reedmarsh]]: the eldest Mother of the Water on the Cinder, at Sedgemere.

### Others you will meet
- Dob and Marigold: Kettle's cousins and crew on the *Grey Dumpling*, who haul, scrub and say "Ah, Kettle's not in" in two different voices.
- Regent: the barge's cat, named for the Regent-Chancellor, and treated with about as much respect.
- [[Nan Crupp]]: not a clan halfling, but she sells what the river tells her to Kettle by the pennyweight.

### What they want in the Ashenreach
Trade routes that stay open, tolls that stay low, and a steady supply of customers. They want the Ninth Lantern to stay because a garrison keeps the road open. They also want to hear everything first.

### How they treat the party
As friends, so long as friendship is profitable. A PC who tells a good story gets a discount. A PC who pays in gossip gets a better one.

### Resources
Boats, contacts, smuggling routes, a network of family ties from the coast to the mountains, and the best information on the continent.

### How they act
- **A rumour:** passed along as a favour, and worth exactly what you pay.
- **A delivery:** a letter, a crate or a person arrives by barge, unannounced.
- **A deal:** a crate "fell off" a barge and is available at a discount.
- **A tip-off:** Kettle warns the party about something, for a price.

:::gm
Secret aim: run the continent's best courier and intelligence network, and sell to all sides. The clans keep a map of every factor's price and every officer's weakness. Kettle reads [[Ansel Mote]]'s letters and knows he is no clerk. She also holds four casks of the company's own good oil (bought off [[Fennick Bottlebrook]] for bread) and a warm crate, Ketterin's stone hand, in the false floor of the *Grey Dumpling*. She has not decided whether to sell any of it, because she likes the PCs, and liking customers is a bad business habit. Posy takes a tithe from the Concord's salt-tows (one coin in thirteen, "the barrel's share") to let the unmarked barrel through the Weir at Sedgemere: see [[The Unmarked Barrel]].
:::

== GM notes ==

Play the clans as cheerful, knowing, and a little too well-informed. Kettle's voice is warm and quick; she asks questions and answers none. They never betray a client's confidence unless the price is right, and they never state the price out loud.
