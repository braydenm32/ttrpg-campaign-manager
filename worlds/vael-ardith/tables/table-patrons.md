Roll once to see who is at the bar, twice for the crowded evenings. Each entry has a want and a line. Soldier names are rolled for you where a soldier is present.

== GM notes ==

Pick one patron to be the night's hook. Reuse patrons across sessions and let them develop opinions about the PCs. The "one-eyed fiddler" and the "silent man" are good for foreshadowing.

== Entries ==

- 2x {Soldier Names}, off duty and over the limit. Wants a fight, any fight, with anyone.
- 2x A Concord tallyman, polite, sober, with a ledger. Wants to know who is in debt and who is not.
- 2x A Raveler undertaker with a very good joke about a corpse. Wants to hear the party's last words (as a precaution).
- A Glasser with a green-stained coat, drinking alone. Wants a buyer for something heavy in her pack.
- An old woman knitting at the corner table. Wants a seat near the fire and no conversation.
- A Vesh scholar with a lamp on a pole, reading in the dark. Wants a quiet table and a candle that does not flicker.
- A halfling barge-captain buying drinks for everyone she does not know. Wants to be told what you just heard.
- 2x A Hollowborn girl with two different eyes, sitting by the door. Wants to leave but cannot decide when.
- A priest of the Unburnt Saint, smiling, sipping water. Wants to hear someone's troubles.
- An orc veteran in a faded tabard, arm-wrestling anyone who asks. Wants to be beaten, once.
- 2x A dwarf who throws no shadow, sitting in the corner. Wants someone to ask where he has been.
- The Candle's pot-girl, carrying a tray of mismatched mugs. Wants someone to take her away, or at least out for a walk.
- A Regency clerk drunk on his first holiday in a decade. Wants to confess to something. Will confess to anything.
- 2x A one-eyed fiddler who plays one tune and charges for each repetition. Wants a duet partner.
- 2x A silent man in a grey coat, drinking slowly. Wants nothing. His cup is always full.
- A courier from the capital, travel-stained, asleep on the bench with her boots on. Wants a bed and a dream-free night.
- A pilgrim in a hooded cloak, mourning a lost saint. Wants to visit the Treaty Stone and has been told the patrol has closed the road since the bell rang itself.
- Two sappers arguing about a bet involving a grumbler and a goat. Wants a judge.
- A grieving mother in widow's black, buying a round for the dead. Wants someone to say the names with her.
- A Shellback clerk, slow and patient, eating a single dumpling over an hour. Wants the party to wait.
- {Soldier Names}, arguing with a Raveler about the price of a funeral. Wants a discount.
- A noble-born runaway pretending to be a farmhand. Wants a way east. Is badly disguised.
