type: table
name: Last Candle Rumours
summary: "Gossip heard at The Last Candle: some true, some false, a few both. The truth key is in the GM notes."
tags: [ random, rumours ]
---

Roll when someone buys a round, listens at the bar, or pays a storyteller. Give the rumour as the speaker would: half-heard, exaggerated, and with an opinion attached. Do not say which are true.

Timing is marked on the entries. An entry that starts with a note in brackets waits until then; reroll it if it comes up early. Every other entry is safe from session 1.

== GM notes ==

Truth key, by opening words. TRUE: "The Reeve has been seen"; "Dannic Rook was not the first"; "The Saint's priests are buying" (candle-stubs carry what was said near them, and the Church listens); "Old Tallow's eyes"; "A tin of oil"; "The pay wagon had Concord coin"; "Spoons keeps a book"; "Madame Vey's seen the captain"; "The Assessor's clerk"; "There's a dwarf in the Smokes" (Tam Hollowell). FALSE: "The captain has sold"; "The lantern is hollow"; "Kingslayers have been seen"; "The Concord has hired"; "Hathe is alive"; "The Treaty Stone bled"; "Lieutenant Sallow's sword". PARTLY TRUE: "Fort Mercy's lights" (the fires are lit as ever, as the party sees in session 2; the Glassers come and go by their own track, off the company's road, and the "something else" is the half-ring of lamps Vexa keeps burning in the cellar); "The Sleepless don't sleep"; "The Velvet Ledger keeps"; "There's a ninth"; "The Ember's closer" (it looks bigger low at rising, and has not grown; it will if the crack opens).

Timing (also marked on the entries). Wait until after the ford: "Dannic Rook was not the first"; "A tin of oil". Wait until after session 1: "The Assessor's clerk", so the players have a chance to forget him. Wait until after session 2: "The pay wagon had Concord coin". Everything else is safe in session 1 at the bar.

== Entries ==

- 3x "The Reeve has been seen at Tollamund's counting-house after dark, three nights running. Nobody visits a banker at night for a good reason."
- 3x "The captain has sold the company to the Concord. I heard it from a man who heard it from a man who cleans Tollamund's boots."
- 2x "The lantern is hollow. There's no flame. It's a mirror and a bit of cleverness, and Tallow's been lying for nine years."
- 2x "Fort Mercy's lights are on all night, and nobody's seen anyone go in or come out. Glassers don't light fires. They light something else."
- (After the ford.) "Dannic Rook was not the first deserter to go missing with lantern oil. He was the first to be found."
- "Old Tallow's eyes are worse than he lets on. He had to be walked to the privy on Hearthday."
- "The Saint's priests are buying back every burned-down candle-stub in town. Not for the light. They want the wax."
- (After the ford.) "A tin of oil went from the stores a week ago, and Fennick has the whole inventory in his head. He hasn't said a word."
- (After session 2.) "The pay wagon had Concord coin in it. Concord. Not Regency. Somebody swapped the sacks and nobody's asked who."
- "Spoons keeps a book on the lantern. Not when it dies, but who will put it out. He's got names."
- "The Ember's closer than it was. My grandmother says it's a hand's width lower every night."
- "Madame Vey's seen the captain. Not like that. Well, like that. But also not like that."
- "Kingslayers have been seen on the Treaty road. Twenty of them, armoured, silent. They didn't stop at the gate."
- "The Concord has hired a company of Marrowport lances to garrison the Keep when the Ninth is dismissed."
- "Hathe is alive. A Raveler told me. He's behind a door nobody can find, and he's very tired."
- "The Treaty Stone bled last Tideday. A pilgrim saw it. Red, thin, down the north face. The vandals denied it."
- "Lieutenant Sallow's sword is cursed. He doesn't sleep. He says the blade talks."
- "There's a dwarf in the Smokes with no shadow, and he rarely leaves the house, and he sets a place at the table for himself."
- "The Sleepless don't sleep because they can't. They're afraid of what's under the dream. They eat salt and drink bitter tea."
- "The Velvet Ledger keeps a book on everyone in town, and Madame Vey reads it before breakfast. She knows your debts, your sins and your mother's maiden name."
- "There's a ninth of something. Nobody can say what. Ninth Lantern, ninth ward, ninth stone. It's all nine."
- (After session 1.) "The Assessor's clerk hasn't blinked in three days. I watched him through the office window."
