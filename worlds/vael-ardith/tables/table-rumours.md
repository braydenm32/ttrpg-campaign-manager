Roll when someone buys a round, listens at the bar, or pays a storyteller. Give the rumour as the speaker would: half-heard, exaggerated, and with an opinion attached. Do not say which are true.

== GM notes ==

Truth key, by opening words. TRUE: "The Reeve has been seen"; "Fort Mercy's lights" (they went out, and Vexa has been seen on the roof at night); "Dannic Rook was not the first"; "The Saint's priests are buying" (candle-stubs carry what was said near them, and the Church listens); "Old Tallow's eyes"; "A tin of oil"; "The pay wagon had Concord coin"; "Spoons keeps a book"; "Madame Vey's seen the captain"; "The Assessor's clerk"; "There's a dwarf in the Smokes" (Tam Hollowell). FALSE: "The captain has sold"; "The lantern is hollow"; "Kingslayers have been seen"; "The Concord has hired"; "Hathe is alive"; "The Treaty Stone bled"; "Lieutenant Sallow's sword". PARTLY TRUE: "The Sleepless don't sleep"; "The Velvet Ledger keeps"; "There's a ninth"; "The Ember's closer" (it looks bigger low at rising, and has not grown; it will if the crack opens).

Timing. Safe in session 1 at the bar: the Reeve, the captain sold, the lantern hollow, Fort Mercy's lights, Tallow's eyes, the Saint's priests, Spoons's book, the Ember, Madame Vey, Kingslayers, the Concord's lances, Hathe, the Treaty Stone, Sallow's sword, the dwarf, the Sleepless, the Velvet Ledger, the ninth, the Assessor's clerk. Wait until after the ford: "Dannic Rook was not the first"; "A tin of oil". Wait until after session 2: "The pay wagon had Concord coin".

== Entries ==

- 3x "The Reeve has been seen at Tollamund's counting-house after dark, three nights running. Nobody visits a banker at night for a good reason."
- 3x "The captain has sold the company to the Concord. I heard it from a man who heard it from a man who cleans Tollamund's boots."
- 2x "The lantern is hollow. There's no flame. It's a mirror and a bit of cleverness, and Tallow's been lying for nine years."
- 2x "Fort Mercy's lights went out last week. Nobody's seen anyone go in or come out. Glassers don't light fires. They light something else."
- "Dannic Rook was not the first deserter to go missing with lantern oil. He was the first to be found."
- "Old Tallow's eyes are worse than he lets on. He had to be walked to the privy on Hearthday."
- "The Saint's priests are buying back every burned-down candle-stub in town. Not for the light. They want the wax."
- "A tin of oil went from the stores a week ago, and Fennick has the whole inventory in his head. He hasn't said a word."
- "The pay wagon had Concord coin in it. Concord. Not Regency. Somebody swapped the sacks and nobody's asked who."
- "Spoons keeps a book on the lantern. Not when it dies, but who will put it out. He's got names."
- "The Ember's closer than it was. My grandmother says it's a hand's width lower every night."
- "Madame Vey's seen the captain. Not like that. Well, like that. But also not like that."
- "Kingslayers have been seen on the Treaty road. Twenty of them, armoured, silent. They didn't stop at the gate."
- "The Concord has hired a company of Marrowport lances to garrison the Keep when the Ninth is dismissed."
- "Hathe is alive. A Raveler told me. He's behind a door nobody can find, and he's very tired."
- "The Treaty Stone bled last Tideday. A pilgrim saw it. Red, thin, down the north face. The vandals denied it."
- "Lieutenant Sallow's sword is cursed. He doesn't sleep. He says the blade talks."
- "There's a dwarf in the Smokes with no shadow, and he rarely leaves the house, and he sets a place at the table for himself."
- "The Sleepless don't sleep because they can't. They're afraid of what's under the dream. They eat salt and drink bitter tea."
- "The Velvet Ledger keeps a book on everyone in town, and Madame Vey reads it before breakfast. She knows your debts, your sins and your mother's maiden name."
- "There's a ninth of something. Nobody can say what. Ninth Lantern, ninth ward, ninth stone. It's all nine."
- "The Assessor's clerk hasn't blinked in three days. I watched him through the office window."
