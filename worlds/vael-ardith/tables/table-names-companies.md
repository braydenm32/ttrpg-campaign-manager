Roll for an orc, a dwarf, a Vesh or a Raveler when one steps out of the crowd. The far peoples are listed below the notes rather than rolled, because when an elf or a Shellback turns up in the March it is on purpose and the GM already knows why. For soldiers of the Ninth, see [[Soldier Names]].

**How the names work.** An orc has a hard given name and either an earned epithet or a Mother's line-name; a soldier of Mother Vosk's line is "Vosk's son" or "Vosk's daughter" until he earns something else. A dwarf has a short hard name and a hold-name; a Returned dwarf adds a second name after (Shadowless, Hollow, Came-Back, Six-Years, Owes-the-Deep) and nobody asks what it means. A Vesh has a soft given name and a trade-surname from the trades of light, and counts age in winters. A Raveler has a croak of a given name and a deed-name earned at the first burial they worked, at six or seven, which is why so many deed-names are about counting, silence and not crying.

**Elves** take soft names of two or three syllables and a house-name that changes when the house forgets itself; after a century mark they put "once-" before the old house: Caelo once-Mar, Ismere once-Fenn, Lirien once-Hale, Oriel once-Sorn, Thessaly once-Vaun, Nereth once-Morn, Aubrel once-Quill, Sabeth once-Lorne. A Remembrancer keeps her house-name as a courtesy and nobody says "once" to her face.

**Shellbacks** have long given names used once a decade, which two people alive know, and a house-name ending in the old word for a tally or a row: Tollamund, Pennamund, Veramund, Keelmund, Harrowmund, Giltmund, Ashmund, Oldmund, Ommerrow, Casterrow, Dunrow. In daily use they are Grandmother, Grandfather, Aunt or Cousin and the house: Cousin Pennamund, Aunt Veramund. A Shellback with no house does not occur, and one who says "I am of no house" is lying for a reason that will take an afternoon.

**Iskavethi** names end soft and the Saint's people take a second name from the lamp or the plateau: Ilyra of Halcyra, Seraphine Ashe, Iosune, Soran, Casimira, Dovan Ilm, Amaris Tal, Neriah Lampwright (a Vesh name borrowed, which the Vesh find rude), Teodora Salt, Imre of the Steps, Yusra Coal, Brother Caddis, Sister Oriane.

**Lithic** names are old, heavy, two syllables with the stress on the first, and no family name; a Lithic is named for the ward their line stands at: Uthrem of the Ninth, Sarrik of the Seventh, Quellith of the Third, Hadrun of the Fourth, Telune of the First, Omneth of the Sixth, Keth of the Second, Bastrem of the Fifth, Morrun of the Eighth, Ishkeh of the Ninth (the name before Uthrem's in the Wardens' roll, and the last he will say aloud). Ketterin had no ward-name because she was the first; the Lithic say it with the stress on both syllables, which no human can do.

== GM notes ==

Orcs: the epithet is a scar with a story; let the player ask. Dwarves: if the second name is Returned, the dwarf has no shadow and the scene has a cold spot. Vesh: give them something burning to lean toward. Ravelers: the deed-name is the joke and the joke is the prayer. The far peoples' names are for W5's cities and the Treaty Stone's pilgrims; in Cinderford an elf is Aelith or Sorrel or Merane, and a Shellback is Tollamund, and anyone else is news.

- Ushka Mell, orc, a Rust Choir runaway, fourteen, who thinks nobody knows whose company she left. The braid in her hair says, to anyone who was raised in it.
- Morrak Ironmouth, orc, the Hock's doorman. Says four words a day and three of them are "no".
- Zhurra Three-Scars, orc, a licensed scar-singer between contracts, waiting at the Candle for a company to sing for. Buys Vosk a drink every Restday and he lets her.
- Ragna Ulvane, surface dwarf, Gerda's tally, blue wire in the plait for Skarn. Swears by the stone that listens and means the gatehouse step.

- Torvald Gault Shadowless, Returned dwarf, sits in the Smokes with his back to the kiln. Holds doors. Will not stand in the drill yard at noon.

- Brenna Stillwell, surface dwarf, the Holds' envoy to the Treaty Stone on Widows' Walk, every year, with a barrel of salt-cod for the Ninth's dwarves. Twelve in the barrel. She counts them in front of Gerda.
- Harrek Mallow, surface dwarf, a cousin of the smith at Hollowhall who forges the key, and has had a letter about it, and has not written back.
- Morwen Dorn, surface dwarf, the Ford's locksmith, who will not make a key she has not seen the door for and has turned down two Shellbacks this year.
- Skarri Pike, surface dwarf, no relation to either Pike, and sick of saying so. Mends the bell-house stair for Dunny and will not ring the bell.
- Oume Candleby, Vesh, Hesk's second apprentice, who left for the mission's lamps and comes back for the arguments about wicks.
- Ilet Sconce, Vesh, a lamp-warden of the Regency archive on leave, who came to look at the Ninth's flame once and has stayed three winters leaning toward it.
- Peth Ashwick, Vesh, a tinsmith on Lantern Hill who makes the company's wick-lamps and tokens and knows which lots have faults, and has a list.
- Mirre Oilgrove, Vesh, keeps the lamp on a Fairlock barge that ties up beside the Dumpling, and smells the four casks in the Dumpling's hold through two hulls, and has said nothing, because it is good oil.
- Soli Taperling, Vesh, nineteen winters, a Sleepless Waker who keeps the cellar lamps and leans toward the dream the way her people lean toward a flame, and knows it.
- Auvi Wickmoor, Vesh, the Velvet Ledger's lamp-trimmer, who sees the sheen over the Rent from the roof every dusk and has told Sorrel Vey and nobody else.
- Dusk Lanternby, Vesh, old, blind, Tallow's one friend from the lamp-wardens' guild, who writes him a letter every winter telling him not to look up.
- Ennu Mothwaite, Vesh, night-clerk at Tollamund & Daughters under Fennel, who reads ledgers by their heat and has noticed that one page of one note is warmer than the rest.

- Abek Counts-Twice, Raveler, keeper of the Field of Lanterns, who counts the nine thousand graves every Ashfall and has got a different number twice.
- Rhauk No-Second-Word, Raveler, the Court's buyer at Gutter Market, who takes nothing from the ring at the Treaty Stone and watches who does.

- Skree Owes-the-Crows, Raveler, who consults the crows on the Rise and has started, this autumn, to get answers.
