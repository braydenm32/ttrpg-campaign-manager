name: Greywind Weather
summary: "The mood of the ash on a given day: from fine drift to burying gale."

Roll in the morning, or when the party leaves shelter. Nothing here kills by itself. Everything here makes the day worse. Other tables can call it by name.

== GM notes ==

If the party is far from shelter, treat a greywind as a Constitution-save hazard (DC 11) for each hour of exposure, or use cover and cloth to negate. The Ember adds a red tinge to every entry on a full-moon night.

== Entries ==

- 3x Fine ash falls like grey snow. The air tastes of old hearths. Everything is quiet.
- 3x A dry, steady wind from the east. Cloaks go grey in minutes; faces go grey in an hour.
- 2x A thin haze lies on the plain. Visible distance is a quarter-mile and shrinking.
- 2x The wind drops entirely. The ash hangs in the air without falling. Everyone coughs.
- A greywind builds on the horizon: a slow, rolling wall. You have an hour before it arrives.
- A greywind arrives. The road vanishes. Voices carry strangely. Stay together.
- A greywind in full cry. Ash drives like sand. Roll for lost items, lost animals, lost tempers.
- The wind carries the smell of something burning that has no smoke. Dogs whine. Horses shy.
- After a night of wind, the road has moved. The old stones are three paces to the left.
- Ash drifts as high as a wagon wheel against every wall. The Keep gate needs digging out.
- The sun comes out grey and cold. Shadows lean the wrong way for a minute at noon.
- Lightning without thunder, far off. Every metal buckle hums.
- Warm wind from the Rent. It smells of cut stone and cold tea. Nobody likes it.
- A still, windless day. Every footstep sounds like a clap. Anything following you can be heard.
- The Ember sits above the horizon in daylight, pale and red-ringed. Soldiers glance at it and look away.
- Ash falls in curls, like shavings. When you catch one it turns to powder.
- A green glimmer in the haze: the glasslands catching the light. For an hour the plain looks like a pond.
- The wind carries a distant bell. It is the Treaty Bell, from the Ford. It has not been rung today.
- Soft rain, the first in weeks. The ash turns to grey paste that clings to boots and takes a day to dry.
- A greywind from the north, cold and fast. Fires will not stay lit.
