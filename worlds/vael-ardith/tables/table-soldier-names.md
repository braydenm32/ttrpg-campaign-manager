Roll for a name when you need one fast. Each entry gives a name, a nickname, a people and a quirk. Use the quirk or ignore it.

== GM notes ==

Mix freely: a Vesh called "Mud" is funnier than a Vesh called "Lumen". If you roll a name twice in a session, they are cousins. Townsfolk, Hollowborn and barge-hands are on [[Names: Humans, Hollowborn and Halflings]]; company peoples and the far peoples on [[Names: Orcs, Dwarves, Vesh, Ravelers and the Far Peoples]].

== Entries ==

- Ottar "Two-Pots" Gorse, human cook. Has a ladle for every occasion and a grievance for every ladle.
- Dree Ashfoot, orc sapper. Missing two toes. Fond of whistling "The Baker's Wife".
- Pip Wattlebank, halfling scout. Counts everything. Four steps to the door, three to the stairs.
- Corporal Nim Thistle, human. Writes poetry on the back of requisition forms.
- Grundi Longshovel, dwarf engineer. Left the Grimmark with a shovel and a grudge.
- Sevar Quill, Vesh lamplighter. Sleeps through the day, argues through the night.
- Little Marl, human boy. Twenty-two, six foot four. Nobody remembers who named him.
- Tessaly Brune, human medic. Soft hands, hard words. Prefers the dying to the living.
- Old Gannet, Raveler camp-follower. Collects teeth. Pays well in gossip.
- Ruk Brightfang, orc sergeant. Dry as dust. Has not smiled since the Siege of Ardmere.
- Matty Pennyfeather, halfling quartermaster's runner. Faster than a rumour.
- Isolde Vane, human archer. Never misses. Never speaks of it.
- Brother Hask, Lithic penitent. Half stone, half silence. Carries an anvil on his back for his sins.
- Valka Three-Knives, orc scout. Has two knives. Will not explain.
- Edric "Bowl" Fenn, human cook's mate. Eats from his helm. Drinks from his boot.
- Nessa Orrow, Hollowborn drummer. One eye white, one eye brown. Talks to the dead between beats.
- Garrow Pike, human trumpeter. Plays a note he calls "the sergeant's bell". It is a fart.
- Sister Brine, human camp priestess of Grum. Prays in a roar. Curses in verse.
- Tam Hollowell, dwarf with no shadow. Sits near the fire. Holds out his hands to nothing.
- Wren Kettlebottom, halfling barge-hand turned cavalry. Rides a pony the size of a dog.
- Aurelio Bask, human deserter, returned. Wears his old tabard inside out as penance.
- Fray, Vesh archivist. Collects smells. Can tell you which hearth a man grew up by.
- Moss Gantry, human sapper. Reads aloud from a list of things that have gone wrong. It is long.
- Hurkul Ninefingers, orc smith. Has ten fingers. The name is a lie.
- Ilsabet Rowan, human spy for no one in particular. Pretends to be a laundress.
- Dorn the Careful, dwarf, rarely careful. Lost a leg to a thunderjar and bought a better one.
- Jory Lamplight, Vesh child of the camp. Eleven. Knows every soldier's last name and secret.
- Tarn Greyfeather, Raveler scribe. Records the minutes of company meetings in sarcasm.
- Calla Redwater, Hollowborn cartwright. Dreams of a river that is not there.
- Hob "Nosebag" Garrity, human ostler's mate (on loan to the company, unpaid). Knows every mule's opinion and gives his own last.
- Wenna Pike-Foot, orc spear-carrier. Gets her spears re-pointed at Ninefingers' for 2 cp and has never been charged twice.
