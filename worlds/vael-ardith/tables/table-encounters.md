Roll when the party travels the plain for more than an hour, or when you want a scene on the road. Counts are rolled for you. Some results call on **Greywind Weather**; if the table is open in the Lectern, it rolls itself.

== GM notes ==

Heavier weights go to the common things (patrols, scavengers, weather). The rare entries (the Grey Procession, the Standing Man) are for when the table needs a jolt. Re-roll anything that repeats within a session.

== Entries ==

- 3x A Ninth Lantern patrol of {1d4} soldiers, tired and short of rations, who will trade news for tobacco.
- 3x {1d4} Glassers dragging a sledge of green shards toward the Low Quay. They ask politely whether the road is watched.
- 2x A caravan stuck in the ash, wheel broken, {1d4} drovers arguing over whose fault it is. {Greywind Weather}
- 2x A halfling barge-hand on foot, carrying a sealed letter and a grievance. She will pay a silver to be walked to the river.
- {1d4} gaunt dogs trailing the party at a distance, in step. They have collars. The collars are Regency issue and forty years old.
- A tall Lithic pilgrim, grey to the elbow, walking in a straight line toward the Rent. He does not answer to his name.
- {1d4} shamblers in tattered company colours, walking home. They do not attack unless blocked; they only wish to pass.
- A burnt wagon with a Regency seal. The pay chest is gone. A bootprint in the ash is too small for a man.
- 2x A greywind rises. The road is lost in an hour. {Greywind Weather}
- {1d4} Sleepless in grey, walking in silence at dusk, hands joined. They offer the party tea and a place at their fire.
- A surveyor from Caer Ardith, with a chain, a tripod and three armed guards. He is measuring the plain for a tax nobody has announced.
- A child sitting by the road, smiling. She has no footprints around her. She says her mother will be back soon.
- The skeleton of a horse, upright in the ash, still bridled. A scroll case hangs from the saddle. Inside is a laundry list in four hands.
- {1d4} bandits of the Fort Mercy road, wearing the Ninth's old tabards. They are not as brave as they look, and they know it.
- A Raveler on a fat grey pony, pulling a cart of bodies with a cheerful whistle. The cart is full. He is looking for a place to put the next one.
- A priest of the Unburnt Saint on a mule, handing out candle-stubs to anyone who will stop. She blesses the party's wounds without being asked.
- A shimmer on the horizon that resolves into the old village of Haldane, standing whole, empty, cold. It is gone when anyone looks away.
- {1d4} orc scouts of the Iron Guard, very still, watching the road. They do not wave. They do not leave until the party does.
- A shrine of stacked stones, fresh flowers on top. Someone has carved a name into it in the last hour. The name is the party's.
- 2x A mercenary band of {1d4} Concord-hired lances, polite and well-fed, who ask where the Ninth keeps its paper.
- A glass-fused cart on its side, a man's arm sticking out of the green, fingers still moving. {Greywind Weather}
- The Grey Procession: forty figures, walking in column across the plain, silent, carrying a wooden door. They do not turn their heads.
- One man, standing alone in the ash with his arms at his sides. He is facing nothing. When the party draws close he says, "Not yet."
- A Hollowborn tinker with a hand-cart of lanterns, all lit, none of them hers. She sells them cheap and will not say where she found them.
