Roll when a PC browses the stalls, or when a Glasser tries to sell them something. Prices are the opening ask; haggle down by a quarter for a good story. The fine print is up to you.

== GM notes ==

Most items are mundane or junk; a few are leads. The Lithic fragment ties to Vexa's writing; the shimmer-coin is a hook to the Weeping Orchard. Prices in silver unless stated.

Keys. The shimmer-coins are Orchard sap, set in a coin-mould by Glassers. The rusted lantern is an iron copy from Corvel Rise, one of the poles in the Field of Lanterns; somebody has been stealing them. The crate of Concord coin stamped with a Regency die is a Quiet Office decoy: the die is too clean, and the coin is not from the Ninth's strongbox, which sits unopened in Fort Mercy. For what the town's own shops have on the shelf, [[What the Shop Has Today]].

== Entries ==

- 3x A cracked glass bowl, still warm: 4 silver. The stallholder swears it hums when full.
- 3x A bundle of melted spoons, fused into a bouquet: 2 silver. Good for a laugh.
- 3x A boot, glass-fused to the foot inside. 1 silver, foot included.
- 2x A hand-sized chunk of green glass with a perfect ring set in it: 15 silver. The ring is gold.
- 2x A broken cavalry sword, glassed to the hilt: 8 silver. It will not take an edge but looks very good on a wall.
- 2x A Regency seal ring, corroded: 6 silver. The signet reads, in neat letters, "Property of the Quartermaster".
- 2x A child's doll with one glass eye: 3 silver. It follows you with the other.
- A glass-fused prayer book, pages locked, with one legible line: 12 silver. The line says "do not open".
- A cracked bell the size of a fist, rings in a minor key: 20 silver. Rings on its own at night.
- A handful of shimmer-coins, minted nowhere, with a faceless head on one side: 10 silver for six.
- A map fragment drawn on a flayed scrap of leather, the Rent in thick lines: 25 silver. Half the symbols are crossed out.
- A rusted lantern, unlit, bearing an eight-dot ring: 18 silver. The seller says it was one of the eight.
- A flask of "glass water", clear and thick: 5 silver. Do not drink. Do not pour on stone.
- A cracked Lithic fingerbone, polished smooth: 40 silver. The seller will not meet your eye.
- A letter case with the seal of Aurel IX, empty: 30 silver. It smells of lavender and rot.
- A cask of preserved ash, labelled "good for the soul": 3 silver. It is, in fact, just ash.
- A glass cube, a hand square, with a cooked egg inside: 7 silver. The egg is still warm.
- A mercenary's tabard in the Salt Guard colours, scorched: 14 silver. Stitched into the hem is a name and a date.
- A flute carved from a cinder-wood branch, plays only one note: 9 silver. That note makes dogs howl.
- Seven teeth of seven different orcs on a cord: 16 silver. The seller says they are lucky. They are not hers.
- A crate of Concord coin stamped with a Regency die: 100 silver. The seller has more and a nervous look.
- An iron door-knocker in the shape of a hand, still locked to a door-frame: 22 silver. Someone knocks on it from the other side.
