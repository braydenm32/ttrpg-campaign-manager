---
type: table
name: Sleeper Dreams
summary: The shared dream at red-moon nights, from faint to unmistakable. Ordered mild to severe.
tags: [ random, dreams ]
---

Roll or choose when the Ember is full and a PC, a Hollowborn or a soldier sleeps within a mile of the lantern. Early lines are for the first red moons; later lines belong to the end of the campaign. Read the line aloud as the dreamer would remember it on waking.

== GM notes ==

Ordered from mild (top) to severe (bottom). Choose from the top on the first red moon, the middle by session 4, the bottom by session 7. If the Sleeper's clock is high, add one line per dreamer: "And someone was speaking to me, and I answered." Repeated dreams are the point; let the PCs compare.

== Entries ==

- You are in a warm room. A candle burns. You are happy and do not know why.
- You stand in a field of low grey grass. A river runs under it. You can hear it whisper.
- A long corridor. At the far end, a door stands slightly open. A line of light shows beneath it.
- You are carrying a lantern. It is heavier than it should be. There is a name on the base, but it is smudged.
- Someone is humming a lullaby you almost recognise. It stops when you turn your head.
- You walk across a plain of ash. Your footprints fill behind you with water.
- A great stone face is half-buried in the earth. Its eyes are open. It is breathing.
- You are underground. The walls are slick. There is a door you have already opened.
- The Ember hangs overhead, lower than it should, red as a wound. Something moves across it.
- Everyone you have ever lost is sitting at a long table. They look up as one. No one says your name.
- The lantern in your hand is going out. You cup it. Your hands go through the flame.
- A child asks you if it hurts to wake up. You do not know what to say.
- You are in the Keep, but all the doors are gone. The walls are glass. Something enormous is moving beyond them.
- You hear the Sleeper's heartbeat. It is slow, and it is under your feet, and it is under the room, and it is under the world.
- A voice from far below says, "Is it morning?" You answer before you can stop yourself.
- The sky cracks along a seam. Light pours through. It is the colour of the inside of an eye.
- You are standing at the Rent, and you are not afraid. The edge is very close. Something below says your name.
- The lantern is in your hand, and it is yours, and it is lit, and it is speaking.
- You wake in the same bed, in the same room, and the Ember is outside the window, and someone is still dreaming you.
- The Sleeper opens one eye and sees you clearly. It is not angry. It is relieved.
