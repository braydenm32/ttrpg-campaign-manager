> A low dry room cut into the valley wall, lamplit, with the Archive's racks going away into the dark beyond an open door: hides on frames, century by century, and a smell of tanned skin and oak-gall that is not unpleasant until you think about it. In the room itself, a reclining couch of pale wood, a tray of needles in a line, pots of black ink with the Old Tree's sap stirred into them so they catch the lamp green at the edge, and a tall elf in grey with his sleeves rolled up and his own forearms written over so densely they read as grey cloth. He is inking a word into the collarbone of a young elf who lies with her eyes shut, and he says each letter aloud before the needle goes in, and she says it back.

The Inkwright's is where [[Ilmareth]] writes itself down. Every elf forgets, a century at a time, and what matters is set into skin while it is still known to matter ([[Elves]]); the Inkwrights are the tattooists who do the setting, and the Readers are the ones who read it back when the owner no longer can, and [[The Remembrancers]] are both, and this room, under the Archive, is the oldest place in Sael where the work is done. Caelo once-Mar has kept it for a hundred and ten years. Before him his teacher kept it, whose name is on Caelo's right wrist, because Caelo can no longer remember her face.

He inks at the collarbone, the first place an elf is written and the last to fade, in Saelish black made from oak-gall and the sap of the Old Tree, which the Circle allows to no one else. He reads old ink for the living at a fee the Circle sets, with the wearer's hand on his wrist, which is the custom and the law: a Reader who reads a skin without its owner's hand on him is a thief, and the Archive's racks are the only skins that may be read freely, because their owners left them to Sael. He also, quietly, writes copies on parchment for the Unlettered who want to remember something and are not ready to be inked, and charges a shilling, and does not tell the Circle, who would call it a dangerous habit.

### Stock
- A memory kept, 2 gp the line, inked at the collarbone in Saelish black: a name, an oath, a child, a debt, the shape of a hand. The wearer says it aloud first. Caelo says it back. Then the needle.
- A reading of old ink, 5 sp the line, with the wearer's hand on his wrist. A line he cannot read, no charge, and a long silence.
- Saelish black, 1 sp the pot, the Old Tree's sap stirred in; it goes grey as it ages and burns, the Readers say, at the red moon. The Circle counts the pots.
- Plain black, oak-gall only, 4 cp, for anyone who is not an elf and wants a name on them.
- A parchment copy "for the Unlettered", 1 sp: a line written out in a fair hand on skin that is not anyone's.
- A needle-case, 3 sp. A Reader's gloves, grey kid, 5 sp, which [[Aelith Sorn]] buys by the pair.
- Not for sale: the Archive's labels, which Caelo writes, in the hand the Circle uses when it does not want to be seen to have written anything.

### Who is here
Caelo, at the couch, saying letters. His two Unlettered, who grind gall and hold wrists. Young elves waiting for their first line, which is their name, and old elves waiting for a reading, which is the rest. Readers in from the racks with their sleeves down and their faces grey from the lamps. A warden of the Wild once a season, for the antler on the sole, which Caelo inks for nothing and says is the only tattoo in Sael that has never faded. [[Merane Tarn]]'s three exam lines, all on one wrist, are his, and he remembers inking the third while she apologised.

### Rumours
- The label on the empty rack, *Vey, S., living, in the Ashenreach*, is in Caelo's hand, and the Circle told him to write it.
- Old ink read in this room has made Readers go quiet mid-sentence and never finish.
- Caelo can read the ink nobody can read, and lies about it.
- The Old Tree's sap in the black is why the ink burns at the red moon, and the Circle knows, and keeps using it.
- Aelith Sorn was sent to the Ashenreach to fetch a skin, not a person, and the cutting-table is already in the Archive.

:::gm
**Which are true.** 1. True. The Circle gave the order by a note with no name on it, in Thawmere 3011, and Caelo wrote the label because that is what an Inkwright is for, and then sat in the Unlettered for a night. 2. True of two Readers in the last nine years, both reading pre-1200 ink; see [[The Remembrancers]]. 3. True; see [[Caelo once-Mar]]. 4. Half: the sap is the Old Tree's and the Old Tree is the Fourth Ward's dressing, and the ink has burned at every full Ember since 3002, and the Circle does not know why and does not stop. 5. True, and the table is not in the Archive. It is in a lead-lined case in Aelith's rooms on Temple Row at Cinderford, and Merane has seen the list for it.
:::

### Things to do
Have a name inked at the collarbone and hear it said back to you by a three-hundred-year-old elf who means it. Have an old line read with your hand on his wrist and feel his pulse change when he reaches a word he does not like. Buy a pot of Saelish black and watch him mark the sale in a book that is otherwise empty. Walk through the open door into the Archive and find, between 1050 and 1200, a hundred empty racks, and on one of them a label in the same hand that just wrote your name. Ask him who wrote it, and be told, with perfect courtesy, that labels are not signed.

:::gm
This is the room where [[Sorrel Vey]]'s story began, eighty years ago. Caelo read her back, once, with her hand on his wrist, recognised the Fourth Ward's sign and the list of the Trespass dead, said he could not read it, and told her to leave Sael before someone could. She left. He has lied to the Circle about her ink ever since, which is the only lie he has ever told them, and he has told it for eighty years. See [[Caelo once-Mar]].

The parchment copies are his second heresy and his hope: if Sorrel's back can be copied onto skin that is nobody's, the record survives without the cutting-table, and Sael can be given back what it burned without anyone being flayed for it. He has the Old Tree's sap, the needles and the hand. He does not have Sorrel, or the Circle's leave, or Aelith's cooperation, and Aelith was his pupil.

Post-session 8: a party that brings Sorrel here, alive and willing, gets the full reading of [[The Saelish Trespass]] in Caelo's room with her hand on his wrist, and Sael gets its memory back on parchment, and the Circle has to decide what to do with a Remembrancer who copied what they ordered cut. A party that brings only Aelith's lead case gets Caelo's silence, and then his needles, because an Inkwright who has read a skin cut from a living elf does not finish the sentence.
:::

== GM notes ==

Caelo says every letter aloud before he inks it: play that rhythm in his speech, careful, one word at a time, as if each might be the one he forgets. The shop is gentle and the Archive's door is the horror: a hundred empty racks and one new label. Let an elf PC be asked, kindly, whether they would like their third tattoo read, and let them have to decide.
