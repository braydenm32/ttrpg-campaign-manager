Ardmere sits on a bend of the Ardis where the river narrows enough to chain. In 2994 Hallam Ostrey's Free Host, three claimants' contingents under one hired general, sat outside it from Bloomtide to Frostcall, and the town ate its horses, its dogs, its boots and, by the end, some of its dead. When [[The Siege of Ardmere]] broke it was not a royal army that broke it but three mercenary companies who had not been paid by anyone and simply decided the siege was bad for business. That was the day everyone understood who the real armies were.

The walls are rebuilt in a different colour of stone. The Widows' Bench, forty women who kept the town alive that autumn, still sits in judgment on market days and the prefect does not cross it. The Mothers keep a hall here, the Iron Hearths, and the orcs who walk in and out of it are the only people in Ardmere who are never asked for papers. Every mercenary company that lifted the siege has a stone on the river wall; the Ninth Lantern's is not there, because the Ninth did not exist yet, but two of its veterans were there, under other banners. [[Ilse Marrow]] was twenty-seven and a supply clerk inside the walls for all 211 days, and does not talk about it. [[Spoons Aldane]] came through the water-gate behind the Long Spoons as their pot-boy, with the first pot, and will tell you about the pot for the price of a drink.

### Hook
Someone has been scratching a fourth company's name onto the memorial wall at night, a company nobody in Ardmere has heard of. The name is the Ninth Lantern.

:::gm
The scratching is done by an Ardmere veteran, Marrow's old messmate from inside the walls, who has heard what the Ninth is owed and what it stands for, and wants the name in stone before it is gone. He will talk to Marrow and to no one else.
:::
