East of the Ardis the land rises in a wall of mountains called the Saint's Spine, named for Iskaveth's old lamp-saints centuries before Ilyra (her followers claim it now), and beyond them lies a high dry plateau of salt lakes, terraced orchards and white towns, falling away at its far edge to the Glass Sea. That is Iskaveth. The empire conquered it in 2390 and spent six hundred years failing to keep it quiet.

### The people
Plateau humans, dark and dry-humoured, who pray with their hands in a lamp flame and consider the rest of the continent damp. Vesh are honoured here as lamp-keepers; orcs are not welcome; Hollowborn are brought to Halcyra for "the Saint's mercy", and some of them come back.

### Who rules
Iskaveth was a dozen temple-cities under an imperial governor until 3002. Then a camp healer named [[Ilyra of Halcyra]] walked out of the fire at Cinders unburnt, and the plateau rose. In 3007 the Synod of Lamps declared her a living saint. She has not given audience since; she is seen only at the Lamp Court's lattice on full-Ember nights, and her word comes through the Synod of Lamps and through envoys like [[Seraphine Ashe]]. The old gods are still prayed to, politely, as her household. [[The Church of the Unburnt Saint]] is the state.

### In the Ashenreach
Iskaveth calls the Ashenreach the Saint's birthplace and has sent a mission, [[The House of Unburnt Light]], to "tend the wounded land". It feeds the poor, heals the sick, and asks a great many gentle questions about the lantern. The Ember is the Saint's Lamp, in their telling, lit the night she rose.

- Iskaveth is building an army behind the Spine.
