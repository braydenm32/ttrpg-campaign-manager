A Lithic monolith five hundred feet tall, standing on its own pillar in the Rent, its crown leaning out over the northern lip. Visible for miles.

> The Stone is a tower that was never built: a single finger of grey Lithic rock five hundred feet from root to crown, standing on a pillar of its own in the dark of the chasm with its top hundred feet leaning twenty degrees out over the lip as if it had been pushed, and down its whole length a crack a hand wide that glows, at night, the colour of the Ember. At its foot a ring of nine lesser stones, each the rough shape of a standing figure, and one of them is missing. On the step of the ninth, where the figure should be, something that was a person sits very still with its legs already grey and its hands folded in its lap, and raises its head, and the sound it makes is of two stones being drawn apart.

:::gm
The Ninth Stone is the anchor of the Ninth Great Ward and the last thing the Nine made. Inside it, awake, is one of the Nine: the youngest, say the Lithic, whose name the gods struck from memory with the rest. She has held the Ninth Ward for three thousand and eleven years. The night of the battle the sorcery cracked her, and a shard the length of a finger blew out of the crack and west, sixty miles, into the Weeping Orchard, and was found by a cook. That shard is the flame in the lantern on Lantern Hill. The ward holds because the shard still burns; the crack widens because it burns sixty miles from where it should be.

### Areas
- **The way down**: the Stone's root is on the Warden's Shelf, about four hundred feet below the north lip. From the lip it is 300 feet of old company rope steps down the wall to the first ledge, then the Glass Stair (walk the red glass) to the Halfway Ledge, where a party can camp, then a short traverse onto the Shelf. The Choir opens in a side cleft off the stair.
- **The ring**: nine figure-stones round the foot, the Nine's companions, each a Lithic who came to stand and did. The ninth socket is empty and the step is worn by nine years of a half-stone man sitting on it. Beside the ring three lamps are cut into the rock, with a Lithic line under them: *Holding. Send relief.* (the Wardens' lamp-code; see [[Signals of Cinderford]]).
- **The Warden's step**: [[Warden Uthrem]], last of the Ninth's line of [[The Last Wardens]], who has stood here forty years; the others have stood and set. He can move his head and his hands, and speaks a few sentences at a time, more when the flame is near. He has spent nine years waiting since the crack for someone who could hear the flame to come, because he cannot go and get it, and he has decided, over nine years of watching the plain, that the one who comes will be a Vesh.
- **The crack**: wide enough at the foot to put an arm in, warm, humming. Anyone who touches the Stone with bare skin hears the one inside breathing. A Hollowborn hears her speak. Ysse hears her ask, warmly and very slowly, to stand with her.
- **The heart-socket**: inside the crack, ten feet up, a hollow in the stone the exact shape of the Ward Shard, with a hairline of red light running from it down and west under the glass, toward Cinderford. A ward needs a living heart; the Nine's youngest has been the heart alone for three thousand years, and the crack is where she is coming apart.
- **The Lithic road**: climbs from the chasm floor to the Shelf. The covered road through [[The Cold Seam]] comes out onto it through the chasm wall, somewhere below the Shelf. Down it, somewhere, is the Sleeper. Uthrem has never gone down. He says the stair is for the one who holds the flame.

### What Uthrem will tell them (session 6)
The truth of [[The Godslaying]], plainly, once, in a voice like gravel: who the Nine were, what they did, what it cost, what the Lithic are, what the lantern is, and what the ward needs. A new heart. Him, if someone carries him into the crack, which will kill him and buy twenty years. A willing PC, which buys a life. Ysse, whom the flame has already chosen, which buys the age. Or nothing, and the crack goes on opening, and he will describe what comes up the stair when it does.

### The three endings, here
- **Reseal**: the shard goes back into the heart-socket with a living heart beside it. The crack closes over a night. The Ember dims. The flame in Ysse's hands goes out; Marrow reads the contract aloud at the Stone, and the company stands down. The cage on Lantern Hill is dark when they get home.
- **Let it crack**: the shard is kept away, or destroyed, or the Saint's people pour what is leaking into their lamp. The flame in the cart goes out, and the dead on the lip turn round. The Stone falls into the Rent and the Nine's youngest with it.
- **Use it**: the shard is held, by a PC or a faction, and the holder learns to speak to what is under the stair and to be spoken to. The crack stays as it is. The holder starts to dream.

== GM notes ==

Keep the Stone off the table until session 6 and then give it the whole session: the approach, the dead, the road, the Warden's voice. Uthrem is not a quest-giver; he is a man who has been sitting on a step for nine years deciding what to say, and he says it badly and all at once. Let Ysse's player feel the flame's pull as a physical thing here. For the script on the Stone, see [[A Rubbing from the Ninth Stone]].
