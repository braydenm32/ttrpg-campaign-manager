> The creek bed turns and there is the lake, a mile of water the colour of pewter and as flat as a plate, in a wind that is moving the ash on the shore. Nothing on it moves. Fifteen houses of ash-brick along the near bank, nets drying, a child on the jetty with a line in the water and one pale eye turned toward you before the other one is. The water by the jetty is clear to the bottom, and the bottom is further down than it should be, and there are people standing on it.

Stillwater is the only settlement on the grey lakes, which filled the low ground north of the Rent in the years after the battle from springs nobody had known were there. The water is drinkable, just, and full of a pale fish without eyes that tastes of nothing and keeps people alive.

The first Stillwater was an older village on the same shore, about three hundred souls, and it went under on 9 Frostcall 3006. Seventy came out alive: parents, a few Ravelers, and most of its Hollowborn children, among them a girl of twelve who would become the Dreaming Widow. They joined the shore settlement that Smokes families had begun in 3004, thirty people who had found the town hard, and that is the Stillwater of today. They fish, dry fish, trade dried fish to Kettle Brindlewake for salt and iron, and look at the water.

The lake does not ripple. Wind, oars, a stone, a body: the surface takes it and is flat again before you can blink. Under it, on calm days, which is all of them, the Hollowborn see the dead standing on the lake bed, hundreds of them, facing east, patient. Everyone else sees mud and fish. The Stillwater folk say the dead are waiting for the door to open and have come to the one place they can see it from. At red moon a bell rings under the water, low and slow, and always one beat behind where it ought to be.

### Who is here
[[Gammer Teal]], late-eyed at ninety: she took the grey eye the night the lake rose, and has been the oldest woman on the shore ever since. Her grand-niece Aud stands in the glass at [[Ambercote]], twenty miles south, with the child she was carrying; the Teals do not speak of it. The families of the drowned village and of the Smokes, who do not always agree. [[The Dreaming Widow]] was one of the seventy: she gave her first sermon on the jetty that same winter, and she still comes back to sit on the shore at red moon.

### Rumours
- The lake is the top of the Rent, flooded.
- The blind fish see the dead.
- Gammer Teal has spoken to the Sleeper.
- At red moon the dead under the lake take one step.

:::gm
**Which are true.** 1. No. But it connects. 2. The Stillwater folk say so and will not eat the heads. 3. She says it spoke to her, once, and said "not yet", and she has not slept properly since. 4. True. They are closer to the jetty than they were.
:::

### Things to do
Buy fish. Ask Gammer Teal about the dream and be told, flatly, what it is. Look into the water as a Hollowborn or beside one. Row out, which nobody does, and see the dead look up. Bring [[Tibbet Marlowe]] here and let him look for his mother's face among them.

:::gm
Tick will not find his mother among the dead under the lake, because Marla is standing behind him; let the player notice that first. Gammer Teal is the one person in the March who will tell the PCs the Hollowborn truth plainly: every Hollowborn dreams the same dream at full red moon, a stone door and a lamp and a hand on the latch from inside, and it is getting clearer. She does not want the Sleeper woken and thinks the Widow is a fool, and she is the Sleepless' natural counterweight if the PCs want one. Use the lake for a scene, not a dungeon: the dead under it do not come up unless the crack opens. The pale ash that drifts up the shore smells of lake water, and it is what cut the lantern's oil: the Widow's people carry it out in jars, and anyone from Stillwater who is asked about the smell will go quiet. The jars leave Stillwater on Smokes carts and are left in the Tollivers' goat-shed at [[Bellwether]], a copper a jar from [[Ambry Coyle]]; the Assessor's errand boy collects them at dusk on Coinday (see [[Goodwife Marn Tolliver]]).
:::
