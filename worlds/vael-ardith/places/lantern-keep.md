> Inside the gate the Keep is smaller than it looked from below. A yard the size of a farm's, a well, a square tower with its top storey still fire-blackened, and a long hall with the company's contract cut into the lintel, the letters picked out in lamp-black. The hall smells of wet wool, onions and lamp-oil. Someone is laughing in the kitchen. Someone is being sick behind the stables. Above it all, through a slit window on the east side, a steady yellow light.

The Keep was the old lord's tower, burned out in 3002 and roofed again by the company in the winter after. It has never been finished; nobody expected to need it finished. The contract over the door is the famous one: *"The company shall hold the Keep and the ford for the signatories, and shall stand down if the lantern ever goes out."* The soldiers' version is shorter: *we go when it goes.*

### Rooms
- **The hall.** Meals, musters, the captain's table on the dais, seven battered lanterns of the dead companies hung dark along the wall beside an empty hook, as a reminder. (The eighth hangs over the bar of [[The Last Candle]].)
- **The captain's room**, above the hall. A desk, a cot, a locked box that is nearly empty, and a window that looks east.
- **The oil-room**, in the tower's base, under the cage. Shelves of oil-tins, a trimming bench, a cot, and the ladder up to the cage through a trap. [[Brother Tallow]] lives here and [[Ysse Lampwright]] has been given the corner. The cage's oil-port is at its foot, fed from here. Entry by Tallow's leave.
- **The stores**, three locks, [[Fennick Bottlebrook]]'s kingdom. Flour for eleven days at full ration (Fennick tells everyone nine), grain for three weeks more if the horses go first, salt for one week, arrows for a morning.
- **The cold-cellar**, under the kitchen, where the dead wait for [[Quorl Many-Words]] and the burial cart.
- **The barracks**, the long shed against the north wall. Straw, snoring, a hundred and forty people's worth of smell.
- **The east wall walk**, Lieutenant [[Garrick Sallow]]'s post, with the best view of the ash and the cage.

### Who is here
Everyone who matters in the company, at one time or another in the day. [[Ilse Marrow]] at the table at dusk. [[Mirrel Vosk]] in the yard at dawn. Fennick in the stores at all hours, counting. Tallow, always.

### Things to do
Eat. Be issued kit that does not fit. Be lit a hooded tin wick-lamp from the Lantern by the lamp-keeper the morning you sign, and keep it burning. Hear the contract read aloud, as it is to every recruit, by Vosk, in a voice that makes it sound like a dirty joke. Be sent down to the cold-cellar with a body and come back changed.

:::gm
The oil-room is the crime scene for session 1: the tins are supposed to be sealed Keel whale-oil, and three of the last dozen have been opened, cut and resealed with Hesk's own grey wax. See [[The Cut Oil Tin]] and [[Hesk's Chandlery]]. The locked box in the captain's room holds one gold piece and a letter from Sorrel Vey.

**The cage.** An iron cage the size of a beehive sits in an open niche on the Keep's outer east wall, twenty feet up, beside the east wall-walk and above the oil-room. It is reached by a locked outside ladder or by the trap-ladder from the oil-room; the oil-port at its foot is fed from the oil-room below and needs no cage key. Three locks, three keys: Tallow's, Marrow's, Vosk's. Turning any key sounds a chime through the Keep. The cage can be unbolted and lifted down (lantern and cage weigh about 30 lb): in session 8 it goes east on a handcart.

**The company's strength** in session 8 comes from the Mutiny clock: about 120 marching at 0 to 2, about 100 at 3 to 4, about 60 at 5 to 6, with Sallow's diehards on the road.
:::

== GM notes ==

Use the hall for the company's big scenes: payday-that-is-not, Sallow's speeches, the captain standing up slowly. The eight dark lanterns on the wall are a good thing to have a veteran point out to a recruit on their first night.
