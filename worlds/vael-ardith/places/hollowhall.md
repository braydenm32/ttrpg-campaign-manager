The dwarves began cutting Hollowhall in 412 AW and have never entirely stopped. Its gate is a cliff face carved into a hundred stone faces, all of them Lithic, all of them worn, and behind it the upper halls run a mile into the mountain under vaults that make visitors whisper. Surface dwarves live here and hold the Lithic holy: ancestors made stone, and the halls are built as a long prayer to them.

The deep halls are another matter. During the war a tenth of the hold went down the Sealed Stair to wait out the fighting, and in 3004 they came out by the great gate casting no shadows and saying nothing. The Stair is still shut, sealed from below with a stone no chisel marks. The Returned live in the lowest open halls, work the forges, pay their tithes, and are watched by their own kin with a love that is becoming fear.

### Hook
A Returned smith at [[The Underlook Forge]], [[Durn Mallow]], has been forging the same object over and over since 3004: a key, hand-long, in a pattern nobody recognises. He melts each one down when it is finished. He says he does not know why.

:::gm
The pattern is [[Hathe's Door-Key]]'s. The smith came up in 3004 with the first Returned, and something in what the Eighth Ward did to him remembers the key without remembering why. The door he cannot name is the Red Seam door under the Glass Barrow, and its two sisters: the Cold Seam landing and the root of the Ninth Stone. He melts each key because he is afraid of what happens when one is finished.
:::

### Where to drink and buy
[[The Shadowless Cup]] in the lowest open hall, where the Returned drink with their backs to the rock and no lamp behind the benches, and a place is set every night for the ones still down. [[The Underlook Forge]] under the gate, the finest tools on the continent, made on a lit side and a dark one. At the gate itself, [[Hold-Mother Greta Holm]] keeps the pebble shrine of Tharos and the one rite nobody has asked her for in forty years.
