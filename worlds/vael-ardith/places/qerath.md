Qerath is where the plateau falls into the sea in a long white scarp, and the town is built down it in steps to a harbour of black sand. The Glass Sea is named for its calm; on a still day it is a sheet, and from the harbour wall you can see down twenty fathoms to the drowned terraces of whatever stood here before Iskaveth.

Every Seedwake a ship leaves the Missionaries' Mole for [[Ostrakai]] with eight or nine of the Saint's faithful aboard, lamps lit, singing. The ships come back with cargo. The missionaries do not. The harbourmaster, an old imperial officer who kept his post through the rising by being useful, paints each name on the wall of his office and has run out of wall.

### Hook
The forty-first name on the harbourmaster's wall was painted over last month, carefully, by someone with a key. The harbourmaster says he did not do it. The missionary in question was a Hollowborn girl from the Smokes of Cinderford, taken to Halcyra for mercy in 3006, and her mother still lives on Tanners' Lane.
