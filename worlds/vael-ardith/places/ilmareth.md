Ilmareth is not so much a city as a valley the elves have lived in for so long that the trees grew around them. Halls are grown rather than built; the paths are moss; and at the valley's head stands the Old Tree, which is older than the elves' arrival in Sael and which they will not discuss.

The Circle meets at the Tree's roots. Below them, in dry halls cut into the valley wall, is the Archive of Skins: the tanned and inked hides of elves who chose to leave their memory to Sael when they died, racked by century. The Remembrancers tend it. Whole racks are empty between 1050 and 1200 AW, and the Remembrancers say there was a fire.

### Hook
A rack in the Archive that has stood empty for eighteen centuries has a label again, in a modern hand: *Vey, S., living, in the Ashenreach*. Nobody on the Circle admits writing it. The hand is an Inkwright's; see [[The Inkwright's]]. [[Aelith Sorn]], then in Caer Ardith, was on the road to Cinderford within the week.

:::gm
There was a fire, and the Circle lit it: the skins of 1050 to 1200 AW recorded [[The Saelish Trespass]], and Sael chose to forget. The label is how the Remembrancers learned where the last record lives: on Sorrel Vey's back.
:::

### Where to drink and buy
[[The Unlettered]], a hall grown from a hollow beech below the Archive, where elves with little ink drink green wine and the tally heals by morning; Warden [[Liriel Tarn]] keeps it. [[The Inkwright's]], under the Archive, where memory is set into skin at two crowns the line by [[Caelo once-Mar]], who taught half the Readers in Sael. Outsiders reach either by one of the three gates with a bond lodged, or not at all.
