> The fort stands on the only rise for ten miles, a square of blackened Regency stone with one tower still roofed and the others open to the sky, and around it, like a skirt, the Glassers' town: tents, lean-tos, carts, a windmill made of shield-boards, and dig-pits going down into the glass in every direction, each with its windlass and its rope and its bucket. Smoke from a dozen fires. A dog barks. From the tower a woman's voice, carrying: "If that's the company, tell them the toll's doubled and I still don't want them."

Fort Mercy was built by the Regency in 2996 to hold the eastern road and named, with imperial humour, for the mercy it was meant to bring the pear country. It burned on a summer night in 3005 with its garrison and three hundred refugees inside, and nobody got the gate open ([[The Burning of Fort Mercy]]). The Glassers moved in the next year and found a well still sweet and a cellar still dry, and have dug from it since. [[Vexa Glassjaw]] took it over in 3008 by the simple method of being the last person standing in the argument.

### What is here
- **The tower**, Vexa's, with a view of every dig and a shovel over the hearth that she calls the Bishop, because it has buried more men than any priest.
- **The yard**, where the Glassers sort, fight, sell to each other and drink something made from stone pears.
- **The well**, the only sweet water east of the Treaty Stone. Vexa charges for it by the bucket and that is the whole of her law.
- **The cellar**, under the keep: cool, dry, and on its far wall, where the Glassers broke through into older stonework, a panel of grey Lithic stone covered in glyphs that Vexa has had lamps on for a year and cannot read.
- [[The Cold Seam]], the deepest dig, below the cellar, where a digger called Brakk went down in Reaping and did not come up, and which Vexa has roped off and goes down herself at night.
- **The dig-pits**, twenty or more, across the glasslands around the rise. Each has a name and a tally of what came up and who did not.

### Who is here
Vexa. Her second, [[Ottoline Crane]], a human dowser who finds metal under glass by walking barefoot and bleeding. [[Skelt]], who drinks. Sixty diggers of every people, including [[Mardle Ock]], a Returned dwarf who says he will not go below the cellar. A Concord factor, often, with a cart. A boy who sells water from Vexa's well to those who cannot reach it and is beaten weekly for it.

### Rumours
- Vexa has found a Lithic city under the fort.
- The Glassers robbed the pay wagon.
- Brakk is still alive down the seam and calls up at night.
- The factor pays in Concord coin for anything that hums and takes it to Gilt.

:::gm
**Which are true.** 1. A waystation. Big enough. 2. Vexa laughs at this, a little too long. 3. Something calls up at night. 4. True.
:::

### The cellar

> Down a stair of Regency brick into a vault that is cool and dry and smells of nothing at all, which after the yard is a kind of shock. A dozen lamps stand on the floor in a half-ring, burned low, the way you leave lamps round a sick person. In the far wall the Glassers' pick-marks end and something older begins: a panel of grey stone, smooth as a plate, cut edge to edge with writing too regular to be scratches and too strange to be letters. One corner of it has been pulled a finger's breadth out of true. The cold that comes from the gap does not move the lamp flames. Somebody has chalked a tally on the floor in front of it and stopped.

### Things to do
Pay for water. Buy a dig's chart. Be hired to go down a pit and bring up what the diggers will not touch. Read the cellar writing, if you can, and watch Vexa's face. Go down the cold seam on a rope and come back up with frost on your beard in Ashfall.

:::gm
Session 2 ends here. The Glassers did not rob the wagon; they were paid to receive it, and are not sure what they did. Tollamund's drivers brought the wagon into her mill-yard at the Ford on the night of 6 Ashfall; at dawn on the 7th it crossed the bridge as a sealed House cart, turned off at the Burned Mill, and the Concord factor, Cousin Pennamund, had Vexa hold the box "until collected". Inside: 600 gp in new Sevenhaven coin and the loan-chit. The strongbox sits unopened in the cellar behind the Lithic panel's loose corner; Vexa has not opened it because a Shellback told her, very slowly, not to.

**The cellar writing** is the Barrow's lintel line, *WHAT WE BURIED IS NOT AT REST*, and below it a longer passage the Glassers have chipped at, which ends *The lamp holds the lock; the heart holds the lamp.* Behind the panel is a black-glass chamber thirty feet down with script on every wall; the Cold Seam drops from the chamber, ninety feet of rope and then ladders. [[Ottoline Crane]], Ilbert Crane's granddaughter, knows the seam is on her grandfather's drawings. Mardle goes down at night, below the cellar he swears he will not enter, and hums three notes until Brakk's voice stops asking for rope. [[Skelt]] guided Dannic Rook into the Glass Barrow on 3 Ashfall and came out alone; he will talk for drink, or to save his skin. Aelith Sorn will pay anything to see the writing. Vexa herself is a fair dealer if dealt with fairly, a terrible enemy if not, and is more frightened of what is under her fort than of anyone in the company.
:::

== GM notes ==

Vexa's voice: loud, orcish, funny, no patience for soldiers, enormous patience for stone. She names her tools and talks to the Bishop. She wants someone to read the cellar to her and will trade the strongbox's location for it if the party plays it right.
