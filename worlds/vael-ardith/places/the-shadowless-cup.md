> The stair goes down past six halls lit like festivals and into one that is not. The lowest open hall of Hollowhall is a vault the size of a cathedral and the lamps in it hang only from the centre of the roof, a long way up, so that the light comes straight down onto the tables and the benches along the walls are in the dark. Dwarves sit on them. You can hear them, cups and low talk and a laugh now and then, but you cannot see whether the floor behind them is bright or not, and that is the point. At the hall's end, a bar cut into the living rock, a woman behind it with her plait tied in grey wire and no beard and eyes that take the lamplight like a cat's, and beside the bar a long table with bowls set along it, full, steaming, and nobody at them.

The Cup opened in the winter of 3004, four months after the great gate of [[Hollowhall]] swung open from the inside and the first column of [[The Return from the Deep|the Returned]] walked out into a sun that showed what they had lost. The surface clans gave them the lowest hall, which was kindly meant and understood correctly. Ingrid Dorn, who had kept a tap in the upper halls before the war and come back from under the mountain without her husband or her shadow, took the bar at the hall's end and had the wall-lamps taken down, and the Returned came, and have come every night since. The surface dwarves call it the Shadowless Cup. The Returned call it the Cup, and do not say the other word, and the surface dwarves who drink here, who are more each year, have learned not to either.

The place at the end is the thing the hall is known for and the thing nobody explains to strangers. Every night Ingrid sets the long table by the bar with a bowl and a cup for every party that went down the Sealed Stair and has not all come up: the Harl miners, the Coombe clan's three brothers, a Skarn deep-road hunting party that went under in the spring of 3008 looking for kin. When one of a party comes up, a bowl comes off that party's place. Nobody eats what is set; at lamps-up it goes to the gate-shrine and the gate-dogs. The Skarn party's place has lost two bowls in three years: one a year and a half ago, one in the spring.

### Menu
- Deep ale, 2 cp. Brewed in the dark with mountain water, black, and the Returned say it tastes of home, and do not say which one.
- Whey-spirit, 3 cp a thimble, at its source. At [[The Last Candle]] in Cinderford they call it "the Returned", because you come back without your shadow; here it has no name, and if you say the Candle's for it, Ingrid pours you a second one and does not charge, which is a worse punishment than it sounds.
- Barley broth with mutton, 2 cp, the bowl the long table is set with. Hot, thick, salted. "What they'd want."
- Flatbread baked on the forge-stones upstairs, 1 cp.
- Pickled eggs, 1 cp for two. The surface dwarves' contribution and the only thing in the hall anyone complains about.
- A bench to sleep on, free, in the dark end. Nobody will wake you and nobody will look.
- Silence, free, and the best on the continent.

### Who is here
The Returned, two or three hundred of the nine hundred on any night, sitting with their backs to the rock. Surface dwarves of the Underlook hold who have a Returned brother or wife or mother and have come down to sit near them in the only room where they can do it without looking at the floor. A hold-priest of [[Tharos]], sometimes, who sits at the bar and does not pray and is not asked to. Durn Mallow, the smith, once a week, with the forge-black still on him, who drinks one cup and looks at the long table's Skarn place and leaves. Gate-dogs, who go where they like, and lie under the long table, and do not touch what is on it.

### Rumours
- The Returned do not sleep, and the Cup is where they go instead.
- Set a place at the long table for someone and they will come up within the year.
- There is a Returned dwarf in Cinderford who sets a place for himself at his own table every night and nobody knows why.
- Ingrid Dorn's husband is still down there, and she sets his bowl under the bar where nobody can see it.
- Something came up with the last of the Skarn party in the spring, and the gate-dogs would not go near him.

:::gm
**Which are true.** 1. False: they sleep, and do not dream, and the Cup is where they go so as not to be watched doing either. 2. Not true, and the surface clans have tried it with every name they have lost since the war. 3. True; the Smokes' Returned dwarf is Tam Hollowell, one of the Skarn party that went down with [[Borrin Halt]], who came up a year before him and went south without stopping: the first bowl that came off the Skarn place was his. 4. True. 5. True: Borrin Halt came up through the great gate in the spring with three years gone, the gate-dogs lay flat, and the bowl that came off the Skarn place that night was his. Ingrid remembers his face. She remembers that the dogs did not stand up until he was through the gate and on the road, and she has not said so to anyone, because nobody asks where you have been.
:::

### Things to do
Drink in a room where nobody can tell what you are, and find that more restful than you expected. Read the long table: bowls for the Harls, the Coombes, the Skarn party with its two gaps, and work out which gap is Borrin's. Sit near a Returned dwarf long enough and be told, in a low voice, what the deep ale tastes of, which is the only thing any of them will say about the deep. Ask Ingrid for the whey-spirit by the Candle's name and learn what a second thimble costs. Be asked, by a surface dwarf two benches down who cannot see your feet, whether you came up or came down, and understand that both answers are allowed here.

:::gm
The Cup is the other end of Borrin's thread, and of Tam Hollowell's and Durn Mallow's. Under the Grimmark the Returned found the Eighth Ward breached and something on the far side of it took their shadows as the price of passage ([[The Nine Great Wards]]); the shadows are not gone, they are with it, and it knows every Returned dwarf by the shape it keeps. The Returned feel this as a cold at the back of the neck when they stand in light, which is why the benches are dark. The one it let come *up* inside a shadow is Borrin's, and the gate-dogs knew.

The long table is the hold's honest ledger of who is still under the mountain. The Skarn party's place, by Ingrid's count, has bowls for the ones who have not come up; she will not say how many went down, because the Cup does not count out loud, but a dwarf PC can count the bowls, and Borrin can tell them whose they were. If the party comes to Hollowhall after session 8 with Borrin, Ingrid takes the last bowls off the Skarn place herself, in front of him, one by one, and asks nothing, and that is the end of that thread or the beginning of the next campaign, depending on whether the bowls are empty when she lifts them.

Spells cast in the lowest hall frost the rock, the same as in the Glass Barrow: the Cup is within a mile of the Stair That Waits, and the Eighth is listening.
:::

== GM notes ==

Ingrid's voice is quiet, practical and entirely without self-pity: she pours, she sets the table, she tells you the rule once. The Returned here should be warm, not eerie: they hold doors, pass the bread, laugh at the pickled eggs. The dread is in the architecture, the dark benches and the full bowls, and in the one thing nobody does, which is look down. If Borrin is at the table, give him a quiet beat when a gate-dog lies flat under the long table and the room does not notice, and he does.
