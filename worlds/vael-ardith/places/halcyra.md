Halcyra is white stone on a hill above a salt lake, and from the plain below it looks on fire every night, because every window, step and rooftop holds a lamp and the lamps are never allowed to go out. The air smells of hot oil and orange blossom. Pilgrims climb the Thousand Steps on their knees and are healed at the top, often.

At the summit is the Lamp Court, the old imperial governor's palace, where [[Ilyra of Halcyra]] lives behind a screen of flame. She has not given audience since 3007; she is seen only at the Lamp Court's lattice on full-Ember nights. Her voice reaches the city through the Synod and through envoys who come back from her presence with a look that does not fade. The Mercy Houses below the Court take in the sick, the poor, and the Hollowborn, and are famous for their kindness. Not everyone who enters a Mercy House is seen again, and nobody who asks about it is answered unkindly.

### Hook
Before she was the Saint, Ilyra had a sister, a mother and a lover in Halcyra. All three now live in the Lamp Court and none has written to anyone since. The lover's brother keeps a stall at the foot of the Thousand Steps and will tell the story to anyone who buys a lamp.

:::gm
The sister is in the Lamp Court's kitchens, alive and not allowed past the scullery door; the mother and the lover are kept with her. The Church's clock "The Saint ascends" ticks behind every locked door in the Court, and the families of the saints are the first locks.
:::
