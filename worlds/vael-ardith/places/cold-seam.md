> Behind the cellar's Lithic panel the Glassers broke through into a crack in the stone, a seam of black glass running down and east at a steep slant, polished on the near side by a year of bodies going down it. A windlass stands over the hole with the rope let all the way out. The air coming up is cold enough to show your breath in Ashfall, and smells of frost and, very faintly, of struck flint. A tin plate nailed to the post says BRAKK, 19 REAPING, in six languages, because the Glassers are not sure which one he will read.

The seam is where the fort's Lithic foundation opens into something larger. Vexa Glassjaw's diggers followed it for ninety feet in the spring, found steps, then a chamber where the walls were carved with the same glyphs as the cellar panel, and stopped when the cold began to hurt. Brakk, who was a Grimmark man with no fear and less sense, went down alone one night in Reaping with a lamp and a bottle. The lamp was found at the bottom of the ladders, lit. The bottle was found a hundred feet further on, full.

Since then the Glassers do not go below the first chamber. Vexa goes down alone, at night, with the Bishop, and comes up grey and says nothing. On still nights a voice at the bottom of the seam calls up, in Brakk's voice, asking for the rope. [[Mardle Ock]], the Returned dwarf who digs for Vexa, says it is not Brakk. He says it is what Brakk met, and he does not say how he knows.

### Rumours (in the Glassers' yard)
- The seam goes all the way to the Rent.
- Brakk found gold and will not share.
- Vexa talks to it.

:::gm
**Which are true.** 1. Probably true. 2. Glassers' optimism. 3. She does. She asks it questions. It has started answering them.
:::

:::gm
The seam is a Lithic road, the Ninth Road itself, the covered way that runs under the Barrow's stair, the Burned Mill, Tally's Cut and Ambercote's square (the same line [[A Lithic Road-Marker]] points along); it runs east under the glasslands toward the Rent and is how session 6's expedition can reach the Ninth Stone's foot without crossing sixty miles of open ash, if the party is brave enough to take it. The cold belongs to the seam, not the road: past the Lithic door on the landing ([[Hathe's Door-Key]] turns it) the road runs forty miles east, dry and still, with waystations along it, and comes out through the chasm wall onto the Lithic road below the Warden's Shelf. Timings, the horses and the door are in session 6, Into the Rent. What took Brakk is a shadow of the same kind that wears Borrin's: a passenger from a breached ward, thin, hungry, patient, and very interested in the Returned dwarf above. It will answer Borrin, or Mardle, in Borrin's voice. Not a fight to force on a level 2 party; a conversation, and a reason to come back. In session 2 it appears as a shadow in a digger's shape that talks; a ghoul only for his body.

**Why go.** Vexa pays 20 gp to bring Brakk back, or to shut his voice up.
:::
