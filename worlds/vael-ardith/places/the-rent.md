> You smell it before you see it: cold stone and pears and something under both like a struck match. Then the plain simply stops. A lip of fused glass, and beyond it air, and the far side a quarter-mile off, and between, going down past where the light goes, a wall of layered rock and glass and darkness that breathes. Slowly. Out, warm, red-tinged, for the length of a long breath. Then in, cold. Along the lip in both directions, as far as you can see, figures standing, facing the gap. They do not turn round. They were not there a moment ago.

The Rent opened on the night of the Battle of Cinders, when the sorcery of eight armies met over the Cinder plain and something under the plain gave. It runs north-east to south-west across the heart of the Ashenreach, thirty-one miles long by the company's last survey and thirty-three by the Glassers', because it is growing. Nothing lives within five miles of it. The dead do.

### What is here
- **The lip**: glass for a hundred yards back from the edge, slick, cracked, and lined with the standing dead, who do not move while watched and are nearer every time you look away. They do not attack. They wait.
- **The Red Breath**: the chasm exhales every few minutes, warm, red-lit from below, and the Ember is said to brighten when it does.
- **The Lithic road**: a stepped way of grey stone cut into the chasm wall, climbing from the floor to the shelf beneath the Ninth Stone. Older than the chasm, which should be impossible. It is how [[The Last Wardens]] came up, once. From the lip above there is nothing so tidy: only the old company rope steps, and the red glass.
- [[The Ninth Stone]], at the northern end, visible for miles: a leaning finger of grey stone, cracked from crown to root, with the crack glowing faintly at night. Its top hundred feet stand above the lip; the other four hundred go down into the dark and stand on a pillar of their own.
- **The company's survey cairn**, at the western end where the Ash Road stops, with the survey tally scratched on a slate: 3003, 29 miles. 3005, 30. 3007, 30. 3009, 31. 3011, blank.

### Rumours
- The Rent is bottomless.
- The dead on the lip are everyone who died in the war, all of them, come to watch.
- Throw a coin in and you hear it land.
- The Rent will close if the lantern goes out, or open if it does; nobody agrees which.

:::gm
**Which are true.** 1. Nobody has found the bottom. The Lithic road goes a long way down. 2. There are not enough. There are more every red moon. 3. You do. After a very long time. And then you hear it picked up. 4. Nobody has tried it.
:::

### Things to do
Reach it: a day and a half's ride to the western end, and the Ninth Stone, at the north-eastern end, is two hard days' ride, three on foot or with a cart. Stand on the lip and breathe its breath. Count the dead and lose count. Find the old rope steps and go down a hundred of them, and feel the Ember through the rock. Reach the Ninth Stone's foot and meet the one who is waiting.

:::gm
The Rent is the Ninth Ward's crack made visible. The standing dead are its leakage: Hathe's door is broken and the Sleeper's dreams draw them here. They are harmless unless the crack widens (Let it crack), when they turn round. Session 6 brings the party here by the Ash Road or the Cold Seam; session 8 ends here under a full red moon. Every spell cast within a mile of the lip is felt by [[Warden Uthrem]] as a blow.
:::
