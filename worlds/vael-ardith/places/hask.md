Hask is forty turf houses on a shingle beach under the northern cliffs, facing the Grey Reach, which is the sea at the top of the world and looks it. The Hask fish cod and seal in boats their grandmothers built, drink something made of fermented whey, and bury their dead at sea because the ground is frozen eight months of the year.

Since 3002 the sea has been sending them back. Not often: a body a season, drifting in on the tide, sometimes one buried thirty years before, and standing up in the shallows with the patience of the very cold. A Raveler priest of dead [[Hathe]] came to Hask in 2981 for a single funeral and has never left; she walks out into the surf each time, says the door-words, and the dead lie down.

### Hook
The Raveler of Hask has written to the Rook-Court at Corvel Rise to say the tide-dead have started speaking before they lie down, and all of them say the same thing. She has not written it down. She says it should be heard, not read.

:::gm
The tide-dead say *the lamp is the lock*, the same five words the dying whisper to Quorl and the fused villagers breathe in the Glass Barrow. The Raveler's letter is one of the pieces [[Mother Corvane]] used to build four of the five words; the Rook-Court's clock "Last words decoded" ticks when it is read.
:::
