> A mile out on the Ash Road a wall rises on the right, whitewashed, the only white thing between here and the sky, and over it the tops of trees. Green trees. Through the gate a courtyard swept clean of ash, a fountain running, a long low house with a lamp burning in every window in daylight, and the smell of bread. A woman in a plain white robe with a saffron sash is kneeling by the fountain washing the feet of a kiln-hand with the dust-cough, and she looks up at you with a face that has been waiting for you specifically and is so glad you have come.

The mission came in 3008 with a letter of passage from every signatory, which was a feat in itself: Seraphine on foot, under a vow; the goods by cart; the cut stone by mule. It was founded on 14 Bloomtide, on the ruin of a farm where, the Iskavethi say, the camp healer Ilyra had her tent the night of the battle. They dug a well that gave sweet water where no well had, planted a garden that grew, and opened the gate. Bread for anyone, every morning. Healing for anyone, real healing, by the laying on of hands and the lighting of a lamp, and no charge, and no sermon unless you ask. Half the Smokes eats here once a week. The company's surgeon sends his worst cases up the road and they come back walking.

Nobody can say what is wrong with it. Everyone who has eaten the bread knows something is.

### What is here
- **The courtyard** and the fountain, where [[Seraphine Ashe]] receives whoever comes.
- **The Lamp Hall**, the chapel: a long bare room with one lamp on a plain altar and the walls lined with smaller ones, each lit for a petitioner. The Saint's face is not shown. Her hand is: a carving of an open hand, unburnt, in the fire.
- **The infirmary**, thirty beds, clean, warm, full.
- **The bath-house**, fed from the fountain, hot at all hours, which in Cinderford is a miracle on the level of the bread.
- **The garden**, behind the house, pears and figs and herbs in black soil brought from the plateau, and at the bottom of it a door in the wall onto the plain that is never locked. Sister Seraphine says the Saint's house has no locks. The lay-brothers say it is so the dead can come in and be comforted. Both are true.
- **The guest rooms**, four, better than anything in the Keep, offered to the Captain and declined, offered to [[Aelith Sorn]], who sleeps there most nights since the Church took notice of her.

### Who is here
Sister Seraphine, who wears a plain red robe on the road and white and saffron at home, and who is kind, beautiful in a way that is hard to look at and hard to stop looking at, and entirely sincere. Brother Caddis, who runs the kitchen and is the best cook in the March and knows it, and young [[Brother Iosune]], who carries the bread. The six Lamp-Wardens, quiet men and women in red surcoats on the road and white-and-gold tabards inside, who wear no swords inside the House; their blades hang in the gatehouse. The sick. Aelith Sorn, in the best guest room, being charming. At supper a lutenist plays, Halcyran wine is poured to anyone who sits, and the house does not ask for anything yet.

### Rumours
- The garden grows because the Saint blessed the soil.
- The Lamp-Wardens are soldiers.
- Seraphine can heal anything but the dust-cough.
- The mission is buying the company's debt from Tollamund.

:::gm
**Which are true.** 1. The soil is from the plateau. It would grow anywhere. The pears, though, are a different matter. 2. They are. Iskaveth's best. They are also devout. Any Warden called to trouble takes a longsword from the gatehouse first, so in session 4 they come armed. 3. She can heal that too. She has chosen, twice, not to, for people who refused the lamp. 4. False. It is offering to. Tollamund has not answered. Slowly.
:::

### Things to do
Eat the bread. Be healed, and lie awake afterwards. Light a lamp for someone and find you meant it. Sit in the garden at dusk and watch the unlocked door, and see who uses it. Be asked by Seraphine, with real warmth, what you dream, and find yourself telling her.

:::gm
The Saint speaks through Seraphine, not always and not on command; the players should notice the moments her eyes catch the lamp-light wrong. Seraphine's eyes are amber; they burn gold when Ilyra looks through her quietly (session 4) and go ember-red at the full Ember or in extremis (session 8). At every full Ember (16 Ashfall, 9 Leafrot) the House keeps the Saint's Lamp vigil: every window lit, the garden door open all night. The Church's clock, The Saint ascends, ticks here. The healing is real and draws on dead Hathe's stolen power, which is why the healed do not quite sleep. Seraphine wants the flame for the Saint and the PCs for the Saint's household, and she will offer each of them, in session 4, the thing they actually want. The garden door is never locked because the Saint wants the dead to come to her, and they do: at red moon the garden is full of them, standing among the pears, and Seraphine walks among them and they lean toward her like plants to a window.
:::

== GM notes ==

Seraphine is the campaign's most dangerous NPC because she is good. Play her warmth straight, give her no sneer, let her be right about the company's suffering and the town's hunger, and make the players argue with each other about her. Her allure is in attention: she listens like nobody else in the March. Brother Caddis is comic relief and a genuinely good man who has started to have doubts.
