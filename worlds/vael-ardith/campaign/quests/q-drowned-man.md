A poster went up in the Ford yesterday: *Wanted, [[Dannic Rook]], private of the Ninth Lantern, who walked out of the Keep with two tins of lantern oil and a grey blanket.* Ten silver to the Assessor's Office if he is brought in alive. [[Sergeant Vosk]] would like him brought in either way.

At noon the bell-ward strikes the [[The Treaty Bell|Treaty Bell]] with his mallet: three and three. Someone is in the water, and the ford-watch is running.

The dead don't always stay down since [[Hathe]] died. Everyone knows that. But they don't usually climb out of rivers with a purpose, either.

### What the party can find
- What Dannic said, if anyone can get it out of him.
- Where the river carried him: the old culvert under the ford, into the dark.
- Why a man who ran away with oil was in the river and not halfway to Sevenhaven.

:::gm
**The timeline.** [[Ansel Mote]] drowned Dannic under the third pier on the night of 7 Ashfall, when he walked out of the Keep for good (Nan Crupp saw a scarfed man and a splash from her hut door). Dannic was never buried. The current carried the body through the culvert into [[The Glass Barrow]], where the Sleeper's leak runs cold and the dead don't rest; in the cold it does not stay still. It carried his bundle down to the Cold Room, then came back up to stand in the shallows under the third pier at Ember-rise, facing the Keep. The ford-watch hook him out at noon on 14 Ashfall; the "two others" who stand with him are two nameless drowned of the Seventh, half-made, from the Flooded Street. His last words, to Quorl or to whoever kneels by him: **"It's cold. It's cold under the glass."**

The quest ends when the party finds his stash in the Barrow and [[The Cut Oil Tin]]. That hands them [[Who Is Poisoning the Flame?]].

**Finish.** He is laid on the Chapel slab only after session 1 and buried on Corvel Rise on the 15th without Hathe's door; if no proper rite is said (Quorl's, or the Door-Key in session 5), the ground will not keep him and he rises again at the session 3 full Ember. When he is put to rest, [[Spoons Aldane]] forgives the 11 silver.
:::
