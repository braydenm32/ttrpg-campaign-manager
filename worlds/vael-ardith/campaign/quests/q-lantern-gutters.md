The [[The Ninth Lantern|Ninth Lantern]] holds [[Cinderford]] under the [[The Treaty of Ashes]]. Its contract is carved over the hall door and read to every recruit: *"The company shall hold the Keep and the ford for the signatories, and shall stand down if the lantern ever goes out."* The lantern in its iron cage on the Keep's east wall has burned for nine years without a flicker.

Now the pay hasn't come, half the company is muttering, and the flame is not what it was. The party's job is the oldest one in soldiering: keep the light on, and find out who wants it dark.

### What the party knows
- The pay notice came with nothing in it again. The Regency has paid nothing since Hollowfrost 3009, and the company has stopped asking when.
- [[Brother Tallow]] tends [[The Lantern of the Ninth]] and says it has never once guttered.
- Everyone in town has an opinion about the lantern. Most of them want something from it.

:::gm
### The arc in one breath
The ninth lantern was lit from a shard of [[The Ninth Stone]], blown loose the night of Cinders and kicked into a cook-fire. Its flame is part of what holds the crack in the Ninth Ward shut. Nobody in the company knows. [[Brother Tallow]] suspects. Every faction in the region is about to find out, and every one of them wants the flame for something different. At the end, under a full red moon at [[The Rent]], the party decides its fate: **reseal** the ward, **let it crack**, or **use it**. See [[Ending: Reseal the Ward]], [[Ending: Let It Crack]] and [[Ending: Use It]].

### How the clues connect
1. **Session 1, Payday.** The pay notice reads 0 silver ([[Pay Notice]]). [[Dannic Rook]], a deserter, rises from the ford; his trail leads into [[The Glass Barrow]], where the party finds [[The Cut Oil Tin]]. Someone is poisoning the flame. The lantern gutters for the first time.
2. **Session 2, The Assessor.** [[Pell Quillon]] arrives to tax a company the Regency won't pay ([[Tax Notice, in Triplicate]]). The missing pay wagon leads to [[Fort Mercy]]. Inside the strongbox: Concord coin where Regency coin should be, and a loan-chit. [[Grandmother Tollamund]] advanced the pay in the company's name and booked it to the company, so the company owes the debt on money it never saw; the House's man had the Glassers hold it. [[Vexa Glassjaw]] trades [[A Glasser's Chart]] of the Rent.
3. **Session 3, Red Moon Rising.** The Ember comes full. Half the town dreams the same dream. [[The Sleepless]] march in [[The Smokes]], led by [[The Dreaming Widow]], Tick's aunt. The lantern gutters again and [[Brother Tallow]] collapses. [[Ysse Lampwright]] has to tend it.
4. **Session 4, The House of Unburnt Light.** [[Seraphine Ashe]] invites the party ([[A Perfumed Letter]]) and offers to "save" the flame by carrying it to Halcyra. At [[The Velvet Ledger]], [[Sorrel Vey]]'s ink and secrets show what the elves came east to read, and who wants the crack open.
5. **Session 5, Last Words.** [[Mother Corvane]]'s ledger of last words spells out "the lamp is the lock". The sap of [[The Weeping Orchard]] and a dying veteran's confession reveal where the lantern was really lit: from a stone shard, in a cook-fire, by the deserters nobody counted.
6. **Session 6, Into the Rent.** Using the Glasser's chart, the party reaches [[The Ninth Stone]] and [[Warden Uthrem]]. He tells the truth of the Godslaying and asks for a new heart for the ward. [[A Rubbing from the Ninth Stone]] can be taken. Borrin's shadow acts on its own.
7. **Session 7, Three Knives.** [[The Quiet Office]] (through [[Ansel Mote]]), [[The Concord Houses]] (through Tollamund) and [[The Church of the Unburnt Saint]] (through Seraphine) all move on the lantern in the same week. [[Garrick Sallow]]'s mutiny breaks open.
8. **Session 8, The Lantern Gutters.** The last night, under a full red moon, at the Rent.

### Who is poisoning the flame (the answer)
Marius Vell's Quiet Office pays [[Ansel Mote]], who buys sealed Keel tins at Hesk's Chandlery, cuts them with lake-ash from [[Stillwater]] (supplied by the Dreaming Widow's people) and reseals them in Hesk's own grey wax. [[Garrick Sallow]] pays [[Dannic Rook]] to swap them for the Keep's clean tins in the oil store, from about 18 Reaping to 7 Ashfall. When Dannic walked out for good, Mote drowned him under the third pier. [[Spoons Aldane]] saw Sallow and Dannic at the oil store twice and said nothing, because he holds the biggest pool in the company on the night the lantern goes out. [[Fennick Bottlebrook]] is the red herring: he sold four casks of good oil to Kettle for bread and wrote them up as burned.

### The clocks
Keep [[The Ninth Lantern]]'s Mutiny clock (starts at 2), the Quiet Office clock, the Concord's Debt clock, the Saint's clock and the Sleeper's world clock on [[The Sleeper Below]] moving between sessions. The Sleeper starts at 3 of 12 (one for each failing ward) and ticks +1 at each full Ember (sessions 3 and 8), so left alone it reaches 5 by session 8. To speed it up (the Lantern going out, spells near the Stone, the Choir, the Sleepless), see "Speeding it up" on [[The Sleeper Below]]. At 6 the dream reaches the whole March; at 9 the Ashenreach dead rise unaided; at 12 it speaks through whoever holds the flame.

== GM notes ==

This is the spine page. Read the arc above once, then run session by session from the Sessions list. Every session's prep has a strong start, scenes, clues and fights ready.

If the party wanders, let them. The factions keep moving whether the PCs are watching or not: tick their clocks, and let the consequences arrive at the Keep's gate.
