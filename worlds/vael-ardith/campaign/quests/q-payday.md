The pay notice came up the road from the Regency with its seal and its signatures and, in a clerk's tidy hand, the sum of **0 silver**. The wagon that should have followed it never arrived. Tollamund's clerk had told the captain the Concord's Treaty share was coming on it.

[[Ilse Marrow|Captain Marrow]] wants the wagon found, or at least wants to know who to be angry at. [[Fennick Bottlebrook]] wants it found before the bakers stop giving credit. [[Garrick Sallow]] wants everyone to keep talking about it.

### Leads
- The pay notice itself: a real seal, a real signature, a very real zero.
- The wagon's escort was paid off at the waystation two days west, on the Fallowden road. After that nobody admits to seeing it. The toll-log in the gatehouse at the bridge-head, where [[Mistress Hebbe]] writes down every cart that crosses, might.
- The barge-folk on [[The Low Quay]] hear everything that moves on the Cinder and most things that move on the road.

:::gm
The Regency has paid nothing since 1 Hollowfrost 3009. In Ashfall Tollamund advanced 600 gold in new Sevenhaven coin in the company's name, under the third line of Marrow's note, and sent it under an old Regency pay seal. The wagon left Fallowden on 2 Ashfall; its Concord-hired escort was paid off two days west of Cinderford on the 4th; her drivers brought it into the mill-yard at the Ford on the night of the 6th; at dawn on the 7th it crossed the bridge as a sealed House cart (Hebbe's log: "House cart, sealed, east. 5 cp", paid in Concord silver) and turned off at the Burned Mill for Fort Mercy (Nance Oddy's Toll at [[The Burned Mill]] is the written witness for the turn-off; Ferrow Gant at Tally's Cut saw the cart on the track at dusk), where Cousin Pennamund had Vexa hold it "until collected". The note now stands at 4,800 gold.

The strongbox sits unopened in Fort Mercy's cellar behind the loose corner of the Lithic panel until the party recovers it in session 2. Inside: 600 gold in new Sevenhaven coin, house-marked, and a loan-chit ([[The Concord Loan-Chit]]; the Concord's copy of the advance) naming the Ninth Lantern debtor "for the full sum, at interest". Other Concord coin in play is not from the box: see the encounters and salvage tables.

Clue for session 1: Fennick mentions that Tollamund's clerk asked, very politely, when the pay was due to arrive. Banks don't ask that unless they already know.

**Outcomes.**
- Spend it on wages: Mutiny -1, Debt +1 (it is House money, and the House will say so).
- Pay the 480 interest: Debt -1, Mutiny +1, and Tollamund still comes on 5 Leafrot, calling the box stolen House property.
- Leave it with Vexa: Glasser goodwill, and a company that eats nothing.
:::
