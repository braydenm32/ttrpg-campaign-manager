The Vesh lamp-folios [[Ysse Lampwright]] carried from her order hold a few lines: when the flame chokes, feed it the tears of the stone trees. There is only one stone orchard in the Ashenreach. [[The Weeping Orchard]] stands two miles north-east, up the orchard lane, every tree turned to grey stone. At the full Ember the whole orchard weeps; between fulls every bough keeps beads of set sap, and the oldest tree trickles a vial a night.

Tallow can't make the walk. Someone has to go, at night, under the Ember, and bring back a flask.

:::gm
The orchard was a Lithic grove in the days of [[Ul-Tarran]]; its "sap" is the slow life of the stone, the same stuff the Lithic become. It does clear the ash from the flame. It also shows that the flame and the stone are kin: dropped on [[The Lantern of the Ninth]], the sap makes it burn tall and white, and for a moment Ysse sees a shape in it like a standing figure.

Complications on the night: Sleepless pilgrims come to drink the sap for dreams (a cult fanatic and 4 commoners); the trees whisper Sleeper dreams (roll on [[Sleeper Dreams]]); one tree has a soldier's face in its bark. Collecting enough takes an hour (three successes before two failures: Survival, Nature or Medicine DC 12).

This side quest is a good way into session 5's "where was the lantern lit" clue: the cooks' camp fire-pit is still there under the oldest tree: glass in it, and a soup-pot ring burnt into the stone.
:::
