**TOLLAMUND & DAUGHTERS**
*of Sevenhaven, and at the Ford, Cinderford*
*Counting-house copy. To travel with the sum.*

**MEMORANDUM OF ADVANCE**

Under the standing credit of the Company of the Ninth Lantern with this House, dated Thawmere 3009 and signed for the Company by I. Marrow, Captain, and F. Bottlebrook, Quartermaster:

| | |
|---|---|
| Advanced, under the **third line** of the said credit, for the upkeep of the Company and at the House's discretion | **600 gold**, in silver of the Sevenhaven mint, this year's striking, house-marked |
| Booked to the Company as debtor **for the full sum** | 600 gold |
| Interest, as in the credit | **a tenth in the quarter**, from the quarter-day of Ashfall |
| The credit now standing, with this advance and interest to date | **4,800 gold** |
| Security | *as in the credit: the Company's possessions, contracts and standing, including the lantern* |

The sum to travel from the House's table at Fallowden under the Regency's pay seal, for the convenience of the road, and to be delivered to the Company at the Lantern Keep by the sixth day of Ashfall, or held at the House's direction.

*Given at the Ford on the twentieth day of Reaping, 3011 AW.*
*For the House: Perrin, by the Grandmother's hand.*
*Audited: O. Pennamund.* (This last line is in a different, faster ink, and has been scored through once and initialled again.)

:::gm
**When to show it.** Inside the strongbox in Fort Mercy's cellar, under the coin, in session 2. If the party never opens the box, Pennamund or Perrin can produce the House's own copy in session 7.

**What it reveals.** The whole shape of the debt in one half-sheet. The pay wagon was never Regency pay: it was Tollamund's own silver, lent to the company in the company's name under a clause Marrow signed when the company was starving, and booked at interest before a coin of it was spent. The note stands at 4,800 gold (see [[The Concord Promissory Note]]), and the quarter's interest of 480 fell due on the 14th. "Including the lantern" is the clause [[Grandmother Tollamund]] reads aloud at the Keep gate in session 7. The Regency seal on the box was cover, and the chit says so in the politest possible way.

Fennick, shown it, goes white: "They've lent us our own wages and charged us for the loan." He is right. If the party spends the 600 on wages anyway, the Mutiny clock rolls back one and the Debt clock ticks one: it is House money, and the House will say so.

Pennamund's scored-through initial is a clue to his own doubts: he audited the advance and did not like it. Perrin, who wrote the chit, is his source inside the house (see [[The Tollamund Daughters]]).

Hebbe's toll-log is the only independent record of the silver that paid it (see [[Mistress Hebbe]]); the thirteenth barrels at [[Sedgemere]] are where the House's working coin travels (see [[The Unmarked Barrel]]).
:::
