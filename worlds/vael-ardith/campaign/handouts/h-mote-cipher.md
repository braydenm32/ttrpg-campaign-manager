*(The front is Form 9-K, Declaration of Wages Owed But Not Payable, Countersigned, blank except for the Regency counter-seal in red wax. The back is ruled in pencil into five rows of columns, like a tax table, each column filled with a figure in a small, neat, upright hand. The columns are of different widths, and the widths are not for the figures in them.)*

| Row | What a tax clerk sees |
|---|---|
| 1 | Seven columns. "Hearth, by quarter." Figures that add up. |
| 2 | Twenty-two columns. "Lamps, by night, carried." Figures that nearly add up. |
| 3 | Forty-six columns. "Garrison, by file, with arrears deferred." Figures that do not add up and were not meant to. |
| 4 | Twenty-one columns. "Fees." All twelves. |
| 5 | Thirty-eight columns, added later in a slightly different ink. "Sundries." |

*(To a tax clerk it is a bad tax table. To a cipher-reader each column's width, in the ruled spaces of the form, is a letter.)*

:::gm
**Reading it.** Intelligence DC 16 with a week and a dictionary, or Ysse with an hour (she reads lamp-wick tallies the same way), or a wizard, gets **one row per attempt**; Pell, shown the form, recognises the column rule at once ("That's a Revenue table. Nobody rules a Revenue table like that") and gets a row a day. The five rows read:

1. *S. paid.*
2. *Oil done by the red moon.*
3. *Lamp to be taken under Levy default; escort at Ardmere.*
4. *Assessor knows nothing.*
5. *The girl at the Ledger is the one. Hold her for the Chair.*

**What it reveals.** Row 1: [[Garrick Sallow]] is on the Quiet Office's books. Row 2: the oil-cutting has a deadline, the full Ember of 16 Ashfall; after that the flame should be low enough to snuff. Row 3: the Regency means to seize the lantern legally, under the unpaid lamp tax ([[Tax Notice, in Triplicate]]), and an escort is already waiting at Ardmere to carry it to the capital. Row 4: [[Pell Quillon]] is honest, and the Office knows it, and is using him as cover. Row 5, if read, is the campaign's quietest knife: [[Tesh]] at the Velvet Ledger carries a thin line of imperial blood, and Marius Vell wants her held for his council. Nobody in Cinderford knows this but Mote.

Returning the form to Pell makes him an ally for life ([[The Assessor's Lost Form]]). Reading its back first makes him a frightened one. If Mote learns the form is gone before session 7, he moves early: tick the Quiet Office clock.
:::
