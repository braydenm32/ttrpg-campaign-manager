*The gatehouse board is where Cinderford reads. The duty sergeant keeps the wanted bills on it; the Reeve pins her jobs there because the company's gate is the one door everyone in town goes past; and everyone else uses the space that is left. On the morning of 14 Ashfall 3011 it holds, top to bottom, left to right:*

---

**OFFICE OF REVENUE for the MARCH OF CINDERS**
*Fourth Assistant Under-Collector's Office, above the cooper's, Ford Street*
Open the second bell to the fifth, Coinday to Wardday. Closed Tideday afternoon for reconciliation.
Enquiries to Mrs Pruitt. Forms are not souvenirs. The Assessor is expected.
*(A neat clerk's hand. Underneath, in charcoal: HE'S EXPECTED ALL RIGHT)*

---

**THE REEVE'S BOARD.** *Four jobs, at the Reeve's rates. The Reeve pays on Coinday. The Reeve pays.*
1. A child of the Smokes who walks toward the Rent in the night and keeps doing it. Her mother says she was sleep-walking. Two silver to learn why and bring her home; the mother's name from Mother Brisk.
2. A cellar on Tanners' Lane flooded with water so cold it has a skin of ice in Ashfall. One silver to look. Three if you make it stop.
3. A quarrel on the Low Quay between Kettle Brindlewake's cousins and a Glasser over a crate nobody will name. One silver to settle it before I have to.
4. A coin that rang wrong in Mistress Hebbe's hand this month. Five silver to tell me where it came from, and I mean where.
*O. Fairweather, Reeve.*

---

**WANTED for DESERTION and THEFT of COMPANY STORES: DANNIC ROOK.** *(A second copy of the bill from the Candle, the same bad drawing, the same A.M.; see [[Wanted: Dannic Rook]]. Somebody has drawn a fish in the margin.)*

---

**ODDS. S. Aldane, corner table, the Candle. Book is honest.**
The night the lantern goes out, any named night this month: 40 to 1.
Frostcall, any night: 14 to 1.
Lt Sallow captain by Longnight: 3 to 1.
The Reeve's hat tomorrow: grey, 2 to 1; green, 5 to 1; the one with the feather, 20 to 1.
Old Tallow to the privy unaided: evens.
The crow talks at red moon: 50 to 1, and I'm being generous.
NEVER: **book closed.** Don't ask.

---

**OIL.** Keel, sealed, bell-stamped, the wax unbroken. Will pay fair, in kind. Ask for B. Tallow at the oil-room, dawn. *Not cut. I will know.*

---

**NO CREDIT.** ESPECIALLY YOU. *H. Varro, chandler.*

---

**LOST:** one company blanket, grey, issue, marked with the cage. Last seen the 7th. Return to the Quartermaster. No questions. Some questions. *F.B.*

---

*(A card, printed, with a lamp on it:)* **THE HOUSE OF UNBURNT LIGHT**, one mile out on the Ash Road, south side. The infirmary is open to all and asks nothing. Walk the coals with us on the Saint's Lamp, the 16th, and be unburnt. Bread after. *The garden door is never locked.*

---

**CHARTS to the glass.** The Barrow, the Fort, the Cut, the lip. Two silver. True as far as I went. Ask for Skelt, Gutter Market, Coinday. *(Somebody has added: HE DIDN'T GO FAR)*

---

*(A board of its own, carved, with a row of peg-holes:)* **STAKE DAY, 7 SEEDWAKE. Work-party and cart. Pegs by Longnight.** 3008: twelve pegs. 3009: twelve pegs. 3010: four pegs, and the names scratched out. 3011: no pegs.

---

**LAST WORDS TAKEN.** Rates on application. Discretion. *Skrell, at the Chapel, any hour. We are usually awake.*

---

**TOLLAMUND & DAUGHTERS** reminds the town that rents and interest fall due on the 15th. The House is patient. The House is not forgetful.

---

*(The company's own pay notice, nailed up by the Captain's order with the total circled twice: see [[Pay Notice]]. Under the 0, in six hands: NICE. / SPEND IT WISELY. / I'M RICH. / arrears / same as last time / and the ninth year too, you bastards)*

---

*(In the bottom corner, in chalk, an open eye behind a closed lid, the size of a hand. Someone has tried to rub it out and someone has drawn it again.)*

---

*(Pinned over the eye, on a torn scrap, in a hand that is not a clerk's:)* **FOUND: a shadow.** Apply within.

:::gm
**What each notice opens.** The Office hours point at [[The Assessor's Office]] and Widow Pruitt, who paid the printer for the wanted bill on the 13th and will say so if asked the right way; the charcoal is the town's opinion of the Regency. The Reeve's four jobs are hooks with a fee: the child is Hollowborn and walking toward the Rent because the dream is getting clearer ([[The Sleepless]], the Smokes); the cold cellar sits over the Glass Barrow's edge and is a second way in; the Quay quarrel is over the warm thirteenth crate ([[Kettle's Cargo]]); the coin that rang wrong is a lead-cored silver passed on Hearthday last by a Low Quay fish-seller, one of the House's working coin from the thirteenth barrel ([[The Unmarked Barrel]]); the fish-seller does not know who gave it to him. (Sallow's new Regency silver is a different trail: [[Where is Lieutenant Sallow getting his money?]]) Spoons's sheet is [[Spoons Aldane]]'s pool in public: the "fat stack on before the red moon's gone" is not on the board because the book is private, but **NEVER: book closed** is Keld's wager ([[The Wager on Never]]), and a PC who asks him why gets the quest. Tallow's notice tells anyone reading that the lamp-keeper no longer trusts his own oil store ([[Who Is Poisoning the Flame?]]). Hesk's is Hesk. Fennick's blanket is Dannic's: Dannic walked out of the Keep on the night of the 7th with a grey company blanket, and it is now the bundle in the Cold Room; Fennick posted this on the 8th, before anyone knew Dannic was dead, and will go grey if asked why the Quartermaster wanted it back without questions (he did not; he wanted the tins, and did not dare write that). The Church's card is the House's open door ([[The House of Unburnt Light]]) and the coal-walk on the 16th is session 3's night. Skelt's charts are false copies of Pike's; the man who added HE DIDN'T GO FAR is Mardle Ock. The Stake Day board is the company's decline in pegs; a party that fills it in the epilogue has said something. Skrell's card is the Rook-Court's. Tollamund's is the only notice on the board that is entirely true. The chalk eye is the Sleepless marking the gate itself. **FOUND: a shadow** is [[Gerda Hollin]]'s, pinned the morning after the first muster, about [[Borrin Halt]], and she does not think it is funny; she wants him to come and talk to her.

**Updating the board.** Session 2: the Tax Notice goes up over the pay notice, nailed by Pell with Mote holding the nails, and Spoons's odds on "the Assessor lasts the month" appear at 2 to 1. Session 3: the Church's card is torn in half and pinned back together. Session 4: Hesk's notice has NO SLEEPLESS added. Session 7: the Concord's lawyers pin a writ, and somebody pins a strand of rope through it.
:::

== GM notes ==

Read it aloud fast and let the players stop you. Every player will want a different notice; give each one its hook and move on. The board is also where the players can leave things: a PC who pins a notice of their own has it answered by the next session, by someone.
