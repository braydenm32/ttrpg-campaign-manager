*(Pencil, in a large careful hand that learned its letters late. The paper is the torn-off corner of a Ninth Lantern ration-chit; the printed word RATION shows on the back, upside down.)*

S. says one more swap and Im square.
Then the clerk wants a word.
Dont like the clerk.

*(Below, smaller, pressed hard enough to tear the paper:)* D.R.

:::gm
**When to show it.** Session 1, scene 6, in the grey blanket bundle in the ring round the Cold Captain's chair, with the purse of twelve new Regency silver and the 3009 pay-chit stamped PAID IN FULL. Dannic wrote it on the night of 3 Ashfall, sitting on the Cold Stair landing, before Skelt took the rope back up.

**What it reveals.** Three clues, one a line. "S." is [[Garrick Sallow]], who was paying Dannic to swap the oil tins; "square" is the eleven silver he owed Spoons and the rest he owed Kettle. "The clerk" is [[Ansel Mote]], whom Dannic had met once on the bridge and did not like: the party has already seen the clerk's initials on the wanted bill and will meet him at the Keep gate in session 2. "Dont like the clerk" is the last judgement Dannic made, and it was right; Mote drowned him four nights later.

The hand is Dannic's: Spoons, Fennick or Quorl recognise it. Compare it with the chalk on the Cold Room wall, *D.R. 3 Ashfall. Back for the rest.*
:::
