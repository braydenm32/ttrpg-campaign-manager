**The question.** Who swapped the lantern's oil for tins cut with ash, and why?

**What the GM knows.** Marius Vell's Quiet Office pays [[Ansel Mote]], who buys sealed Keel tins at [[Hesk's Chandlery]] through "a clerk with a cold" (paid in advance; it is in Hesk's order-book), cuts them with lake-ash from [[Stillwater]] supplied by [[The Dreaming Widow]]'s people, and reseals them with Hesk's own grey wax. Mote gives them to [[Garrick Sallow]], who pays [[Dannic Rook]] to swap them into the oil store (about 18 Reaping to 7 Ashfall); [[Brother Tallow]] pours the dawn measure from the store into the oil-port. Mote drowned Dannic when he walked out. [[Spoons Aldane]] saw the swap twice and keeps quiet to protect his betting pool; he saw who resealed the oil. Hesk did not cut it. [[Fennick Bottlebrook]] is the red herring: four casks sold to Kettle for bread, written up as burned.

**Where the answer lies.** [[The Cut Oil Tin]] (the ash smells of lake water; the wax is a moth); Hesk's order-book; Sallow's mint-fresh Regency silver; Hennet's slate and the new coin in her cup; Spoons' book; the cipher on the back of the Assessor's lost form; the corkboard "Who's poisoning the flame?".

**Payoff.** Sallow and Spoons in session 3 or 4; Mote in session 7.
