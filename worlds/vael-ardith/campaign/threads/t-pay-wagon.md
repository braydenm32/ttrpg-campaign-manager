**The question.** If the Regency's notice says 0 silver, what was the wagon carrying, and where is it?

**What the GM knows.** 600 gold in new Sevenhaven coin, advanced in the company's name by [[Grandmother Tollamund]] under the third line of Marrow's note (the note now stands at 4,800), and sent under an old Regency pay seal as cover. Route: Fallowden 2 Ashfall; escort paid off two days west on the 4th; Tollamund's own drivers into her mill-yard at the Ford, night of the 6th; dawn on the 7th, across the bridge as a House cart (Mistress Hebbe's log, 5 cp in Concord silver) and off the Ash Road at the Burned Mill to [[Fort Mercy]], where Cousin Pennamund told [[Vexa Glassjaw]] to hold it "until collected". The strongbox sits unopened in the cellar behind the loose corner of the Lithic panel. The empty wagon was burnt at the Burned Mill turn-off.

**Where the answer lies.** Mistress Hebbe's toll-log at the bridge-head; [[Kettle Brindlewake]] (the route); the Three Cups in Fallowden (the escort sergeant spent his Concord pay there); wagon ruts in the ash (Survival DC 12, before a greywind covers them); Fort Mercy.

**Payoff.** Session 2.
