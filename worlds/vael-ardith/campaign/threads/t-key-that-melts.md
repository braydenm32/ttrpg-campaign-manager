**The question.** At [[Hollowhall]] a Returned smith forges a key, hand-long, in a pattern nobody recognises, finishes it, melts it down and starts again. He says he does not know why. The pattern matches the keyhole in the grey Lithic door at the end of the Red Seam under Cinderford, in [[The Glass Barrow]], which nobody from Hollowhall has ever seen. It also matches [[Hathe's Door-Key]], which the Rook-Court lends for funerals and which opens doors the dead have closed. What is the smith remembering, and what is the door?

**What the GM knows.** The smith is [[Durn Mallow Came-Back]], and the key he cannot stop making is the key to the door at the bottom of the Sealed Stair under Hollowhall, which the Returned shut from below when they came up in 3004 and which the thing that took their shadows gave them the shape of, so that they could lock it, and so that they could never forget it. The Lithic doors on the Nine Road under the March (the Barrow's Red Seam, the Cold Seam landing, the root of the Ninth Stone) were shut by the standing dead, which is why the key of the dead turns them; the Eighth's door was shut by something else, with the same key, and that is the thing the Returned do not say. [[Borrin Halt]] went under three and a half years ago with a Skarn deep-road party looking for kin who did not come up with the first Returned, and came up alone six months ago with a shadow that is not his, and the shadow is a door-warden of the Eighth Ward that wants to see what the Ninth does ([[Why does Borrin's shadow lag?]]). Tam Hollowell, who sits by the Candle fire with no shadow and sets a place at his table for himself, was one of Borrin's party and came up earlier, and cannot say where the others are. Durn's key, finished and not melted, would open the Red Seam door in the Barrow from this side; what it would open from the other side is the second campaign.

**Where the answer lies.** Durn Mallow at the Underlook Forge, if anyone from Cinderford writes to him or walks north. [[Warden Uthrem]], if he lives past session 8: the Last Wardens know what sealed the Eighth, and he will say it in a sentence, slowly, if asked by someone who has stood with him. Hold-Mother [[Greta Holm]], who read the page of Skarn's Book of Grudges for the winter of 3004 before Clan Harl's tallyman tore it out, and knows what it said. Tam Hollowell, who will leave town if he sees Borrin's shadow move. The Red Seam door itself, opened with Hathe's key or Durn's. The Cold Seam under Fort Mercy, which is a road, and Ilbert Crane's drawings of where it goes. The Sealed Stair, sealed from below, and the question of who is on the far side holding the key's twin.

**By ending.** *Reseal:* Borrin's shadow bows to the Stone, goes home under the mountain, and the night the seal holds Durn finishes a key and does not melt it; he does not know why that either. *Use it:* the shadow stays, and grows bold, and the smith's keys start coming out of the forge with a second ward's mark on the bow. *Let it crack:* the Eighth opens too, from below, and the Returned in Cinderford go north in a body without being told.

**Payoff.** The first session of the campaign after this one: a key that does not melt, a stair that is no longer sealed, and the Grimmark's Eighth Ward with nothing standing at it.
