**The question.** Six children were born in Cinderford on the night of [[The Battle of Cinders]], [[Tibbet Marlowe]] among them. All six grew from cradle to grown in about seven years. Every [[The Hollowborn|Hollowborn]] child born since has aged like anyone else. Why those six, and why that night?

:::gm
**What the GM knows.** They were born in the hours the Ninth Ward cracked and [[The Ember]] rose, the night [[The Sleeper Below]] dreamed hardest, and the dream got into them before their first breath. They did not grow evenly. They grew in steps, one at each full red moon, the dream drawing them up the way a lamp draws seedlings, and they stopped when they were grown enough to be of use. They are the first adult Hollowborn, and so the first who will hear the Sleeper in daylight: it is raising its mouths early. [[The Dreaming Widow]] calls Tick its firstborn and is not wholly wrong.
:::

**Where the answer lies.** The birth-rolls at the Maelis shrine on [[Temple Row]]: six names dated the night of Cinders, with heights that climb by the red moon. [[Ottilie Marlowe]]'s cellar door, where she measured all six. Tick's own memory of the marks. The Sleeper's dreams at each full Ember. [[Warden Uthrem]], if anyone thinks to ask him. [[Mother Brisk]] keeps the rolls in a chest under the kettle-shelf.

**Payoff.** Session 3, when the red moon pulls hardest on the Cinders-born; session 6, when Uthrem says, in his slow out-breaths, that those born in the hour a ward breaks are carried by it for a while, and must be let walk in the light; session 8, at the Rent.

== GM notes ==

How it ties to the Sleeper and the Ember: the growth came in steps at each full Ember, so the Cinders-born are a living record of the Sleeper's dream getting stronger. Each full Ember that ticks the Sleeper's clock pulls a little harder on all six, and hardest on Tick, because he stands closest to the flame. Let the players put it together from the clues (the rolls, Ottilie's door, Tick's marks) rather than telling them. If the ward is resealed, the pull stops and the six simply go on being young adults with very short childhoods. If it cracks or the flame is used, they are the first to speak with the Sleeper's voice.
