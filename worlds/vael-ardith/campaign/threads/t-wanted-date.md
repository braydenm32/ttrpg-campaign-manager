**The question.** The poster on the wall of the Last Candle says Dannic Rook was "last seen on the Low Quay, 12th Ashfall". By the state of him, he'd been in the river since the 7th, and the poster's own description gets him wrong.

**What the GM knows.** [[Ansel Mote]] wrote the poster (as clerk of [[The Assessor's Office]], signed "A.M.") and invented a sighting, so the company would blame a living, runaway Dannic for the oil and nobody would go looking for a body. He didn't count on the body coming back on its own. The description is Mote's guesswork, because he never saw Dannic alive: he's twenty-four and had both ears, and talked like a Corvel Rise foundling. Pruitt or Hennet will say so.

**Where the answer lies.** [[Wanted: Dannic Rook]] handout; [[Ottun Fray]], the jobbing printer on Temple Row, who set it and kept the copy-text in Mote's own hand on the spike behind his press (paid on the 13th by [[Hester Pruitt]], who knows exactly who told her to); [[Kettle Brindlewake]], who was on the Low Quay all day on the 12th and saw no Dannic; [[Keld Owes-Nothing]]'s slate at [[Sedgemere]], which records cold water with glass in it on 8 and 13 Ashfall: the Barrow's breath, and the proof the body was in the water on the 8th.

**Payoff.** Session 2, when the party meets Mote and sees his handwriting.
