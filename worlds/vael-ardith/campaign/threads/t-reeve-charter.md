**The question.** [[Odile Fairweather]] is visiting [[Tollamund & Daughters]] late at night. What is the town's Reeve buying, or selling?

**What the GM knows.** The town's charter. She has agreed to sell it to [[The Concord Houses]] (fine print: the Keep and "all fixtures and fittings"), so that Cinderford has a master who pays its bills. She signs it between sessions 3 and 7 but has not sealed it; in session 7 she seals it or tears it up. She thinks it's the only way the town outlives the company. She might be right.

**Where the answer lies.** Her letter-case; Tollamund, who never lies but answers only the question asked; the bank's daughters, who are three hundred years younger and gossip.

**Payoff.** Session 7, when the Concord calls in its debt and the Reeve has to stand on one side or the other.
