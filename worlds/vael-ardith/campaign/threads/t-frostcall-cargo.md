**The question.** A factor in [[Cowl]] has been asking the barge clans to quote for "a sealed cargo, man-sized, not to be opened, from the ash country to the Bight, Frostcall delivery". The pay is very good. The Brindlewakes turned it down. Who is it for, and what is in it?

**What the GM knows.** As the Cowl hook is written, the cargo is a Lithic statue fragment bound for [[Aelith Sorn]], who wants it out of the March before the Church takes closer notice of what she collects; the factor is paid from a Remembrancer's purse. If nothing in the campaign changes that, that is the answer. After session 8 the quote is asked again, in the same words and at a better price, and **who is asking depends on how it ended**.

- **Reseal.** If Uthrem gave his heart, he stands now on the Warden's Shelf as a stone figure the size of a man, and the Bench of [[Corvex]], which already pays a Lithic family to keep the Fifth Ward's "bought heart", would like to own the proof that a ward can be kept by a bought warden. The factor is the Bench's.
- **Let it crack.** If [[Seraphine Ashe]] fell at the Stone, the Church wants her carried home to the Lamp Court by water and would rather not use its own roads.
- **Use it.** The one who holds the shard is the cargo: alive, in a crate with air-holes, bound for Gilt at [[Tollamund & Daughters]]' own invitation, and has been told it is a courtesy.

**Where the answer lies.** The factor's letter (copies are in the post-stair box, not that anyone should look); [[Barge-Mother Posy Reedmarsh]], who is asked on 10 Leafrot and will tell the party; the Cowl Smugglers' Bench; the Gilt vault clerks, who report a delivery of "heavy, cold and humming" every month and have started to say "this month, a man."

**First beat.** 10 Leafrot, the morning after session 8: a bargeling arrives at Sedgemere with a letter for Posy. If the party is within reach, Posy sends it on to them unopened.

**Payoff.** The Tollamund & Daughters vault at [[Gilt]], by Frostcall.
