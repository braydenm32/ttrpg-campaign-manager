**The question.** [[Seraphine Ashe]] could court the Reeve, the captain, the bank. Why is she sending perfumed letters to the newest recruits?

**What the GM knows.** [[Ilyra of Halcyra]] looks through her when she is quiet (Seraphine's amber eyes burn gold), and the Saint wants the PCs: they are the ones the flame is starting to notice, and a saint who becomes a god will need people who have stood close to a ward. The pull is real. Each PC who spends a night at [[The House of Unburnt Light]] wakes warm and rested and knowing exactly what they want, and it is always, a little, the Saint.

**Where the answer lies.** [[A Perfumed Letter]]; [[Sorrel Vey]], who knows the Saint's methods because they're her goddess's methods, done badly; [[The Church of the Unburnt Saint]]'s clock.

**Payoff.** Session 4, and again in session 8, when the Saint's priests urge the crack. In session 4 Sorrel tells Seraphine to her face, once and on purpose, that she is Marrow's lover.
