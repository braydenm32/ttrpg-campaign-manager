**The question.** The company has eaten all year without wages. Where did the money come from?

**What the GM knows.** [[Ilse Marrow]] paid the company from her own savings from Reaping 3010 until they ran out in Reaping 3011. She told no one. Fennick noticed by arithmetic and has hidden it in his books so nobody would feel like charity. She is now broke. [[Sorrel Vey]], her lover, offered money and was refused twice; since then she has paid the company's small debts through an anonymous "friend of the company" credit that turns up in Fennick's books every Coinday. Marrow does not know.

**Where the answer lies.** [[Fennick Bottlebrook]]'s second ledger; Marrow's strongbox, which holds one gold piece and a letter; the Velvet Ledger's accounts (session 4); and Mother Hennet's slate-book, whose quarter-day headings from Ashfall 3010 to Highsun 3011 read "Paid from the Keep. A part. Old imperial silver, the same worn coin every time" (see [[The Price Was an Arm]]).

**Payoff.** Session 2 or 4. When the company learns, the Mutiny clock loses two segments: nobody mutinies against the woman who fed them.
