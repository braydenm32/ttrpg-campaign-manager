**The question.** [[Ysse Lampwright]] can feel the flame failing, and it talks to her at night. Who is talking?

**What the GM knows.** Two voices, and Ysse can't yet tell them apart. One is slow and warm and heavy as a hillside: the Ninth of [[The Nine]], awake in the cracked stone, asking for a heart. Under it, sweeter and colder, is [[The Sleeper Below]], wearing the first voice like a coat and saying how much easier it would be just to *hold* the flame. Warm voice: "Stand with me." Cold voice: "Keep me." Give her one line from each, every session. As run: S1 "Hold." S2 "Closer." S3 "Stand with me" and "Keep me". S4, the other voice: "Somewhere warm. Keep me somewhere warm." S5 "Home." S6 "You came" and "There you are". S7 "Not yet" and "Keep me". S8, aloud: "Keep me."

**Where the answer lies.** [[Brother Tallow]], who heard a voice once himself, decades ago; [[Warden Uthrem]], who has waited nine years for her.

**Payoff.** Session 6 (Uthrem names the warm voice) and session 8 (which voice she answers).
