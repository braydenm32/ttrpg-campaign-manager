**The question.** In the weeks after session 8, a Lector of the Solar Curia rides into Cinderford in black edged with gold with a sealed paper: a pardon list, "in anticipation of Crownfast", for the deserters of the Battle of Cinders, nine years late. Pardons are the Curia's gift on 15 Highsun, by the dozen; why has one come early, in winter, to a town the Regency has not paid in two years, and why are these the names on it?

**What the GM knows.** The names are: *Halloran Pike* (dead since 24 Ashfall, which the Curia does not know); *Aurelio Bask*, who deserted Greywake's Spears and sews on Ford Street; *Hesper Hennet*, who never deserted anything, was the cook of a company that died on the wall, and takes being pardoned for it as the worst insult the Regency has offered her yet; and *Tesh, of Cinderford, of the blood*, who was a child in 3002 and never stood in a line in her life. The pardon list is a claimant list wearing a pardon's clothes. The Curia's genealogists keep the imperial rolls in a vault under the capital and have one policy: find an heir, any heir, a ward-sensitive hand on the leash ([[Aurelan]]). [[Marius Vell]]'s own claimant cousin has stopped speaking and is failing, and Vell needs a line that hears the Sleeper and can be managed; [[Tesh]] is one, through a by-blow of Aurel IX's uncle, and the Curia and the Regent-Chancellor have, for once, the same list. Pike, Bask and Hennet are on it to make it look like a pardon: three names anyone in Cinderford would recognise, so that nobody looks hard at the fourth. [[Pell Quillon]], who keeps Crownfast alone with one candle wherever he is posted and knows every form the Regency prints, can tell at a glance that the paper is not a pardon form at all; it is a Form of the Records, the genealogists', with a pardon's heading pasted over it.

**Where the answer lies.** Pell, if he is still in Cinderford and still honest. Examiner Pardew's sealed post at [[Whitepost]], where the Lector overnighted and where the Office of Charters' standing orders are kept. [[Edda Haverleigh]]'s desk in the Disbursing Office at Caer Ardith, where the list was drawn and where Widow Pruitt's niece can be written to. Hennet, who will tell the Lector what he can do with his pardon, in a Grimmark voice, in front of the whole Candle, and who will then, quietly, ask the party why Tesh's name is under hers.

**By ending.** *Reseal:* the list comes anyway; the Curia wants Tesh whether or not there is a flame to hold, because the throne is still empty and a throne is a hole. *Use it:* by the time the Lector arrives, whoever holds the flame is on the list too, in a different ink, and the Lector has come to collect them. *Let it crack:* the Lector does not arrive. His horse does, on the Ash Road, with the list in the saddlebag and nobody to read it to.

**Payoff.** A second campaign's road west: Caer Ardith, the vault under the capital, the eleven ward-sensitive bloodlines on the genealogists' list, and the question of who sits down.
