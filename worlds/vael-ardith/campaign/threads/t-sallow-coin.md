**The question.** [[Garrick Sallow]] stands the whole Last Candle a round on payday, with silver nobody else has. Where's it from?

**What the GM knows.** The Quiet Office, through [[Ansel Mote]]: forty silver a month to grow a mutiny and see the lantern go out, with a promise of a captaincy in a new Regency company afterwards. The coins are new Caer Ardith silver (Investigation DC 12 on one of them: the year is 3011), the same as Dannic's purse in the Cold Room and the coin Hennet keeps in a cup under her bar. Nobody in the Ashenreach had seen new Regency silver in two years.

**Where the answer lies.** A coin from Sallow's round; his room in the Keep (a letter, unsigned, in a small neat hand); [[Spoons Aldane]]. The two mints are told apart on [[Coins and Money Slang]].

**Payoff.** Session 7, when the mutiny breaks open, or earlier if the party pushes. Exposing him early ticks the Quiet Office's clock and makes Mote act sooner.
