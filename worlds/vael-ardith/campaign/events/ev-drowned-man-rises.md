At noon the bell-ward Dunny Pike struck the [[The Treaty Bell|Treaty Bell]] with his mallet, three and three: someone in the water. The ford-watch had hooked a body from the shallows under the third pier of the toll bridge: [[Dannic Rook]], a week gone from the company. He stood up on the bank, and so did two others nobody recognised.

:::gm
See [[The Drowned Man]]. Dannic was drowned under the third pier on the night of 7 Ashfall and never buried; he has stood in the shallows since the Ember-rise, facing the Keep. The two others are nameless drowned of the Seventh, half-made, from the Flooded Street. Dannic's last words: "It's cold. It's cold under the glass."
:::
