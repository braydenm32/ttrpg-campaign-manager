At the end of session 1, towards midnight, with the whole company awake and watching, the flame in its iron cage on [[Lantern Hill]] shrank to a bead of blue no bigger than a fingernail. For the length of a long breath it seemed to go out. Then it came back.

Nobody spoke. [[Spoons Aldane|Spoons]] was seen closing his betting book very slowly. [[Brother Tallow]] did not leave the cage until dawn.

:::gm
The end of session 1. The Mutiny clock ticks to 3. Sleeper clock unchanged: it ticks at the full Ember on 16 Ashfall (session 3). Every wick-lamp in the company dims with the Lantern, then comes back.
:::
